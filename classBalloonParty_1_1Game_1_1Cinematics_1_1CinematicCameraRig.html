Box-in-box follow: moves the camera (inner box = frustum) toward target while keeping the frustum fully inside the outer bounds (walls + offset).

Returns the clamped position. When the inner box grows (zoom out), freedom to move shrinks; at full base size it may be locked.

Curve-driven restore using the box-in-box model.

The curve's X = real seconds, Y = blend factor (0 = follows target clamped to outer bounds, 1 = original framing). The ortho size interpolates from current toward base, progressively shrinking the inner box's freedom until it snaps home.
