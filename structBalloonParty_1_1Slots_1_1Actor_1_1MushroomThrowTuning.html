Detailed Description

How a mushroom's cap flies when a blast blows it off whole (MushroomCutTimeline): how far it is thrown, how long it is in the air, how high it is lifted and how far it tumbles.

Authored with the cut's numbers (MushroomCutTuning.Blown).

The look only. Which caps a blast takes off, and when they grow back, are the rule's.
