Detailed Description

How a mushroom's cap falls when a lance cuts it and how it pops back (MushroomCutTimeline): how far the two pieces part, turn and shrink, how long they take, and how the cap grows back.

Authored on the mushroom settings (IMushroomSettings.Cut).

These are the look's numbers only. When a cap is cut and when it grows back are the rule's to say: it publishes MushroomCutMessage and MushroomRegrownMessage.

Member Function Documentation

◆ RoomBeyond()

float BalloonParty.Slots.Actor.MushroomCutTuning.RoomBeyond ( float radius)

The room a cap that has come off needs beyond the cap's own radius, in world units, for a mushroom of radius : what the quad the mushroom is drawn on has to hold, for a lance's two pieces or a blast's whole cap, whichever goes further.

It is the bound the shader boxes them by (its vertex stage's flown: the radius grown by the lift and the turn, plus how far they are moved), a sum and not the true furthest point, so the mesh is never smaller than the box the shader asks for.
