Detailed Description

An effects map (Effects Layer Clearing) for the camera it sits on.

While the clearing mask is open, the camera stops drawing its effect layers; an effects camera renders them into the map instead, and a camera-fitted quad lays the map back over the frame, faded where the mask is clear. With the mask empty that is the same blend into the same frame, so it only runs while a clearing is open: otherwise the effects draw straight into the frame, as they would without it.
