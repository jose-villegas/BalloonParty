Detailed Description

Owns a ColorStreak per ball.

A pop records against the ball that caused it (an item pop against the ball that set the item off); a pop no ball caused, or one credited to a ball already retired, records against one shared streak that resets on every load. A ball's streak is dropped when the registry retires the ball, and every streak resets on level-up.
