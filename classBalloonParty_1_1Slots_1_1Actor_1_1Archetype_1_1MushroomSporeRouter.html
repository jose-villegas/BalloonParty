Detailed Description

Lets a meadow mushroom's spores go (Meadow Mushrooms): hears what happens to a cap (a ball's bounce, a lance's cut, a blast), asks MushroomSporePlanner what that is worth, and sends the smoke to the game's smoke field and the specks to the mushroom's own view.

The smoke is the smoke field's. A cone is three or four of its capsule stamps laid end to end along the burst's way, each wider than the last and thinner (PaintSource.MushroomSpores is the profile: its colour, its base opacity). The field lights it, drifts it on its wind and fades it as it does all its smoke: a hit's small puff is gone in about a second.

A blast's burst waits. A bomb's own cloud covers the mushroom at first, so the burst is held for the tuned delay in a small fixed list, with the view it is for, and let go from Tick(). It is let go only if that view still stands in that slot, and the list is emptied when the level changes and when the run starts over: a burst never lands on the mushroom a new board put where the old one stood.
