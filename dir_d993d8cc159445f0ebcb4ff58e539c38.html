Detailed Description

Two balloons are rewards rather than threats, and no level's mix brings them. The golden balloon comes on a purple level, from the line that finds the level three quarters done, and popping it can bring another, up to three a level. The heart balloon comes to a purple level on a schedule, up to three, even at full hearts, where its pop gives an extra heart past the maximum; on any other level it comes to a player who is missing hearts, more often the more are missing. At most one of each is on the board. The plan is Plans/PLAN-RewardBalloons.md.

The golden balloon itself is Balloon/Model/GoldenBalloonModel: the shot's own ordinary hit bounces off it, a pierce pops it (it is tough, so the pierce plows it and it shatters at the next wall), and no item touches it: an item's damage has no DirectHit, and its hit is HitOutcome.None. It is colourless and scoreless, resists paint, and carries no item. Its prefab (Prefabs/Balloon/Golden) is built from the tough balloon's, so it looks like a balloon, with a gold-plated body (Shaders/BalloonParty/Balloon/GoldenBalloon.shader) and a dark gold knot. The prefab keeps the tough balloon's variant, which only draws cracks for a balloon with durability, so on the golden one it does nothing. It has its own cues (CombatSoundRouter): BalloonDeflectGolden when the shot bounces off, the unbreakable's metallic chime a fifth above the board's key with a high gold glint over it, climbing on each further bounce of the same shot; and BalloonPopGolden when it breaks, the gold-band balloon's pop with a balloon's burst under it and the chime an octave up as a coin ring. Both are layered from clips already in the sound bank (SoundBankConfiguration), so they can be retuned there.

The heart balloon is Balloon/Model/HeartBalloonModel: any hit pops it, the shot's or an item's, whatever its catalogue entry's hit count says. It is colourless and scoreless, resists paint, and carries no item, so its pop leaves the streak alone. Its pop gives back one heart, never to a player already out: up to the maximum, or on a purple level past it, as an extra heart (read from the level being played when it pops, so one popped after a purple level ends heals as on the next level). Its prefab (Prefabs/Balloon/Heart) is the balloon's, with a heart body and bandages drawn by Tools/art/make_heart_balloon.py, and its own highlight (Shaders/BalloonParty/Balloon/HeartShine.shader, lit from the heart's baked normals), because the round balloons' shine is drawn for a circle. Its pop cue is BalloonPopHeart, a kalimba note for now.

When each one is due

Only while the level is being played (LevelUpPhase.Playing), and only for a type the balloon catalogue has an entry for.

  • Golden. On a purple level (IsExtraColorLevel), the first once LevelCompletion reaches GoldenAtCompletion (0.75): each colour's points, capped at the goal, over all the goals. Its pop rolls once for a second: GoldenRepeatChances (25%, then 10% for a third, so three at most). A won roll brings it GoldenRepeatDelayLines (3) lines after the pop. Only a pop rolls (the service reads ActorHitMessage for it), so a golden balloon a board clear takes, or one popped after its level ended, brings no other. A new level starts over at the threshold, and anything still owed is dropped. One that could not be placed stays due for the next line. It wins a line over the heart.
  • Heart, on a purple level. The schedule PurpleHearts: a step at 30% completion (sure), one at 60% (a 50% chance) and one at 90% (25%), so three at most. Each step rolls once, on the line that reaches its completion, at full hearts too, and needs no pop of the heart before. A step won while a heart is on the board waits until it leaves, and a big clear that passes several steps in one line rolls them all and brings what they win one at a time. A new level starts the schedule over and drops what was owed. The roll while hurt (below) is off on a purple level.
  • Heart, elsewhere. A roll per line while a heart is missing: HeartChanceMissingOne, or HeartChanceMissingTwo with two or more missing. After one is placed, HeartCooldownLines lines pass before the next roll. A heart rolled for a line with no room for it is owed to the next line, unless the player has healed to full in between. Extra hearts past the maximum count as full.

The tuning is IRewardBalloonSettings, a block on BalloonsConfiguration.

Leaving the board

Every way off the board (a pop, a board clear, a level change) calls the leave callback, which frees the slot for the next of that type. Only a pop pays, and only a pop rolls for another golden balloon, which is why the payout and the service read the hit, not the leave. The pop's leave runs before its hit is published, so the service guards its roll by the placement, not by whether a golden is on the board. Each placement has its own number, so a callback from an earlier balloon, or from before a run reset, changes nothing. Anything a reward gives belongs on its pop, not on the leave.
