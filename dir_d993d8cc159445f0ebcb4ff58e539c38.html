Detailed Description

Two balloons are rewards rather than threats, and no level's mix brings them. The golden balloon comes once on a purple level, from the line that finds the level three quarters done. The heart balloon comes to a player who is missing hearts, more often the more are missing. At most one of each is on the board. The plan is Plans/PLAN-RewardBalloons.md.

When each one is due

Only while the level is being played (LevelUpPhase.Playing), and only for a type the balloon catalogue has an entry for.

  • Golden. On a purple level (IsExtraColorLevel), once LevelCompletion reaches GoldenAtCompletion (0.75): each colour's points, capped at the goal, over all the goals. Once per level: a new level re-arms it. One that could not be placed stays due for the next line. It wins a line over the heart.
  • Heart. A roll per line while a heart is missing: HeartChanceMissingOne, or HeartChanceMissingTwo with two or more missing, plus HeartChanceOnPurple on a purple level. After one is placed, HeartCooldownLines lines pass before the next roll. A heart rolled for a line with no room for it is owed to the next line, unless the player has healed to full in between.

The tuning is IRewardBalloonSettings, a block on BalloonsConfiguration.

Leaving the board

Every way off the board (a pop, a board clear, a level change) calls the leave callback, which frees the slot for the next of that type. Each placement has its own number, so a callback from an earlier balloon, or from before a run reset, changes nothing. Anything a reward gives belongs on its pop, not on the leave.
