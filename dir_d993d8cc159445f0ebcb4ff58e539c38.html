Detailed Description

Two balloons are rewards rather than threats, and no level's mix brings them. On a purple level both are rolled every few lines, each further one rarer: the heart balloon from the level's start, up to five, even at full hearts, where its pop gives an extra heart past the maximum; the golden balloon from 15% done, up to three. Level 5 always gets a single golden one early on, the player's first, and now and then another ordinary level does too. Off a purple level the heart comes to a player who is missing hearts, more often the more are missing. At most one of each is on the board. The plan is Plans/PLAN-RewardBalloons.md.

The golden balloon itself is Balloon/Model/GoldenBalloonModel: the shot's own ordinary hit bounces off it, a pierce pops it (it is tough, so the pierce plows it and it shatters at the next wall), and so does a laser beam: the laser marks its damage DamageFlags.Beam. No other item touches it: an item's damage has no DirectHit, and its hit is HitOutcome.None. A bomb's blast on its neighbours carries Piercing like the beam, but never Beam, so a bomb beside it hands out no card. It is colourless and scoreless, resists paint, and carries no item. Its prefab (Prefabs/Balloon/Golden) is built from the tough balloon's, so it looks like a balloon, with a gold-plated body (Shaders/BalloonParty/Balloon/GoldenBalloon.shader) and a dark gold knot. Faint god rays turn and breathe behind it (Balloon/Type/GodRaysLook, the Shaders/BalloonParty/Sprite/GodRays.shader fan on the GoldenRays material), so it stands out on a busy board: they sort below every balloon, so they show in the gaps around it. Their colour and strength are the palette's GoldenRays entry; their shape and motion are the material's. The prefab keeps the tough balloon's variant, which only draws cracks for a balloon with durability, so on the golden one it does nothing. Its pop shatters it into gold plates (PSVFX_GoldenPop, the unbreakable's shatter drawing the Sprites/Balloons/Golden/golden_parts sheet, cut from a capture of the balloon by Tools/art/shatter), lit by the time of day like the balloon (Particle/SceneLit). It has its own cues (CombatSoundRouter): BalloonDeflectGolden when the shot bounces off, the gold pop's chime (Chime 0010) a fifth above the board's key with a high gold glint over it, climbing on each further bounce of the same shot; and BalloonPopGolden when it breaks, the gold-band balloon's pop with a balloon's burst under it and the chime an octave up as a coin ring. Both are layered from clips already in the sound bank (SoundBankConfiguration), so they can be retuned there.

The heart balloon is Balloon/Model/HeartBalloonModel: any hit pops it, the shot's or an item's, whatever its catalogue entry's hit count says. It is colourless and scoreless, resists paint, and carries no item, so its pop leaves the streak alone. Its pop gives back one heart, never to a player already out: up to the maximum, or on a purple level past it, as an extra heart (read from the level being played when it pops, so one popped after a purple level ends heals as on the next level). Its prefab (Prefabs/Balloon/Heart) is the balloon's, with a heart body and bandages drawn by Tools/art/make_heart_balloon.py, and its own highlight (Shaders/BalloonParty/Balloon/HeartShine.shader, lit from the heart's baked normals), because the round balloons' shine is drawn for a circle. Fainter god rays than the golden's turn behind it, white and shorter (GodRaysLook again, on the HeartRays material and palette entry). Its pop is a beat: its catalogue entry's PopPulse swells it to 1.25 and shrinks it away, while PSVFX_HeartPop throws a burst of hearts on along the shot, drawn with the HUD heart's own sprite (HeartTrail_Combined: its shine and its soft baked shadow), spinning only in the board's plane so the shadow keeps falling to the lower right (the override's AlongHit; a board clear throws them up). Its pop cue is BalloonPopHeart, a harp pluck (harp005 by t1mp, CC BY) the sound bank pitches to the board's key.

When each one is due

Only while the level is being played (LevelUpPhase.Playing), and only for a type the balloon catalogue has an entry for.

  • On a purple level (IsExtraColorLevel), both kinds roll every PurpleRollEveryLines (6) lines, each on its own count, and each kind's first roll is on the first line it can. Lines while one of that kind is on the board don't count. Each roll's chance is the next entry of the kind's list, so each further one is rarer and the list's length caps the level: PurpleGoldenChances (35%, 20%, 10%: three at most) and PurpleHeartChances (40%, 30%, 20%, 12%, 6%: five at most). The golden rolls only once LevelCompletion reaches PurpleGoldenAtCompletion (0.15; each colour's points, capped at the goal, over all the goals); the heart from the level's first line, at full hearts too, and the roll while hurt (below) is off there. A won roll stays due until it is placed (a line with no room for it passes it on). A new level starts both counts over and drops anything owed. The golden wins a line over the heart.
  • Golden, on another level. Rare, and a single one. Every OrdinaryGoldenEveryLevels (5) levels, a level that isn't purple rolls once, on its first line, against OrdinaryGoldenChance (40%). A won level places one golden balloon at GoldenAtCompletion (0.5). SureGoldenLevel (5) needs no roll: it always places its one, and early in the level (SureGoldenAtCompletion, 10% done), so every player meets a golden balloon with the level still ahead.
  • Heart, elsewhere. A roll per line while a heart is missing: HeartChanceMissingOne, or HeartChanceMissingTwo with two or more missing. After one is placed, HeartCooldownLines lines pass before the next roll. Each line rolled without one while the player is in need raises the next line's chance (IAssistSettings.HeartPity, a Game/Assist/PityRoll: on the last heart 12%, 18%, 24…). A heart rolled for a line with no room for it is owed to the next line, unless the player has healed to full in between. Extra hearts past the maximum count as full.

The tuning is IRewardBalloonSettings, a block on BalloonsConfiguration.

Leaving the board

Every way off the board (a pop, a board clear, a level change) calls the leave callback, which frees the slot for the next of that type. Only a pop pays, and only a pop rolls for another golden balloon, which is why the payout and the service read the hit, not the leave. The pop's leave runs before its hit is published, so the service guards its roll by the placement, not by whether a golden is on the board. Each placement has its own number, so a callback from an earlier balloon, or from before a run reset, changes nothing. Anything a reward gives belongs on its pop, not on the leave.
