Detailed Description

A spinner's geometry: a straight line of three or five through one slot, its centre, which the member in the middle holds.

It turns about that member: each of the others moves 60° round the centre at its own distance from it, so the line goes from flat to one diagonal to the other and round again (HexCoordinates.Step numbers the six directions from east, clockwise). Pure: it reads the grid and plans moves, and moves nothing.

Plans one turn into moves : the member on centre stays, each other member goes 60° round it at its own distance, and a balloon it lands on takes the slot it left.

False, with no moves, when a member stands on none of the lines through the centre, when a slot the line would turn into is off the board or holds something it can't trade places with (SnakeWalk.IsOpen), or when the turn would close wall (null checks nothing).
