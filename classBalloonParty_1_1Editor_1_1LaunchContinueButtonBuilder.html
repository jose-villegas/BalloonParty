Detailed Description

Adds the launch screen's Continue button to the Launcher scene: a copy of the Play button under it, with Play's press actions (hide, title out, scene activation) kept, its LaunchPlayTrigger.Play calls swapped for LaunchContinueView.Continue, which plays the ascent itself once the resume is requested, and the view handed to LaunchLifetimeScope.

Starts inactive: the controller shows it only while a run is saved. Running it again does nothing once the scene has a Continue button.
