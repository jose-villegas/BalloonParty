Detailed Description

Builds the launch screen's Continue in the Launcher scene, replacing any built before: a root stretched over the launch canvas holding LaunchContinueView, the spring line, the cloth ribbon with "continue" on both faces, and a small white copy of the Play balloon.

The copy keeps Play's press actions (hide, title out, scene activation), with Play's LaunchPlayTrigger.Play swapped for LaunchContinueView.Continue, which plays the ascent itself once the resume is requested; its hide hides the whole Continue, and its scene activation is Play's own. Starts inactive: the controller shows it only while a run is saved.
