◆ MushroomReward

enum BalloonParty.Shared.MushroomReward
package

What a ball that is not a lance gets from a mushroom it meets: a bounce, with or without the lance, or no bounce at all.

Enumerator
None 

The ball bounces; nothing is paid.

Arm 

The ball bounces and the count reached the chain target: it becomes the lance.

PassThrough 

No bounce.

The stretch has had every bounce it is allowed (MushroomChainRules.MaxBouncesPerStretch), so the mushrooms let this ball through until it meets a wall. The caller must not turn the ball and must not ask the bounce rule.

◆ UpgradeKind

enum BalloonParty.Shared.UpgradeKind

What an upgrade card changes. Each kind is read at one decision point.

Enumerator
BallCap 

+IUpgradeCardSettings.Amount balls in flight at once (rounded).

FasterReload 

Reload time multiplied by (1 − IUpgradeCardSettings.Amount) per stack.

Shield 

+IUpgradeCardSettings.Amount starting shields per ball (rounded).

ItemOdds 

+IUpgradeCardSettings.Amount chance of one more item per item roll.

RaiseLimits 

+IUpgradeCardSettings.Amount to every other card's IUpgradeCardSettings.MaxStacks (rounded).

Dealt only once another card has reached its limit.

SnipeChance 

+IUpgradeCardSettings.Amount chance that each ball the thrower loads starts armed as a Snipe lance (the total never passes 1).

RewardOdds 

The reward balloons (golden, heart, disco, nuke) come likelier: every roll that brings one has its chance × (1 + IUpgradeCardSettings.Amount) per stack, up to certain.

A sure one stays sure.
