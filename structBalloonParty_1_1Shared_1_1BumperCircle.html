Detailed Description

One permanent bumper (the cap of a meadow mushroom) as the ball meets it: a circle the ball's centre bounces off.

Caps stand in walls: one to three of them in neighbouring slots.

◆ Radius

readonly float BalloonParty.Shared.BumperCircle.Radius

How far from Center the ball's centre is when the two touch: the bumper's own radius plus the ball's.

Every bounce rule works on the path of the ball's centre, so a corridor is clear of this bumper when that path stays farther than this from its centre.

◆ Wall

readonly int BalloonParty.Shared.BumperCircle.Wall

Which wall the cap belongs to, 0 or more: the caps of one wall share it.

MushroomChainRule counts by it (one hit a wall, not the same wall twice in a row), and the kick bends a ball only toward a cap of another wall. Caps of one wall may stand so near that their circles overlap; caps of two walls may not.
