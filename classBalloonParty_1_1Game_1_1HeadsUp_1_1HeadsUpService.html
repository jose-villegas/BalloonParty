Detailed Description

Takes the routers' heads-up requests, lets HeadsUpQueue decide which show, and drives IHeadsUpPresenter.

Clears every heads-up when the run resets or the player leaves for the launch screen (at once), and on game over or the level-up popup (fading); takes no requests outside the game screen. Moves the lines on unscaled time, so they keep writing through slow-motion, but hands them no live time under a pause, so they freeze with the game.
