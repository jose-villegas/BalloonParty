Detailed Description

Says whether anything is alive on a layer, so a camera that renders nothing but that layer (an effects map) can skip the frames it would render empty.

Every pooled object with a renderer is tracked from the moment its pool creates it, and counts on its renderers' layers while it's active: pooled effects live exactly as long as they're out of the pool. A persistent object that shows and hides by its renderer instead (the nuke cloud, the dust speck field, the formation glow ring) is watched by that renderer.
