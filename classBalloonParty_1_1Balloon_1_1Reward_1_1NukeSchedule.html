Detailed Description

When the nuke balloon is due (PLAN-NukeBalloon), and whether one is on the board.

Every clock line from INukeOddsSettings.FromLevel on rolls INukeOdds.Chance, up to INukeOddsSettings.PerLevel a level, once INukeOddsSettings.CooldownLines lines have passed since the last was placed. INukeOddsSettings.SureLevel brings one for sure, INukeOddsSettings.SureAfterLines lines in. A won roll stays due until the nuke is placed: a line with no room for it passes it on. A new level drops one still owed.
