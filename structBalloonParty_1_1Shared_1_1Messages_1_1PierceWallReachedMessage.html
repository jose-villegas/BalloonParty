Detailed Description

A shot that was piercing reached a wall: the straight run it plowed ends at Position.

Published after the wall's discharge has resolved, so the shot's IsPiercing already says whether the lance carries on past this wall. Also published for the wall a piercing shot dies on. Discharged says whether the toughs it plowed shattered at this wall — the run that spent the lance, as opposed to one it merely carried.
