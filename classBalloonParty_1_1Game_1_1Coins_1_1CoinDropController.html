Detailed Description

Rolls a coin for each pop from ICoinSettings.CoinsFromLevel on and publishes CoinDroppedMessage for each drop; CoinCreditor adds it to the wallet.

The rolls come from a stream seeded by the level and restarted with each attempt, so every attempt at a level drops the same way for the same pops.
