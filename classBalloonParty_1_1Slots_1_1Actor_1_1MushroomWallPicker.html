Detailed Description

Picks a level's mushroom layout from the baked catalogue (MushroomWallCatalogue): the seed chooses where in the catalogue to start, and the first layout from there whose slots are all free and allowed is the one.

It flies nothing, so a deal costs a walk over a few dozen entries.
