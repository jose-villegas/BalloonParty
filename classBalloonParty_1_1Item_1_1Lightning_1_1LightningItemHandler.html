Detailed Description

Handles the Lightning item: chains through same-color balloons nearest-first and destroys them.

A rainbow holder instead converts a whole colour group to rainbow (never destroys) — the colour chosen by the last projectile fired, so it seeds a combo rather than clearing the board.
