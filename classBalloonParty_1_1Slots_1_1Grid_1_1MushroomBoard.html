Detailed Description

The meadow's mushrooms as the ball meets them (Meadow Mushrooms): one bumper circle for every whole MushroomBumperModel on the grid, in the order of their ids, and the caps a lance has cut, which are no bumpers until they grow back.

A circle's id is the cap's own and its wall the one the cap stands in, its centre the slot's world position at rest, and its radius the cap's own plus the ball's (BumperCircle.Radius is where the ball's centre is at the touch). The centre comes from the slot and not from a view: a mushroom never drifts, and the shot solver and the aim line have to bounce off the same circles.

The circles are kept and rebuilt only when the grid has changed since they were read (SlotGrid.MutationVersion) or the ball's radius has (it reads as zero until the thrower starts). Reading them on an unchanged board costs two comparisons and allocates nothing, which is what lets the flight ask on every fixed step.

Cut caps. Cut takes a cap out of the circles there and then and remembers its model with the beats it has to wait (IMushroomSettings.RegrowBeats). The model stays in its slot, so the slot stays taken, and a rebuild leaves a cut cap out. The cut caps are kept in the order they were cut. The board keeps no clock: it is told each beat (CountBeat) and told to put a cap back (Regrow). A cut cap whose model has left the grid is forgotten at the next rebuild, so a level's end, a retry and a reset leave nothing cut: the next level's caps are new models.

◆ Cut()

void BalloonParty.Slots.Grid.MushroomBoard.Cut ( int index)

A lance cut the cap at index of Bumpers: it has left the list when this returns, and the indices after it have moved down by one.

Its slot stays taken (the stem stands there) and the caps left of its wall keep their wall.

The list is the same one Bumpers answered before the call, one cap shorter: a caller sweeping it for the next cap goes on with what it holds. A field that cannot cut the cap (an index it does not have) leaves the list as it was, which a caller reads as nothing cut.

Implements BalloonParty.Shared.IBumperField.

◆ Bumpers

IReadOnlyList<BumperCircle> BalloonParty.Slots.Grid.MushroomBoard.Bumpers
get

Every whole cap, in the order of their ids.

A cap keeps its id and its wall while others are cut, so an index is not an id: ask BumperCircle.Id. Empty on a level without mushrooms and while every cap is cut. The list is the field's own and is refilled when the board changes: read it, do not keep it.

Implements BalloonParty.Shared.IBumperField.
