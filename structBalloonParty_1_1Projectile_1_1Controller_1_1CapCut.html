Detailed Description

One cap a lance cut (a meadow mushroom's) that ProjectileMotionResolver resolved during a fixed step.

A step can hold several; the resolver keeps them in order for the view to announce (ProjectileMotionResolver.StepCapCuts). The cap has already left the bumper field.

◆ Point

readonly Vector2 BalloonParty.Projectile.Controller.CapCut.Point

A point inside the cap that the split passes through; with Direction it is the line the cap splits along.

It is the point of the lance's line nearest the cap's centre, the line carried on both ways: for a lance that began its step inside the circle that point can lie behind the ball. When the line passes outside the cap itself and only the ball's edge clipped it, the point is drawn in toward the centre, off the line the ball's centre flew, so that the split still crosses the cap and a sliver comes off. So it is not always a point the ball's centre flew through.
