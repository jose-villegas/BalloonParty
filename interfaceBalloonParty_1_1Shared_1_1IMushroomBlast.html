Detailed Description

Blasts reaching the meadow's mushrooms (Meadow Mushrooms): what the bomb and the nuke tell when they go off, so that neither has to know what a mushroom is.

A bomb went off: the one that stood in bombSlot , at centre , with killRadius .

The whole caps it would break an unbreakable in the place of are blown off (the slots next to it; for a rainbow bomb, the centres within its kill radius), and the other whole caps its blast reaches sway. Nothing on a board without mushrooms.
