static bool BalloonParty.Shared.Extensions.ActorHitMessageExtensions.TookCoat ( this in ActorHitMessage message)
static

Whether the hit took a layer of foam off a coated balloon (ICoatable) rather than hurting it: the coat turns any ordinary damaging hit into a pass-through.

Read from the outcome alone, so it relies on every coatable balloon popping to one hit; a coatable that took more would read its ordinary survived hits as coat hits too.
