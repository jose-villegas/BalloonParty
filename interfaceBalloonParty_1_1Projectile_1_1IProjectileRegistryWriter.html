Detailed Description

The write half of IProjectileRegistry.

A shot that dies lands itself right before it publishes ProjectileDestroyedMessage and retires itself right after, so every subscriber sees it gone yet can still read its state. Every other exit (game over, reset, a replaced held ball) is the thrower's, and it lands and retires together.
