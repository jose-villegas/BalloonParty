Detailed Description

The on-screen tooltip: a short line of text that fades in over play, holds briefly, then fades out. Anything in the game can show one by publishing a TooltipMessage; the view that publishes it owns the wording.

Contents

File What it does
TooltipBanner MonoBehaviour view on the Tooltip prefab (in GameCanvas). Subscribes to TooltipMessage and shows its text: fades its CanvasGroup to _visibleAlpha, holds for _holdDuration, fades out. A new line replaces the one showing and restarts the timing; an empty one shows nothing. With _writeOn assigned (a UI/HeadsUp/WriteOnText on the label, as on the prefab) each line is written on in pencil instead of appearing whole, starting once the fade-in is done, and the hold is lengthened by the write. The tween runs on unscaled time, since lines land during slow-motion beats and frozen screens. It also words BonusCardDealtMessage with _bonusCardFormat and holds it until navigation is back in Game: the card is dealt during the retry's reset, while the game-over screen is still up. Injected via RegisterComponentInHierarchy<TooltipBanner> in GameLifetimeScope
Shared/Messages/TooltipMessage The line to show. Registered as a MessagePipe broker in GameLifetimeScope
Shared/Extensions/StringFormatExtensions FormatOrRaw: formats an editor-authored string, returning it as written when it's malformed, so a typo in a format never throws mid-flow

Wiring requirements

  • _group and _label must be assigned; the prefab assigns the Banner child's CanvasGroup and its Text (TMP). _writeOn is optional; the prefab assigns the WriteOnText on that label.
  • The CanvasGroup should not block raycasts: the tooltip sits over play and must never eat a tap.
