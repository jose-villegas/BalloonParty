Detailed Description

How a ball's bounces go, where it happens: +n bounce at a shield it picks up (n counting its pickups this flight), -1 bounce where a wall spends one (loud when it leaves the last), and xn ricochet where it deflects off balloons n times in a row.

A ricochet that paid the ball a shield writes the +1 bounce under itself, so that shield's own pickup writes nothing, though it still counts toward the next.
