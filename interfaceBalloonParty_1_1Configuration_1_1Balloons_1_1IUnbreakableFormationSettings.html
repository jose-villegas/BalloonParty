Detailed Description

How unbreakables gather into formations, hold them, pick the next shape, and call in more unbreakables while left alone.

◆ BeatSeconds

float BalloonParty.Configuration.Balloons.IUnbreakableFormationSettings.BeatSeconds
get

Seconds of play between the unbreakables' beats.

An unbreakable snake steps on every beat; every BeatsPerCycle beats make a cycle, the clock formations form, hold and change shape on and a lone unbreakable moves on.
