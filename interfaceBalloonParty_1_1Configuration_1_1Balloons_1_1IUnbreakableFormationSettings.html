Detailed Description

How unbreakables gather into formations, hold them, pick the next shape, and call in more unbreakables while left alone.

Property Documentation

◆ BeatSeconds

float BalloonParty.Configuration.Balloons.IUnbreakableFormationSettings.BeatSeconds
get

Seconds of play between the unbreakables' beats.

An unbreakable snake steps SnakeStepsPerBeat times a beat; every BeatsPerCycle beats make a cycle, the clock formations form, hold and change shape on and a lone unbreakable moves on. It is the board's beat: bubble clusters merge on it too, a share of a beat after each one (IRipeBubbleSettings.MergeOffBeat).

◆ GiftCoverPreference

float BalloonParty.Configuration.Balloons.IUnbreakableFormationSettings.GiftCoverPreference
get

How strongly a cover prefers a gift balloon (golden, heart, disco ball, nuke) to a balloon carrying a bomb, a snipe or a lightning, in slots of travel for each member.

A spot's cost is the slots its members travel to it, plus 3 for each balloon they move out of the way; a gift's spot counts as this many slots cheaper for each member of the cover. So the gift is covered unless covering the item carrier would save every member more than this many slots on average. 0: no preference, the cheaper spot wins.

Implemented in BalloonParty.Configuration.Balloons.UnbreakableFormationSettings.
