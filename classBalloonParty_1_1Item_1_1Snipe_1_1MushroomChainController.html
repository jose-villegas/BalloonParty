Detailed Description

Pays the meadow's mushroom chain (Meadow Mushrooms).

It hears every bounce off a mushroom and, when the chain rule answered MushroomReward.Arm for it, arms the ball as the Snipe lance through ISnipeArmer and announces it (MushroomLanceMessage).

It keeps no count and no state of its own, and makes no judgement of its own: the count lives on the ball's flight, the rule that reads it is MushroomChainRule, and the answer rides on the bounce's message. So a run reset, a level change and a game over leave nothing here to clear.

When the ball becomes a lance. A bounce that pays ends the ball's fixed step where it happened (ProjectileMotionResolver): the ball has turned and flown no further. The bounce is published from inside that step, before the ball is moved, and messages are delivered at once. So the ball is armed on the mushroom, at the point of the bounce that completed the chain, and everything after that point (the next step, at the lance's speed, and whatever wall, balloon or cap it meets) is the lance's: from there it bounces off no mushroom and cuts the caps in its way. No wall, and so no death, shares a step with the arming.

Only a ball still in flight is paid. One that has died, or was taken off the board by a reset, a level change or the game over, is no longer in flight in the registry, and its reward is dropped with it.
