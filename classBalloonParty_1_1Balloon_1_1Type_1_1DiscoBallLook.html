Detailed Description

The disco ball's body (BalloonParty/Balloon/DiscoBall): gives each ball its own turn of the mirror grid, so two on the board never flash in step, and holds the scene capture its tiles reflect while the ball is on screen.

The grid turns in the shader, so nothing here runs per frame.
