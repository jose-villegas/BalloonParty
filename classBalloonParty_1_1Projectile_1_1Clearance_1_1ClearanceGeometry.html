Detailed Description

The bend a trail takes round a clear circle.

A point inside the circle's outer band (its radius × (1 + softness)) is pushed out along the line from the centre, and everything inside is squeezed into the band, so nothing deflected ends inside the circle itself. The map is smooth and monotonic, and it meets the identity at the band's edge with a matching slope, so a trail brushing past bends gently and one passing through never snaps.

The band lies just outside the circle, while the effect fade's soft edge (Include/Clearance.cginc) lies just inside it, so a bent trail is never also faded.

Deflects point out of circle .

With r the distance to the centre, R the radius and B = R(1 + softness), a point with r < B lands at R + (B − R)(r / B)^k, where k = B / (B − R) makes the slope 1 at the band's edge.
