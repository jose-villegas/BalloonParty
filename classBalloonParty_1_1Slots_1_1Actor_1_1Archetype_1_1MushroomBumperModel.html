Detailed Description

A meadow mushroom (Meadow Mushrooms): a permanent bumper the ball bounces off.

It stands in its slot for the whole level, as a bush does: it is never hit, never popped, and nothing moves it.

It has no collider and takes no part in the hit pipeline: the ball's bounce is worked out against the circles of MushroomBoard, which reads these models off the grid.

What keeps it still is what it is not. The balance race, the wave-start roam and pressure shoves only ever move an IWriteableDynamicSlotActor, which this is not; a snake walks only through balloons; and it is not IPassThrough, so a rising balloon stops under it, as under a bush.
