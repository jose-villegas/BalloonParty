Detailed Description

The order of the tough snake, head to tail: the most armour (hits left) leads.

Toughs with equal armour keep the order they stand in. The snake moves toward that order one place per step, so a hit or a tough joining at the tail never sends a member leaping along the body.

static bool BalloonParty.Balloon.Formation.ArmourOrder.StepTowardOrder ( List< IWriteableFormationMember > members)
staticpackage

One step of members toward most armour first: from the head down, each member with more armour than the one ahead of it trades places with it.

No member moves more than one place, so a tough joined at the tail, or one hit at the head, works its way to its place a step at a time, and the one it passes waits a beat beside it. Equal armour never trades. True when anyone moved.
