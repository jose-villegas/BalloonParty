Detailed Description

The order of a tough snake, head to tail: the most armour (hits left) leads.

Toughs with equal armour keep the order they stand in. A walking snake moves toward that order one place per step, so a hit or a tough joining at the tail never sends a member leaping along the body.

Re-pairs a snake's row (assignments , one slot per member) so the most armour takes the slot at the leading end (east or west) and the rest follow by armour.

Ties go to the one nearer the leading end, so a row of equal armour already in place keeps its slots. Leaves the assignments head first.

static bool BalloonParty.Balloon.Formation.ArmourOrder.StepTowardOrder ( List< IWriteableFormationMember > members)
staticpackage

One step of members toward most armour first: from the head down, each member with more armour than the one ahead of it trades places with it.

No member moves more than one place, so a tough joined at the tail, or one hit at the head, works its way to its place a step at a time, and the one it passes waits a beat beside it. Equal armour never trades. True when anyone moved.
