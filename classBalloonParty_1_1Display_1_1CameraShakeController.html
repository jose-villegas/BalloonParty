Detailed Description

Decides WHEN the camera shakes: punches on WaveDamageMessage (once per heart lost) and recoils on ProjectileFiredMessage, both suppressed while a cinematic reports CinematicTraits.BlocksShake.

Lives in the run scope so its subscriptions tear down each run; drives the persistent CameraShakeView, which it resolves from this scope or its parent.
