Detailed Description

Tuning for how shield chains are planned: the fan of openings tried, and how selective the planner is about where a shield may land.

These are balance knobs, not implementation constants — the band they define is what decides whether a level's shields read as a route or as scatter, and it is retuned against playtests rather than derived. Guards that only keep the algorithm terminating (surface epsilon, leg cap) stay in the planner.
