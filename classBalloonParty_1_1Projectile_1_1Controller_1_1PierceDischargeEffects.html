Detailed Description

The shared pierce-discharge feel: an outward shockwave stamp at the shattered line plus a brief slow-mo dip, played whenever a piercing shot discharges (cruise-earned or Snipe — off PierceDischargedMessage).

The rainbow bloom is separate (see SnipeDischargeBloom).
