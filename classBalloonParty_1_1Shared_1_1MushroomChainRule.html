Detailed Description

The meadow's mushroom chain: a ball that bounces from one wall of caps to another enough times in one flight, without touching a wall of the board in between, is paid the Snipe lance.

One rule for the game and for the shot solver, so the two cannot disagree on which hit pays.

Only two things move the count, and each has its call: a mushroom bounce (OnBounce) and a wall (OnWall). A balloon pop and a ricochet off an unbreakable or a tough balloon leave it alone, so there is no call for them: a flight may pop and ricochet between two mushrooms and keep its chain.

A lance does not come here. A piercing ball, however it was armed, does not bounce off a mushroom: it flies straight on and cuts every cap its path enters (IBumperField.Cut). A cut counts nothing and breaks nothing: the chain a lance carries is as it was when the ball was armed, until a wall clears it. So the rule is only ever asked about a ball that is not a lance, and the bounce that reaches the target always arms.

A mushroom bounce spends no shield, so the rule itself bounds a flight: a stretch between two walls has at most MushroomChainRules.MaxBouncesPerStretch bounces, whether they counted or not. Past that the mushrooms let the ball through, and it flies straight to a wall, which does spend a shield. Without the cap a ball could be kept for good: a ricochet off an unbreakable can send it back to the mushroom it just left, a hit that does not count and is kicked again.

Counts the bounce of a ball that is not a lance off a cap of wall wall (BumperCircle.Wall, 0 or more) and says what it pays, or answers MushroomReward.PassThrough and counts nothing when the stretch is spent (IsSpent).

A wall of caps is one hit: the wall hit last does not count again until another one has been hit, whichever of its caps the ball meets, unless MushroomChainRules.RepeatHitCounts. So a rattle along one wall is worth one hit, and a crossing between two walls is what counts.

The counted hit that brings the chain to MushroomChainRules.ChainTarget answers MushroomReward.Arm; every other bounce pays nothing. Once armed the ball is a lance and bounces off no mushroom, so a count past the target is only reached by a ball whose arming never landed (it had left the board), and pays nothing. The rule does not cap the armings: a lance spent at a wall (it discharges there once it has pierced a tough balloon) is an ordinary ball again, the wall has cleared its chain, and a new chain arms it again.
