Detailed Description

Decides what a mushroom lets go when something happens to its cap (Meadow Mushrooms): a ball's hit puffs a small cone of spores the way the ball was going, scaled by its speed; a lance's cut a little more, along the lance's way; a blast a wide burst, the way the cap is blown.

Pure: it is told the time and answers a MushroomSporeBurst, and draws nothing.

Kept quiet two ways. A mushroom that has puffed for a hit says nothing for the tuned refractory time, so a rally between two walls reads as a few puffs. And the whole board shares one budget, counted in hits and coming back over time: what is asked when less is left comes out that much smaller, down to nothing for a hit and to a floor for a blast, which is the one moment that must always show. So a nuke's seven caps let go three bursts whole and four small ones.

It keeps one number a mushroom that has puffed and one for the budget; nothing is allocated for a hit once a slot has been seen.
