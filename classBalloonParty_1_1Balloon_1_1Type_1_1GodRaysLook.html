Detailed Description

Tints a reward balloon's god rays from its palette entry (the golden balloon's GoldenRays, the heart's HeartRays).

The rays turn and breathe in their shader (BalloonParty/Sprite/GodRays), whose material holds their shape, so nothing here runs per frame and no tween outlives the balloon: they hang off the balloon's root, so they scale in with its spawn and go with it when the pool takes it back.
