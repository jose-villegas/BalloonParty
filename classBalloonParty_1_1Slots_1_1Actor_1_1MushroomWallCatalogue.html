Detailed Description

The layouts a meadow level's mushrooms are dealt from, by how many walls they have.

Every one was flown from every angle the thrower can aim at and kept because of what it measured (MushroomLayoutBand), so a level picks a layout and flies nothing.

The entries are in MushroomWallCatalogue.Generated.cs, written by the editor's baker (Tools/BalloonParty/Bake Mushroom Wall Catalogue) and never by hand. They hold for the board, the cannon, the aim grid and the mushroom settings they were baked with (BakedWith); a test flies every entry again and fails when one of those has moved.
