Detailed Description

What a mushroom's cap does while nothing touches it: it breathes, and it rocks in the disturbance field the grass and the bush leaves answer.

Authored on the mushroom settings (IMushroomSettings.Idle). The look only: the cap the ball bounces off is the same circle whatever these say.

All of it is the shader's work, a vertex: MushroomView hands these over once, as the vectors below, and stays switched off while its cap is at rest.
