Detailed Description

What a piercing shot in flight (a Snipe lance, or a cruise-earned pierce: the same state on the shot) reports to the impact bus beside an ordinary ball's stamps, for the water.

Along every stretch it flies, two blasts that run the stretch in the step's time: a wide, weak one whose shallow furrow throws a wave out to both sides, and a narrow, strong one that bares a thin sliver of floor under the shot. One blast can't do both, since a furrow deep enough to bare the floor bares most of its width. Stretch after stretch they make one wake, from where the shot was armed to the wall its charge ends at. There the wall stamp every shot already leaves (ProjectileView's ProjectileImpact stamp) carries one small round blast (WallBlast), the wake's full stop. Nothing but the water answers a blast.

Reports the wake of one fixed step of a shot's flight, from from to where step left it.

wasPiercing is the shot's state as the step began and stillPiercing once the wall it reached (if any) has been resolved: the way to a wall is the lance's it flew in as, the way on from it only a lance's still armed.

A lance is not turned by a mushroom (it cuts the cap and flies on), so its wake turns at walls alone. The one step that both bounces off a mushroom and ends piercing is the one whose bounce armed the ball: it ends on the mushroom, the caller passes the bounce point as from , and there is no stretch to report.

The blast the wall stamp carries when a shot meets a wall: the small crater that ends a lance's wake, where its charge ends.

That is the wall a lance dies at, or one it reaches having plowed toughs (pendingPlows ), which shatter there, or having lost its pierce on the way (stillPiercing false). None for an ordinary ball, and none for a lance that only bounces, still armed with nothing plowed: its wake turns with it.
