Detailed Description

A ball took on a kind of buff it didn't have: the rainbow (any colour keeps its streak) or a speed buff.

Another buff of a kind it already holds, such as a Snipe charge re-applying the rainbow, sends nothing, and neither does one that expired the moment it was applied.
