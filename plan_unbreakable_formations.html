Unbreakable Formations

When three or more unbreakables are on the board, they stop roaming. They line up in a formation, hold it for a few turns, then switch to a different one. While they are in formation their red lights turn blue, and a blue glitter swirl spins behind each of them.

No changes until this plan is actioned.

Goals

  1. Formations. With at least 3 unbreakables on the board, they gather into a shape:
    • a horizontal line,
    • a diagonal in either direction (/ or \),
    • an item cover: a row of unbreakables right under a balloon that carries an item, so the player, who shoots from below, can't hit the item directly.
  2. Holding. A formation stays in place for N turns. Its members do not roam, and they do not float up in the balance race. When the N turns are over, they change into a different formation.
  3. Blue while in formation. Everything that is red on an unbreakable turns blue while it is in formation, and turns red again when the formation breaks.
  4. Glitter behind. The spiral glitter from the night top bar renders behind each member, tinted blue.

A turn here is a spawn wave, three lines on the spawn clock. Unbreakables already jump once per wave (BalloonBalancer.Balance(atWaveStart: true)), so a wave is the unit their movement is counted in today.

How unbreakables move today

  • Once per wave they roam. BalloonBalancer.RelocateRoamers offers every IPreBalanceRelocatable the board's resting slots. These are empty slots that need no further settling. UnbreakableBalloonModel.TryPickRelocation jumps to the board's opposite side, preferring far slots.
  • Between waves they float up. Like every balloon, an unbreakable takes part in the balance race (BalancePlanner). It rises while either slot above it is empty (GridBalanceQuery.IsUnbalanced). Its entry has unlimited steps, speed 2 and DirectBalanceMotion, so it glides straight to where it ends up.
  • A spawn shove relocates it. In PressurePropagation its PushResponse is RelocateFarthest, so a shove from below makes it jump to the farthest free slot instead of pushing it.
  • The shot solver copies the race. ShotBoardDynamics runs the real BalancePlanner on a headless grid to predict where balloons will be during the in-flight rebalance pulses. Every target that has a BalanceProfile is a mover there.

A formation has to switch off all four of these for its members. If it doesn't, the race floats a horizontal line up as soon as there is a gap above it, and a spawn shove scatters it.

The glitter effect

The night top-bar glitter is ColorProgress.prefab → Slider/Shadow (1). It is a UI Image with the ColorBarNightGlitter material, using the shader BalloonParty/Sprite/GlitterSwirl (Assets/Shaders/BalloonParty/Sprite/SpriteGlitterSwirl.shader). Its CanvasGroup sits in ColorProgressBar._nightFadeExtras, so it fades in with night.

The shader is a sprite shader, so it works on a world-space SpriteRenderer as well as on UI. LaserGlitter.mat and PickupInSightGlitter.mat already use it in the world. It never draws the sprite itself. The sprite's alpha, times the renderer's alpha, only masks where specks may appear, and the renderer colour tints them. So:

  • A soft disc sprite (circle.png) on a child renderer behind the shell confines the swirl to a halo around the balloon.
  • SpriteRenderer.color.a fades the whole halo in and out.
  • The blue comes from the palette (see below), through the renderer colour.

The bar material is tuned for a long bar (density 64, speck size 0.5, swirl speed 20). A halo about the size of a balloon needs its own material, UnbreakableFormationGlitter.mat, with lower density. The values get tuned in the Editor.

Design

Rules

  • Who joins. Unbreakables on the board, up to MaxMembers. Each formation change picks the members closest to the formation's spot. Any extra unbreakables keep roaming as today.
  • When. A formation is checked at each wave start, before the roam pre-pass:
    • No formation and at least MinMembers (3) unbreakables: pick a shape, find a spot for it, and move the members there.
    • Holding, with turns left: count one turn down. Nothing moves.
    • Holding, no turns left: pick a different shape and move into it. That counts as a new formation, and its own hold starts.
  • Where. Every formation slot must be empty, or already held by one of the members. A formation never pushes other balloons out of the way. Slots in the bottom BottomClearance rows are never used, so spawn lines always have room to enter. If no spot is valid for the chosen shape, the next shape is tried. If none fits, there is no formation this wave and the unbreakables roam.
  • Which spot. Among the valid spots for a shape, the one with the least total travel wins, with a small random pick among the near-best so it doesn't always form in the same place.
  • Breaking up. If members are popped (a piercing hit) and fewer than MinMembers are left, the formation breaks at once. The rest turn red, are released into the balance race, and roam from the next wave. A new unbreakable that spawns while a formation holds keeps roaming, and joins at the next change.
  • Run reset or level-up: the formation is cleared.

Shapes on the hex grid

The grid is pointy-top, with odd rows shifted half a column right, and row 0 at the top (HexCoordinates). For k members:

Horizontal Diagonal "\" Diagonal "/" Item cover (k = 3)
row r: U U U row r: U row r: U row r: [I]
row r+1: U row r+1: U row r+1: U U U
row r+2: U row r+2: U
  • Horizontal: (c, r) … (c+k-1, r).
  • Diagonals: step row + 1 each time. The column follows the odd-row shift, the same neighbour rule as HexCoordinates.HexNeighborIndices.
  • Item cover: the item host's two lower neighbours first (row r+1). The row then grows outward, alternating left and right, until there are k slots. If several balloons carry items, the one whose cover costs the least travel is picked.

Members hover in midair: they are anchored, not supported. Balloons below a formation rise until they reach it, so a formation also works as a ceiling, and holes open above it. That is the danger the formations add: a run of unbreakables the player has to shoot around. The score is not touched.

Architecture

Balloon/Formation/ (new folder, namespace BalloonParty.Balloon.Formation)
FormationShape enum: Horizontal, DiagonalDown, DiagonalUp, ItemCover
FormationPlanner pure: shape + grid + members + item hosts → target slots, or none
FormationAssignment pure: members → target slots, least total hex travel
UnbreakableFormationService controller: hold counter, shape order, break-up; IRunResettable
README.md
Slots/Actor/IBalanceAnchor.cs bool IsAnchored: the race, roam and pressure leave it alone
Balloon/Model/IFormationMember.cs IReadOnlyReactiveProperty<bool> InFormation (for the view)
Configuration/Balloons/UnbreakableFormationSettings.cs + IUnbreakableFormationSettings
  • Model. UnbreakableBalloonModel implements IBalanceAnchor and IFormationMember. It gains an internal SetInFormation(bool) that only the service calls. While it is in formation:
    • TryPickRelocation returns false,
    • IsAnchored is true,
    • PushResponse is not used, because pressure treats an anchored actor as a static blocker.
  • Controller. UnbreakableFormationService is a plain C# class registered in VContainer. At wave start, BalloonBalancer.Balance(atWaveStart: true) calls _formations.PlanWaveStart(moves) before RelocateRoamers. The service returns the moves only. The balancer carries them out the way it carries out roam moves (Reserve, Remove/Place, IsStable = false, RecordPath), so all grid changes and animation stay in one place. The service then sets each member's InFormation. Break-ups are found from the grid's change stream (SlotGridChangedEvent), so there is no polling every frame.
  • Balance seam. BalancePlanner.TryBalanceSlot, BalancePlanner.CollectPassCandidates, BalloonBalancer.HasPossibleMove and BalloonBalancer.RelocateRoamers all skip an IBalanceAnchor { IsAnchored: true }. PressurePropagation treats it as immovable, the same as an actor without IPressureMovable.
  • Solver. ShotBoardGather gives an anchored target no BalanceProfile, so ShotBoardDynamics places it as a static contact and the predicted pulses don't move it. This keeps the aim solver and the playtest bot honest about formations.
  • View. UnbreakableBalloonVariant subscribes to IFormationMember.InFormation in Bind and eases a 0 → 1 blend over ColorFadeDuration. It is driven from its existing Update, only while the blend is changing:
    • Body (7) and Body (8): SpriteRenderer.color lerps from its authored red to the palette's UnbreakableFormation blue, keeping the authored alpha. These go in a new serialized _coreLights array.
    • The inner rim: an MPB _RimColor override on the renderers that use UnbreakableMatIN, lerped the same way.
    • The trail: two Gradients built once in Awake, the authored one and a blue copy with the same alpha keys. They are swapped at the midpoint of the fade. A per-frame gradient lerp would allocate.
    • The motion light and the burst stamp use the formation palette index while the blend is past 0.5, so the specks around a formation member puff blue too.
    • FormationGlitter: a new child SpriteRenderer behind the shell, with the circle.png sprite and UnbreakableFormationGlitter.mat. Its alpha follows the blend, and it is disabled at 0, so it costs no draw call out of formation.
    • OnDespawned / despawn resets the blend to 0 and restores the red, as pooling requires.
  • Palette. A new UnbreakableFormation entry in GamePalette.asset (blue, presentationOnly), with a GamePalette.UnbreakableFormationColorId constant. All the blue comes from this one entry.

Settings (IUnbreakableFormationSettings)

Nested in BalloonsConfiguration as [SerializeField] UnbreakableFormationSettings _unbreakableFormation, so no new asset or scope registration is needed. The values below are defaults, to be tuned after a playtest. The questions below may change them.

Setting Default Meaning
Enabled true Master switch, for A/B playtests
MinMembers 3 Fewest unbreakables that form
MaxMembers 5 Most that join one formation
HoldWaves N, open question Waves a formation holds
BottomClearance 2 Bottom rows a formation never uses
ShapeWeights per shape How often each shape is picked (see open questions)
ColorFadeDuration 0.4 s Red ↔ blue ease

Open questions

  1. N: how many waves a formation holds.
  2. Walk or snap: do members glide into formation (the existing direct glide at their speed) or snap there (vanish and reappear with a flash)?
  3. Order: how the next shape is picked.
  4. Item cover against lines: when it wins.

The answers are recorded under Decisions once given.

Decisions

Answered by the user on 2026-11-10:

  1. N = 3 waves. HoldWaves defaults to 3.
  2. Walk. Members glide into formation with their existing direct glide (speed 2), and turn blue when they arrive. No new blink effect is needed.
  3. Random, no repeat. A weighted random pick among the shapes that have a valid spot. The shape just held is excluded, unless it is the only one that fits.
  4. Item cover joins the rotation only while a balloon on the board carries an item. Its weight is higher than each line's: defaults 1 for each line shape and 2 for the item cover.

As built

Changes from the design above, made during implementation and review:

  • Fade duration. ColorFadeDuration is a field on UnbreakableFormationLook, next to the other visual tuning on the prefab. It is not in IUnbreakableFormationSettings, which keeps only the rules.
  • The look component. The blend lives in its own IBalloonViewBinding, UnbreakableFormationLook, not inside UnbreakableBalloonVariant. The variant only switches its burst stamp's palette index.
  • When it turns blue. A member turns blue once it has arrived: when it is in formation and IsStable. So the motion light, which only lights while it moves, stays red.
  • No balance request on break-up. The next line's balance or the timed pulse floats the released members. A request from inside the grid's change event would also fire midway through level transitions, as the board is cleared one balloon at a time.
  • No flicker. A shape change marks out only the members the new shape drops. Members that stay never dip out of formation.
  • Glitter sorting. The glitter is in BalloonView._spriteLayerRenderers at index 1, right after the baked shadow, so its per-slot sorting order always sits behind the sphere. UnbreakableFormationLook turns it back off when OnSpawned re-enables it.
  • Spawns already stop under a formation. BalloonPlacementResolver treats every balloon as a ceiling, members included.
  • The covered item isn't anchored. If it pops or rises away during the hold, the cover stays where it is until the next change.

Phases

Phase 1: Formation logic

  • IBalanceAnchor, IFormationMember, the model changes, FormationPlanner, FormationAssignment, UnbreakableFormationService, and the settings.
  • The balancer, planner, pressure and solver changes for anchored actors.
  • Tests (EditMode):
    • FormationPlanner: line and diagonal slots on even and odd rows; occupied slots rejected; bottom clearance kept; the item cover takes the host's lower neighbours first; no spot gives none.
    • FormationAssignment: least total travel.
    • The service: under 3 never forms; 3 form and anchor; a hold of N waves moves nothing; a change after N picks a different shape; dropping below 3 breaks it and releases the rest; a newcomer during a hold roams; a reset clears the formation.
    • BalancePlanner: an anchored actor in an unbalanced slot stays, and balloons below rise to it.
    • PressurePropagation: an anchored actor blocks the push.
    • ShotBoardGather: an anchored target has no balance profile.
  • Then run the architect, test-everything and reviewer agents, and curate their findings.

Phase 2: Visuals

  • The palette entry, the variant's blend (core lights, rim, trail, light and stamp colour), the FormationGlitter child and its material, and the prefab wiring.
  • Needs an in-Editor check: the shader values on the new material, the halo size and sort order, and how the blue reads against each terrain. dotnet build does not check prefab or material edits.
  • Run the reviewer and optimizer agents (the glitter's draw calls, and allocations from the blend).

Phase 3: Docs and playtest

  • Balloon/Formation/README.md; updates to Balloon/README.md (balance phase), Balloon/Type/README.md (the variant), Slots/README.md (IBalanceAnchor) and Solver docs.
  • Tools/playtest/playtest_bot.py comparison with Enabled on and off: levels, shots, hearts. The bot is not a real player, so this only compares the two tunings.
  • The user's in-Editor playtest and sign-off.

Risks

  • A horizontal line can cut the board. Balloons below it can't rise past it. With MaxMembers 5 on a wider board, there is always a way around, and BottomClearance keeps spawns open. If the playtest shows lines blocking too much, cap the line length below the board width.
  • The roam pre-pass and the formation share the wave start. The formation runs first, so roamers see the formation's slots as taken.
  • Prefab YAML edits. Adding a child and serialized references by editing the YAML is fragile. Each edit is checked by reopening the prefab in the Editor, through the Unity CLI once the change is on main.
