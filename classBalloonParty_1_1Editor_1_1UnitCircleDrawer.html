Detailed Description

Unit-circle direction picker for [UnitCircle] Vector2 fields: click or drag inside the disc to aim the vector (written back normalized; GUI y-down is flipped to the world's y-up).

The numeric row above and the angle field beside the disc stay editable for exact values — the angle is degrees CCW from +x (0° = right, 90° = up), matching the full-circle gradient mapping (VectorMathExtensions.Angle01).
