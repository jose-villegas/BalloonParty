Detailed Description

Writes a TMP_Text on left to right as if by a pencil: a soft reveal edge, slanted forward, sweeps across the glyphs while an optional pencil tip rides it.

Works on any TMP text, on a canvas or in the world. Ticks on unscaled time, so it keeps writing through slow-motion and frozen screens, and only while writing: a written text costs nothing.
