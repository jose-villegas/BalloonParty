Detailed Description

Published by BubbleMergeService as one bubble cluster gives bubbles to its neighbour, just before the two counts change, so a view can still read what each showed.

Absorbed is set when the giver gave everything and flows into the receiver; otherwise it stays in GiverSlot with fewer bubbles.
