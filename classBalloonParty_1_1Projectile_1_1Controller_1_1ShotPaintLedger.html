Detailed Description

Which balloons each shot painted rainbow itself (the disco ball's wave, PLAN-DiscoBalloon).

The shot that breaks a disco ball is still flying, and a rainbow pop climbs its streak whatever the colour, so without this it could paint the board and then climb on everything it painted. A pop of a balloon its own shot painted scores at the streak as it stands and leaves it there (DamageFlags.OwnPaint); another shot's pop of it climbs as any rainbow pop does. A shot's entries go when it retires.
