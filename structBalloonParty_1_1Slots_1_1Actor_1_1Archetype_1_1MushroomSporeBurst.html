Detailed Description

One release of a mushroom's spores (Meadow Mushrooms), as worked out by MushroomSporePlanner: a cone of smoke and a count of specks, where they leave from and which way.

Nothing here draws: the smoke goes to the smoke field and the specks to the mushroom's own particles.
