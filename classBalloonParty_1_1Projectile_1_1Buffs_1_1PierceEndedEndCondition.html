Detailed Description

Ends the buff when the projectile stops piercing — the wall that spends the pierce after a tough plow, or the shot despawning.

Unlike WallBounceEndCondition (which ends on the first wall), this rides the whole pierce, so a Snipe lance keeps its speed until the pierce itself is consumed.
