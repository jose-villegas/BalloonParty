Detailed Description

Maps a score group to its choreography handler by group total: the highest-MinPoints entry whose threshold the group clears wins.

Shared by ScoreTrailService (which spawns) and LevelUpCinematic (which derives the tipping id) so the id can never diverge from what the handler actually registers.
