Detailed Description

The spores a meadow mushroom lets go (Meadow Mushrooms): a puff of smoke and a few specks when a ball hits its cap, a little more when a lance cuts it, and a burst when a blast blows it off.

Authored on the mushroom settings (IMushroomSettings.Spores).

The look only. The smoke is the game's smoke field, so its colour and how long it lingers are that field's (its MushroomSpores profile and its decay); these numbers say where it is put and how much of it. The specks are the mushroom's own particles.
