Detailed Description

After a disco ball's pop, the next few clock lines arrive rainbow (PLAN-DiscoBalloon), so the party outlasts the wave.

DiscoWave grants the lines; each line that brings a balloon pays one, by the wave's rules (PaintTargets): a paintable balloon turns rainbow, and one immune to paint keeps its look. The painted balloons are booked to the shot that broke the ball (ShotPaintLedger) while it flies, as the wave's are. A level change or a run reset forgets the lines still owed.
