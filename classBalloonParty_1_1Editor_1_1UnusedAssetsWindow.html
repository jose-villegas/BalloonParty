Detailed Description

Lists assets that no root can reach — candidates for deletion, not verdicts.

Roots: every scene under Assets/, everything in any Resources/ folder (runtime-loaded by name), Assets/Settings/ (referenced from ProjectSettings, which dependency walking can't traverse), and the player's preloaded assets. Reachability via AssetDatabase.GetDependencies(string[], bool). Scripts, docs, editor tooling and plugins are excluded from the scan — their usage isn't asset-reference shaped, so they'd only produce noise.
