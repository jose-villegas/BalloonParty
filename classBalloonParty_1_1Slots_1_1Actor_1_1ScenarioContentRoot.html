Detailed Description

Parent transform for scenario static content; a level transition moves this root, not each child.

Outgoing is a child holder for the old level's content a transition carries out (its balloons, and snapshots of its scenery), kept apart from the incoming statics but still riding the root. The root's transform is composed here from the transition's travel and the mist reveal's depth, so the two never fight over it: write SetTravel and SetDepth, never the transform.
