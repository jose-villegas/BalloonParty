Detailed Description

Projectile bounced off a meadow mushroom (Meadow Mushrooms).

Published once for every bounce, in the order they happened, the ones that did not count included. A ball the mushrooms let through (MushroomReward.PassThrough) publishes nothing, and neither does a lance: it bounces off none and cuts their caps (MushroomCutMessage).

Count is the flight's chain after this bounce was counted, and Reward what the chain rule answered for it: the lance to arm, or nothing. The reward itself is paid by whoever listens; the ball is not changed by the bounce beyond its turn. Incoming and Speed are the ball's as it arrived, for a reaction that leans away from the hit.
