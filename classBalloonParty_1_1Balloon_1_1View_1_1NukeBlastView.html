Detailed Description

Draws the nuke's blast (PLAN-NukeBalloon) on the BalloonParty/Sprite/NukeBlast material: a white flood that blinds the board as it spreads, a white-hot core and a shock ring at the edge (GrowingDiscView).

It carries a scene light whose radius follows the disc, so the board itself is lit white as the disc crosses it, fading once the disc covers the board. The blast's start gives the disturbance field its strongest push, whose impact report carries the blast that digs the water's crater on a water level, and the fuse before it an implosion that pulls the field in.
