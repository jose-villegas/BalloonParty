Detailed Description

A rare level that plays one more colour than its step gives (the fourth, purple), with no threats and lines arriving much faster: the pressure is a colour more to juggle and a busier board, not armour to break.

Rolled per level from the run's seed, so runs differ and a retry replays it. Never two in a row, so the level after one is back to the usual colours.
