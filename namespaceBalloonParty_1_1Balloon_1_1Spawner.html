How far placement may reach when a column's own entry is blocked.

Enumerator
OwnColumn 

Only the column's own reachable entry; skip when it is blocked (pop-spawn extras).

Rehome 

Own entry, else rehome into the nearest column with an open entry (initial fill).

Pressure 

Rehome, then shove existing balloons aside to open a slot (turn spawns).
