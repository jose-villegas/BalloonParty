Detailed Description

Whether a mushroom stands in the way of a ball on a straight run ahead of it: what the doomed glide's path check, the cruise lookahead and the pierce telegraph ask, so the three agree with the flight (ProjectileMotionResolver) on what the ball would bounce off.

Nothing stands in the way of a lance: a piercing ball flies through the mushrooms and cuts their caps, on every leg it is looked along (TryFindAhead is told whether the ball is piercing). For a ball that is not, two things about its own flight take a mushroom out of its way, and both hold only until its next wall, so only for the leg the ball is on:

  • A spent stretch (MushroomChainRule.IsSpent): the mushrooms let the ball through until it meets a wall. The wall clears the chain, so on every leg past it they bounce the ball again.
  • The mushroom the ball is leaving (ProjectileFlightState.LeavingBumperId), which a line close to its tangent would be read as entering.
