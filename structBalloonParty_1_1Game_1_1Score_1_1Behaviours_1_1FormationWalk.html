Detailed Description

A CLOSED WALK a pen orbits forever: an ordered cyclic list of vertex indices whose consecutive pairs (including the wrap from last back to first) are the edges to trace.

A 2-vertex walk is a back-and-forth shuttle (the degenerate loop that IS the "line" shape). Arc chooses interpolation: an arc slerps the (sphere-surface) local positions so ring segments read as curved bands, a chord lerps them so polyhedron edges read as straight lines. Segment count == Vertices.Length (cyclic).
