Detailed Description

Pure math shared by the dynamic-board sim: the nudge envelope (mirrors BalloonMotionTicker.Reach exactly — plan_shot_geometry §7c) and the moving-circle entry solve (relative-velocity quadratic, §7b) used for contacts against a balloon whose centre is a function of time rather than fixed.

No allocation, no state — safe to call from the sim's hot per-event loop (thousands of calls per sweep).

Smallest positive distance travelled by a projectile moving at speed along unit direction from origin at which it enters a circle of combinedRadius centred at center moving at constant world-units/second velocity .

Distance-parameterised (like the static sim's wall/balloon solves) rather than time-parameterised: with d the projectile's travelled distance, the projectile sits at origin + d·direction and the balloon at center + (d/speed)·velocity, so the relative displacement is (origin − center) + d·(direction − velocity/speed). Solving its squared length against combinedRadius reduces to the EXACT static line-circle entry when velocity is zero (no rounding introduced — the velocity/speed term is exactly zero regardless of speed).
