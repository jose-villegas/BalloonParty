Detailed Description

A strip of ribbon filling its rect: swallowtail-notched at both ends, with a wave running along it that the driver moves on by setting WaveTime.

It is cel shaded in the scene's light by its material (BalloonParty/Sprite/CelRibbon), from what the mesh tells it: how each fold turns, and a copy of the strip for the drop shadow. WaveAt is the same wave, for text riding on the ribbon to bend with it.
