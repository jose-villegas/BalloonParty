Detailed Description

Where a level sits in the run's scenario schedule: the stretch's scenario, the level's place in it (0 = the stretch's first level) and how many levels the stretch lasts.

float BalloonParty.Configuration.Level.ScenarioStretch.PuffCloudShare

The share of its puff-cloud count a level keeps.

A meadow arrives under clouds and clears halfway: its first ⌊n/2⌋ levels (at least one) have puff clouds, the last of them at half the count, and the rest are clear. Sky levels keep the full count.
