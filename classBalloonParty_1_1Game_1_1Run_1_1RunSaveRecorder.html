Detailed Description

Keeps IRunSave at the run's last level boundary: as each level begins (after a level-up's transition, and at a retry's reset) it saves what the level begins with, and on a loss it saves the game-over screen.

A fresh run clears the save. Saving at boundaries only means a resume replays the level from its start, with no mid-level board to restore.
