Detailed Description

What a mushroom's cap is doing once it has come off (Meadow Mushrooms): a lance's cut, the two pieces parting, turning, falling and fading; or a blast, the cap thrown off whole, lifted, turning, landing and fading; the bare stem after either, and the cap popping back.

Pure: MushroomView steps it and draws what it holds.

Every value is worked out from the time since the cut or since the regrow began, not carried from frame to frame, so it is the same at any frame rate and ends exactly at rest.
