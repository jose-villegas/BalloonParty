Detailed Description

Night-mode time-of-day driver: walks the ambient light direction around the circle as the player climbs.

Level 1 sits at the authored rest direction; each further level advances by ITimeOfDaySettings.DegreesPerLevel, and the level-up transition sweeps between them — always-forward (the angle is continuous, never wrapped, so it never reverses across the midnight→dawn seam) and on unscaled time so it plays through the transition pause. Does nothing unless ITimeOfDaySettings.NightModeEnabled, leaving the light at its authored direction. Owns only the cycle policy; the ambient state/push lives on TimeOfDayService.
