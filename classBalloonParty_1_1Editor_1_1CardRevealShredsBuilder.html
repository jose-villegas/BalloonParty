Detailed Description

The card reveal's shreds, part of CardRevealBuilder: the burst of torn rubber the reveal flings out as its balloon tears, flying far and fluttering down off the screen.

Sets up the scraps' texture (Tools/art/balloon_pop/make_shreds.py), and makes their lit material and the particle prefab if they are missing, so a rebuild keeps their tuning; assigns the prefab on the settings.
