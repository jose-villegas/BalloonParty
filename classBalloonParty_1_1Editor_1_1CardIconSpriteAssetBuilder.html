Detailed Description

Makes the CardIcons TextMeshPro sprite asset the card titles draw their icons from (UpgradeCardText): one sprite per square cell of the atlas that Tools/art/make_card_icons.py draws, named by its UpgradeKind.

Running it again rebuilds the tables in the same asset, so the asset keeps its GUID.
