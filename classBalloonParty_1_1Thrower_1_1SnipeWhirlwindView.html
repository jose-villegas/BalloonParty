Detailed Description

The Sharpshooter card's announcement when a ball loads Snipe-armed: a bright light drops from the sky onto the cannon, dimming and tightening as it falls, and where it lands a whirlwind plays around the thrower: the Snipe's own cone spiral drilling into the muzzle, and a ring of glitter turning around the cannon, both tightening in as they fade.

A child of the thrower, so the whirlwind turns with the aim; the light falls straight down the world, whatever the aim.
