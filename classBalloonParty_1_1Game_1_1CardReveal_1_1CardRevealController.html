Detailed Description

Queues a reveal for each card the player wins: a level-up's card as it's held (UpgradeCardHeldMessage) and a retry's bonus card as it's dealt (BonusCardDealtMessage).

One plays at a time, and only over the board (in play, or a level-up's ascent, where a level-up's card plays), so a bonus dealt behind the game-over screen waits for play to resume. Each reveal gets a pop chosen at random, a reveal ahead (NextPop), never one of the last ICardRevealSettings.RecentPopsAvoided shown. Nothing is queued while no stage is attached, so until the reveal's view exists the cards stay the tooltip's.
