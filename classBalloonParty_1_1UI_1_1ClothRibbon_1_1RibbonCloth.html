Detailed Description

A line hanging from an anchor with a ribbon of cloth tied to its end, simulated as points joined by springs (position-based, at fixed steps).

The line's last point is the ribbon's pivot: the middle of its head, in the notch. The ribbon is three rows of points (top edge, middle, bottom edge) by Columns, held upright so it turns about the vertical. A breeze streams it out behind the anchor's sideways travel and a wave runs down it toward the tail; when the travel reverses, the breeze turns round and a push out of the screen swings the tail through depth to the other side, so its back shows on the way. Space is the caller's: x right, y up, z toward the viewer; the ribbon's front faces +z while it streams to +x. Obstacles push points out sideways, never in depth.
