Reward Balloons

Two rare balloons that pay the player instead of pressuring them. The golden balloon comes to a purple level, late in it, up to three a level: plated in gold, it shrugs off every normal hit and item, and only a piercing shot pops it, for a free upgrade card. The heart balloon is a heart wrapped in bandages that gives a heart back; it comes to purple levels on a schedule (an extra heart at full health) and to a player who is losing hearts.

No changes until this plan is actioned.

Goals

  1. Golden balloon.
    • Only on purple (extra-colour) levels, placed when the level is about 75% done. Its pop rolls once for another (25%, then 10% for a third: three a level at most), which comes a few lines later. Never more than one on the board, and only a pop rolls: one a board clear takes brings no other.
    • Normal hits do nothing: the shot bounces off it as off an unbreakable. Items do nothing either (bomb, laser, lightning, paint): a bomb beside it must not hand out the card.
    • A piercing shot pops it: a cruise-earned pierce or a Snipe lance. It is plowed and shatters at the shot's next wall bounce, as an unbreakable is.
    • Popped, it gives a free upgrade card, kept for the run, shown with the card reveal.
    • It still looks like a balloon (body and knot, unlike the unbreakable's sphere), plated in metallic gold: plates wrapping it, each catching the light on its own, with glitter where the speculars land.
  2. Heart balloon.
    • A heart-shaped balloon with cloth ribbons wrapped over it like bandages.
    • Any pop gives back one heart, up to the maximum. It pops like a plain balloon (any hit, items too).
    • On a purple level it comes on a schedule, at full hearts too: one at 30% completion, a 50% chance at 60% and a 25% chance at 90%, each rolled once and needing no pop of the one before. Popped there, it gives an extra heart past the maximum, with no design cap; a level-up refill keeps extras, a retry starts the hearts over. The roll while hurt is off there.
    • On any other level it comes while the player is missing hearts: the fewer left, the likelier. Never at full hearts.
  3. Both are colourless: they never count toward a colour's goal, and never break or feed the streak.

Off a purple level, full hearts never roll one, so the heart balloon is help, not loot. A purple level brings its schedule regardless, as part of that level's reward. A level ends with it unpopped and it's gone with the board, like any balloon.

The looks

  • Golden: a balloon, built from the tough balloon's prefab (body, knot, the shine sprites, the baked shadow), so it reads as one of the balloons and not as an unbreakable. Its body is Balloon/GoldenBalloon.shader: the tough balloon's sphere projection and scene light, plated in gold. The plates are even hexagons over the sphere, crowding toward the rim like tiles on a ball. They are even on purpose: irregular Voronoi plates read like the tough balloon's cracks. The plates are thick and stand apart: toward the rim each shows its side wall, each casts a shadow into the gaps, and the balloon's orange rubber shows at the bottom of the gaps, lit as one smooth sphere with its own gloss, so it reads as a balloon wearing the plates. Each plate is bevelled at its edges and held by two domed bolts, at its top and bottom corners, which gives it relief. Each is also tilted a little and wobbles slowly, so it shades, reflects the horizon and glints on its own as the light moves, and the glitter (Include/Glitter.cginc) is gated by the glint, so it sparkles only where the light catches. The knot is dark gold. Checked in the Editor at noon and at night.
  • Heart: a heart-shaped balloon body, drawn by Tools/art/make_heart_balloon.py and lit like the others (SpriteDiffuse), red. Its volume is baked in: the heart is inflated from a height map, so each lobe rounds off and the cleft and the tip fall into shade. Two cream cloth strips wrap across it below the lobes, slightly frayed, tied in a knot on the right lobe; they are children of the sway pivot, so they sway with the balloon. Its highlight is its own: the round balloons' shine is an arc drawn for a circle and swung round the balloon's centre, which a heart's outline doesn't follow, so Balloon/HeartShine.shader lights the highlight from the heart's normals (baked from the same height map) toward the scene light. It lands on each lobe at noon and follows the light round, with the round shine's night response. Checked in the Editor at noon and at night.

Phases

  1. Reward hook. LevelCompletion, RewardBalloonService, IRewardBalloonRequests, the spawner's consumption and prewarm, the settings. Tests for completion, the 75% trigger once per level and only on purple, the heart chance by missing hearts and the cooldown.
  2. Golden balloon. Type, model, IsTough, item immunity, a placeholder prefab (a gold retint of the unbreakable, replaced in phase 3), IFreeCardGrant and the reveal, sound and pop colour, solver parity. BalloonModelFactory must map Golden before its catalogue entry goes in: it throws for an unmapped type, after Placed, which would leave the balloon counted on the board for the rest of the run. The spawn-balloon cheat lists every type, so it needs to route reward types through the service or leave them out. The card grant goes on the pop, never on the leave callback, which a board clear and a level change fire too. Tests for every hit path (projectile, pierce, bomb, laser, lightning, paint) and the grant.
  3. Golden look. The gold-plate shader with glint-gated glitter, its material, and the prefab rebuilt as a balloon from the tough balloon's. In-editor check at noon and night, frames sent for sign-off.
  4. Heart balloon. Type, model, the heal, the heart sprite and bandage strips, prefab, sound. Tests for the heal cap and that only a missing heart brings it.
  5. Docs and measure. READMEs (Balloon, Type, Spawner, Upgrades, Health), the bots on a forced purple level and on a low-heart run, a device build.

Each phase follows the per-phase review: implement, architect, test-everything and reviewer, then fixes, then sign-off.

Measured

The playtest bot's average player (Tools/playtest/, --purple and --hearts), in the Editor. It is a model of a player, not a person, so these numbers are for comparing tunings.

Session Golden placed / popped Heart placed / popped How it went
The seed's first purple level (15), 3 hearts 1 / 0 3 / 2 Level 15 won in 102 s with 2 hearts lost, game over 18 s into level 16
Its first purple level (13), 2 hearts 1 / 0 2 / 1 Game over on level 13 after 83 s
One heart from level 1, 7 minutes 0 / 0 0 / 0 The level-up refills had all 3 hearts back by level 3; no heart lost up to level 10

What it says:

  • The heart balloon comes where it's meant to, on a hard level after mid-level losses, and the average player catches most of them (3 of 5).
  • Early on, the level-up refill heals faster than a heart balloon arrives, so a low-heart start barely sees one. That's fine: by then the help isn't needed.
  • The golden balloon arrives on time, but the average player never broke it: only a piercing shot does, and this player doesn't seek one out. Whether that's too hard for a casual player is open (below).

Open

  • Whether the golden balloon should be easier to break for a player who doesn't hunt pierces. The average bot left it standing on both purple levels it played.
  • Whether the card reveal should say where the card came from ("golden balloon!") in a heads-up.
  • Whether a heart balloon should also drift, to make catching it a small skill test; it sits still in this plan.
  • Before a third reward balloon: give the reward prefabs their own empty RewardBalloonVariant (the golden one borrows the tough variant and the heart the simple one, both doing nothing there), and let RewardBalloonPayout keep one map of the balloons it has paid instead of a field per type.
