Reward Balloons

Two rare balloons that pay the player instead of pressuring them. The golden balloon comes once to a purple level, late in it: plated in gold, it shrugs off every normal hit and item, and only a piercing shot pops it, for a free upgrade card. The heart balloon is a heart wrapped in bandages that gives a heart back; it comes to purple levels and to a player who is losing hearts.

No changes until this plan is actioned.

Goals

  1. Golden balloon.
    • Only on purple (extra-colour) levels, once per level, placed when the level is about 75% done.
    • Normal hits do nothing: the shot bounces off it as off an unbreakable. Items do nothing either (bomb, laser, lightning, paint): a bomb beside it must not hand out the card.
    • A piercing shot pops it: a cruise-earned pierce or a Snipe lance. It is plowed and shatters at the shot's next wall bounce, as an unbreakable is.
    • Popped, it gives a free upgrade card, kept for the run, shown with the card reveal.
    • It looks metallic: gold plates wrapping the sphere, each catching the light on its own, with glitter where the speculars land.
  2. Heart balloon.
    • A heart-shaped balloon with cloth ribbons wrapped over it like bandages.
    • Any pop gives back one heart, up to the maximum. It pops like a plain balloon (any hit, items too).
    • It comes to purple levels, and to any level while the player is missing hearts: the fewer left, the likelier. Never at full hearts.
  3. Both are colourless: they never count toward a colour's goal, and never break or feed the streak.

At most one of each on the board A second one waits until the first is gone

Full hearts never roll one, so the heart balloon is help, not loot. A level ends with it unpopped and it's gone with the board, like any balloon.

The looks

  • Golden: the unbreakable's sphere shading (UnbreakableBalloon.shader, the sphere state the variant pushes) as the base, with a gold plate layer: a cell pattern laid over the sphere, each plate tilted a little so its own specular comes and goes as the light moves, thin dark seams between them, and the glitter (Include/Glitter.cginc) gated by the specular so it sparkles only where the light catches. A new shader, not a retint: the plates are the point. Needs an in-editor look at noon and night.
  • Heart: a heart-shaped balloon body (a new sprite, drawn by script under Tools/art/ like the card icons) lit like the others (SpriteDiffuse), red, with two or three cream cloth strips wrapped across it, slightly frayed, a knot at one side. The strips sway a little with the balloon.

Phases

  1. Reward hook. ILevelProgress.Completion, RewardBalloonService, IRewardBalloonRequests, the spawner's consumption and prewarm, the settings. Tests for completion, the 75% trigger once per level and only on purple, the heart chance by missing hearts and the cooldown.
  2. Golden balloon. Type, model, IsTough, item immunity, prefab with a placeholder gold retint of the unbreakable, IFreeCardGrant and the reveal, sound and pop colour, solver parity. Tests for every hit path (projectile, pierce, bomb, laser, lightning, paint) and the grant.
  3. Golden look. The gold-plate shader with specular-gated glitter, its material and prefab. In-editor check at noon and night, frames sent for sign-off.
  4. Heart balloon. Type, model, the heal, the heart sprite and bandage strips, prefab, sound. Tests for the heal cap and that only a missing heart brings it.
  5. Docs and measure. READMEs (Balloon, Type, Spawner, Upgrades, Health), the bots on a forced purple level and on a low-heart run, a device build.

Each phase follows the per-phase review: implement, architect, test-everything and reviewer, then fixes, then sign-off.
