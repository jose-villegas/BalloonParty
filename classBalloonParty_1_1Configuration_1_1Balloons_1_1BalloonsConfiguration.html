◆ DefaultBalloonMoveSpeed

float BalloonParty.Configuration.Balloons.BalloonsConfiguration.DefaultBalloonMoveSpeed
get

Fallback spawn/balance travel speed (world units/sec) for entries whose own BalloonPrefabEntry.MoveSpeed is 0.

Movement duration is distance ÷ speed, so every balloon travels at a constant speed regardless of how far it must go.

Implements BalloonParty.Configuration.Balloons.IBalloonsConfiguration.
