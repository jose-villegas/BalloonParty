Detailed Description

Builds the launch screen's two drifting balloons in the Launcher scene, replacing any built before.

Play keeps its own balloon, the Play button where the launch canvas has it. Its label comes off the button's prefab, and a root stretched over the launch canvas, drawn under the button, holds its LaunchBalloonView, spring line and cloth ribbon with "play" on both faces. A press hides that root along with the button.

Continue is a root of its own, drawn over Play: a LaunchBalloonView and LaunchContinueView, the line, the ribbon saying "continue", and a small white copy of the Play balloon. The copy keeps Play's press actions (hide, title out, scene activation), with Play's LaunchPlayTrigger.Play swapped for LaunchContinueView.Continue, which plays the ascent itself once the resume is requested; its hide hides the whole Continue, and its scene activation is Play's own. Starts inactive: the controller shows it only while a run is saved.

Each balloon is handed the other, to share the screen with while both show: Play its upper part, Continue its lower.
