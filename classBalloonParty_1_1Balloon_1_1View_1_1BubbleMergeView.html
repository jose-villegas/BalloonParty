Detailed Description

Plays BubbleMergedMessage: the bubbles a giver passes on hop to the receiver one after another, each a single bubble drawn with the cluster's own material.

The giver drops a bubble and wobbles as each leaves; the receiver gains one, wobbles and splashes as each lands. Both clusters hold their shown counts (IBubbleMergeReactive) until the last bubble lands, as the models changed at once. A giver absorbed whole flows in on its own (BalloonController.AbsorbInto); the receiver takes it in when it arrives. A hop follows its receiver if it moves, and is dropped if the receiver leaves the board.
