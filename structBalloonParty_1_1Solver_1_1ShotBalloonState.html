Detailed Description

Mutable per-simulation copy of a ShotBalloonSnapshot — HitsRemaining decrements on a deflect; a popped entry is swap-removed from the caller's active count.

SlotIndex is copied up front so identity survives both the swap-remove (which scrambles working-set order) and a null Actor (statics have no actor backref to read it from). Actor is null unless the snapshot carries a ShotBalloonSnapshot.Balance AND the sim was given a ShotBoardDynamics — in which case it is the persistent stub backing this entry's position/nudge state.
