What a merge or a foam's hand-out may fill a cluster to right now: max , the merge cap, while another cluster may still be filled (FillsLeft), otherwise one bubble short of it, where the cluster waits without ripening.

It is a limit on what a cluster is given, never on what it holds: one already at the cap keeps its bubbles. A cap of 1 or less has no bubble to hold back.

static int BalloonParty.Balloon.Controller.RipeBubbleRule.PlanHandout ( int bubbles,
int[] coatRoom,
int[] clusterRoom,
int clusterFills )
staticpackage

Where a cluster whose fuse ran out hands its bubbles (at most RipeBubbleBurstMessage.MaxHandout), encoded as RipeBubbleBurstMessage.Handout.

One bubble per taker a round, in direction order, coats (coatRoom , layers each plain neighbour can still take) before clusters (clusterRoom , bubbles each neighbouring cluster can still take); rounds run until the bubbles or the room run out, and any bubbles left over pop as leftovers. Only clusterFills clusters (FillsLeft) take the last bubble of their room, the one that fills them to the cap: counted as the plan fills them, so one hand-out never fills more than that, and every other cluster stops a bubble short. Both arrays are indexed by direction and used up as the plan takes from them.

Whether a ripe cluster's burst at burstCentre reaches the slot whose centre is slotCentre : within radius world units, the edge included.

It is measured between slot centres, never where a balloon is drawn on its way to one, so the game and the shot solver agree on a board in motion. A radius of 0 or less reaches nothing.

float fastSeconds )
staticpackage

Whether a ripe cluster's fuse throbs as it runs from before seconds left to after : once as it crosses each whole second, and each half second in the last fastSeconds .

A step that crosses two beats throbs once; a fuse that holds (the level clock stopped), runs back up (ripening) or has run out (the burst takes over) does not throb.
