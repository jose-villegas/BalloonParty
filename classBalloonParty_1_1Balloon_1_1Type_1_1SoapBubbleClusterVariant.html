Detailed Description

Drives the SoapBubbleCluster shader's bubble count and float/spin clock; the shader animates itself from _Time.y after Bind.

During a merge it shows the count a bubble at a time and wobbles (IBubbleMergeReactive). A ripe cluster (BubbleClusterModel.Ripe) swells its renderer to IRipeBubbleSettings.SwellScale, floats IRipeBubbleSettings.FloatSpeedup times faster, throbs as its fuse (BubbleClusterModel.FuseLeft) crosses each second (RipeBubbleRule.Throbs), and is tinted toward IRipeBubbleSettings.WarnTint as the fuse runs out. The fuse only moves while the level clock runs, so a pause or the level-up holds the throb and the tint with it. A cluster popped while ripe folds before its pop shows (IBalloonPopPrelude): over IRipeBubbleSettings.BurstSeconds it steps down to one big bubble (RipeBubbleRule.FoldCount, RipeBubbleRule.FoldScale), keeping the flush it had at the hit. All of it is undone on every bind and when the binding is dropped.
