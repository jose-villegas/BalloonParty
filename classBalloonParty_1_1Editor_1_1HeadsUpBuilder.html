Detailed Description

Builds the heads-ups in world space, on the HeadsUps prefab the Game scene holds, each line a TextMeshPro in the tooltip's pencil font with its WriteOnText settings and a HandwrittenHeadsUp: the cannon's "+1" (ReloadHeadsUp) and the ball's "+n bounce" / "-1 bounce" (BounceHeadsUp).

Not on the game canvas, which is bound to the camera: in world space a line stays where it happened through the camera rig's zoom and shake. A starting look to restyle by hand; running one again rebuilds it, and removes the canvas lines an earlier build left on GameCanvas.
