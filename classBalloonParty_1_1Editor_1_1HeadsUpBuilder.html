Detailed Description

Builds the heads-ups on the game canvas, each line a copy of the tooltip's pencil label (its font and WriteOnText included), made small, with a HandwrittenHeadsUp: the cannon's "+1" (ReloadHeadsUp) and the ball's "+n bounce" / "-1 bounce" (BounceHeadsUp).

A starting look to restyle by hand; running one again rebuilds it.
