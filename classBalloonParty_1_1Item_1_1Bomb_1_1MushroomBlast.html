Detailed Description

What a blast does to the meadow's mushrooms (Meadow Mushrooms): a bomb blows the caps off the ones next to it and sways the others it reaches, and a nuke blows off every cap its blast reaches.

Told by the bomb's handler and by the nuke's blast as they go off; it has no tick and keeps nothing.

Which caps a bomb takes is the bomb's own rule for what it breaks outright, the one that breaks an unbreakable balloon (BombBlast.Pierces): the mushrooms in the six slots next to the bomb's, and for a rainbow bomb the ones whose centre is within its kill radius, which on the shipped board are the same six. Nothing of it is a number of the mushrooms' own.

Which caps sway. Every other whole cap the blast reaches as it reaches a balloon: its circle overlaps the kill radius (MushroomBlastRule.SwayReach, widened or switched off by IMushroomSettings.Blast). The sway is 1 at the distance of the slots next to the bomb and falls to 0 at the reach.

A cap that is blown off is cut out of the bumper field (IBumperField.Cut) exactly as a lance's cut takes it out, and announced with MushroomCutCause.Blast. So everything that follows a cut follows a blast with nothing more to build: nothing bounces off the stem, the kick and the lookahead no longer see the cap, and it grows back on the board's beat, the fifth after it went, a beat later while a ball is in its way.

A blast is no hit of anyone's chain: no flight is told, so a ball in flight keeps the count it has and simply finds the caps gone. A cap already cut is not in the field, so a blast leaves it as it is: its wait is not started again, and it is not swayed.
