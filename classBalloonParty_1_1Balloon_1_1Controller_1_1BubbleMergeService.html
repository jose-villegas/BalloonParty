Detailed Description

Merges bubble clusters that sit in neighbouring slots (BubbleMergeRule): the smaller gives the larger as many bubbles as it has room for, up to a full cluster, and is absorbed once it has given them all.

Looks again the frame after anything could open room: the grid changing (a spawn, a pop, the board settling), a cluster being hit, or a merge of its own. Each cluster merges at most once a pass, so a group gathers a step a frame.
