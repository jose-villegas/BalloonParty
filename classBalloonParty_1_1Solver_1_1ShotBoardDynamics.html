Detailed Description

Owns a real headless SlotGrid + GridBalanceQuery + BalancePlanner over stub actors, plus the per-balloon nudge-impulse bookkeeping — the dynamic-board half of plan_shot_geometry §7 (tasks 4b/4c).

Reused across an entire sweep's worth of angles: build once from a gathered board, then call ResetForNewFlight at the start of every ShotSimulator.Simulate call rather than reallocating (mirrors the sim's caller-owned workingSet pattern).
