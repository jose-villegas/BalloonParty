Detailed Description

One item activation's selection inputs — the host balloon's own state at the moment it popped, not yet an effect (see ShotItemLayer.Resolve).

Origin is the host's centre at contact time; ProjectileDirection is Vector2.zero for a CHAINED activation (a popped item triggering another item) — live passes Vector3.zero too, and Paint fans its blobs upward in that case. SpinDegrees is the Laser's extrapolated-to-contact-time rotation (gather captures the rate; the sim advances it to the predicted hit).
