Detailed Description

Plays a ripe cluster's spiral and RipeBubbleBurstMessage.

The spiral's source turns with the cluster it follows (IRipeSpin, RipeBubbleRule.SpiralTurnRate). A ripple is a small explosion out to the edge of its blast (RipeBubbleBurstMessage.Radius): a ring over the water, one soap bubble drawn with the coat's material, under the balloons, spreading from the big bubble the popped cluster folded into as it fades, and IRipeBubbleSettings.BlastBubbles bubbles in the warning colour thrown out over the balloons, fast at first and fading at the end. Foam plays the hand-out: the cluster, off the board but still on screen in its warning colour, lets one bubble go every IRipeBubbleSettings.FoamStepSeconds (RipeBubbleRule.FoamDepartSeconds), its shown count stepping down with them until it is gone. Each bubble hops to its taker on the merge's arc (SoapBubbleHop): a coat stays hidden (BalloonView.HoldCoat) until its bubble lands, and a neighbouring cluster holds its count and takes the bubble as a merge's receiver does (IBubbleMergeReactive). A leftover drifts a little way out and puffs away. A hop whose taker leaves its view (popped, despawned, bound again) is dropped, and that view let go of what it held.
