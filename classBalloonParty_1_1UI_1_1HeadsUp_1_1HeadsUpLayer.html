Detailed Description

Draws the heads-ups HeadsUpService lets through, as pooled HandwrittenHeadsUp lines cloned from _linePrefab.

A line written where something happened is packed clear of the lines still showing and inside the view (HeadsUpPacking); one at a HUD endpoint (the heart, a colour's bar, the cannon) follows it every frame. A line that can't be placed (its endpoint not registered yet, no camera) reports itself finished at once, so its slot isn't held forever.

Moves every line on.

Writing, holding and a line's own fade run on liveDelta , zero under a pause, so they freeze with the game, and a line shown under a pause waits unwritten. Released, evicted and cleared lines fade on unscaledDelta , which always runs, so a line a -1 pushes out during a pause doesn't stay frozen beside it.

void BalloonParty.UI.HeadsUp.HeadsUpLayer.Rewrite ( HeadsUpHandle handle,
in HeadsUpRequest request,
HeadsUpStyleSettings style )

The showing line of handle writes the request's number instead.

It stays where it is, whatever the request's anchor (a streak along a pierce would otherwise move at ball speed), and its write and hold start over, bringing back a line already fading. Several in one frame (a pierce ploughing a row) should cost one rewrite: keep the latest and apply it in Advance; an unchanged one (a held ready) does nothing.

Fires exactly once for every shown line that wasn't released, evicted or cleared first: when it has faded out by itself, or at once from inside Show when it can't be placed (an endpoint not registered yet, no camera).

Its slot is free again; a line that never reports holds it forever.
