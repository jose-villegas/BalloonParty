Detailed Description

The projectile struck a wall.

Position is the contact point and ShieldsRemaining is the shield count after this bounce. Published alongside ShieldLostMessage today (every bounce spends a shield), but a distinct event so wall hits and shield loss can diverge later without touching subscribers.
