Detailed Description

While a projectile is in flight, holding a finger on the screen lerps the global time scale up to IProjectileFlightConfig.HoldSpeedUpMax.

Releasing or projectile death lerps back to 1×. Only active between ProjectileFiredMessage and ProjectileDestroyedMessage.
