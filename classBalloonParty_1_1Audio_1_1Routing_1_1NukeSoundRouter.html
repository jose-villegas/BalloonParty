Detailed Description

The nuke's fuse and blast in sound (PLAN-NukeBalloon): a click and an alarm as it is armed, a beep on each count of its fuse, a step higher each time, the boom as it goes off, and a gust of wind as the breather after it starts.

Its dents' clank is CombatSoundRouter's, as for the other balloons with durability.
