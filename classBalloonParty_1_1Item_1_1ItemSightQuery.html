Detailed Description

The single place that scores a slot (or the whole board) against the aim's prediction trace for item-hosting purposes — pure geometry, no ordering or membership policy of its own, and deliberately no centrality threshold: a shallow graze along the very edge of an item's contact circle is still a real crossing (TryFindSurfaceHit returning true at all is the bar), matching what PredictionSightProbe's continuous ramp reactions already treat as sighted.

Every consumer that needs "is this item host sighted, and where" (today ItemRangePreviewController and ItemSightTracker) reads through here instead of re-deriving the same qualifying checks and TraceHitGeometry call independently, which is what let the two drift apart before this existed — ItemSightTracker used to add its own enter/exit hysteresis on top, which silently excluded exactly this kind of graze even though the preview walk and the per-item reactions both already counted it, undercounting a real chain (see its own remarks for why that hysteresis was removed). Ordering (by trace offset) and any further filtering (preview-registration) stay each consumer's own concern, layered on top of the raw hits this returns.
