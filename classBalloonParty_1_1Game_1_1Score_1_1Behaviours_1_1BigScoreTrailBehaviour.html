Detailed Description

Decomposes a big score into a catalog of 3D shapes and launches them all at once.

Every point is one orbiting pen trail: the group's total splits into the fewest pieces over ShapeCatalog.Denominations (optimal coin change; see Decompose); with 2 and 3 both denominations, every total this handler ever sees decomposes remainder-free, so each piece becomes one formation of that many pens with nothing left over (see AssertNoRemainder). The formations bloom around the pop at spread sub-centres, tumble and orbit toward the bar, and each reports its own contiguous score range on landing — the LARGEST takes the top range (so it carries LastScore and is the principal the level-up cinematic tracks). The ShapeFormationTicker owns the per-frame simulation; this handler only decomposes, lays out, fits, and launches.

◆ GetPrincipalId()

TrailId BalloonParty.Game.Score.Behaviours.BigScoreTrailBehaviour.GetPrincipalId ( in ScorePointsGroupMessage msg)

The id the level-up cinematic waits on and matches arrivals against.

The handler nominates it because only the handler knows which of its visual objects spawns immediately and is therefore safe under the cinematic's bounded registry wait — for the staggered default fan-out that is the FIRST trail (msg.FirstScore); a carrier that spawns at once could nominate LastScore.
