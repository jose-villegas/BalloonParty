Detailed Description

How a score shape's ink is drawn (ShapeInkView, on the shape's baked mesh): the material that gives the pen stroke its look, and the ink's width, fade and colour along its length.

The pen is the ink's head: the shader closes it with a round end as wide as the ink is there.
