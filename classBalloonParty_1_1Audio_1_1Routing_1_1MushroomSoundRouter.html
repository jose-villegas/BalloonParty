Detailed Description

The meadow's mushrooms in sound (Meadow Mushrooms): a soft bump on every bounce, a step higher with each wall of caps the chain counts, the Snipe's own cue when a chain pays the lance, a stroke for each cap a lance cuts, a step higher with each cap the flight has cut, and the Snipe's cue once more, a step up, for the first charge a flight's cuts bank it.

The bump is the mushrooms' own (BounceSound, MushroomBounce): a short rubbery clip made for it, with its own entry in the sound bank. So the entry's own limits are the bump's and nobody else's: four voices at once, the fifth ending the oldest bump, and the sound service's burst gate. A ball can bounce every 80 ms or so and the clip is 130 ms long, so two or three bumps overlap at the most, and this router keeps no voices of its own.

The other two are still borrowed. The reward plays what a Snipe pickup plays, so a lance earned off the mushrooms is announced as any lance is; the pierce loop that follows is CombatSoundRouter's, off the ball's own piercing state. The cut borrows the strikethrough's stroke (CutSound), the one sound in the bank that is a blade's pass: a lance cuts a handful of caps a flight at the most, each once.

The bank (decision K: every cap a lance cuts banks its flight a charge). A lance through a wall of caps banks three charges within a few frames, so the Snipe's cue is not played for each: it rings once a flight, on the first charge (MushroomCutMessage.Charges is 1), a whole tone above the pitch an arming plays it at, which is what a banked charge rang before the bank was dropped. Every charge is heard in its cut instead: the stroke climbs a whole tone with each cap the flight has cut, up to MaxCutSteps.

A blast plays nothing here. A cap a bomb or a nuke blows off (MushroomCutCause.Blast) is heard in the blast's own sound: a bomb taking four caps would otherwise play four strokes at once. A sway is silent too.
