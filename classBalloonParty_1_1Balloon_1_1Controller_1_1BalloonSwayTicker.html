Detailed Description

Plays the plain balloons' idle sway in one loop, in place of an Animator on each: a balloon's Animator cost far more a frame than the one rotation it wrote.

The sway itself is unchanged. Its curves and crossfade times are baked from the Animator controller (IBalloonIdleSwaySettings), and BalloonSwayPlayback steps each balloon through them as that controller's state machine did.

A balloon's view begins its sway as it spawns and ends it as it despawns or is handed to a cinematic that owns its rotation, where it used to enable and disable its Animator.

An ILateTickable on scaled time: after everything Update spawned and before any LateUpdate, which is where and on what clock the Animators wrote their pose.
