Detailed Description

Reads the run's retry state against IRunConfig.RetriesBeforeAd: the first retries are free, every one after needs a rewarded ad.

An ad that can't be shown never costs the player the retry; only skipping one does. The bonus retry is a reward, so it needs its ad watched.

Shows the rewarded ad and, once it's watched through, stages the retry at level with a bonus card.

A reward needs the ad watched: a skip, no fill or an SDK error stages nothing, and the plain retry is still on offer.
