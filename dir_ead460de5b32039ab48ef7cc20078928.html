Detailed Description

Help that comes only when the player needs it. A rolled help (a Snipe from the Sharpshooter card, a heart balloon, an item on a wave) keeps a low base chance, so a player doing well rarely sees it. While the player is in trouble, each roll it misses makes the next one likelier, until it hits. Being forgiving where it counts is what lets the base rates stay low. The same need also tilts the picks toward the items and balloons that help, and eases off the threats that come by chance.

File What it does
IPlayerNeed / PlayerNeed How much trouble the player is in, 0 to 1, read when a roll asks. It is the larger of two needs. The hearts' is 0 at full and 1 on the last heart, linear between (2 of 3 hearts is 0.5); hearts past full count as full. The board's is IDangerLevel.Level (1 when the next line would cost a heart) times IAssistSettings.DangerNeedWeight (1). A run of one heart reads no need from its hearts
ThreatEase Scales a threat's chance down by IAssistSettings.ThreatEaseAtFullNeed (0.5) times the need: half as likely on the last heart, unchanged at full health. Also hands the need to the balloon pick
PityRoll One forgiving roll. Each roll it misses adds the need at that moment to its pity, so misses out of trouble add nothing. The chance is base × (1 + GrowthPerMiss × pity × need now), capped at MaxChance (a base above the cap is kept). Out of trouble that is the base, so the help is there only while it is needed, and pity built earlier comes back with the trouble. A hit clears the pity

Each roll keeps its own pity, cleared by a hit and by a run reset. The Snipe's cooldown after a hit still applies, and loads inside it neither roll nor build pity.

The other side: pressure

Need only ever helps. The push back on a player who is comfortable lives in the spawn clock (Balloon/Spawner/SpawnClock, ISpawnClockSettings.Pressure*). While the board stays under its hold occupancy and the need is low, the clock runs ever faster, up to 2×, until the board climbs. The need slows that build, and on the last heart it builds none. A struggling player never builds it, and a strong build is pushed until its board climbs.

Adding a roll

Give the roll a PityRoll with its own PitySettings on AssistSettings, read IPlayerNeed.Need when it rolls, and clear the pity on a run reset. A roll with more outcomes than hit or miss (the item wave's count) draws its own result and reports it with PityRoll.Record, reading ChanceFor for the raised chance. A roll that already scales with need (the heart's chance grows with missing hearts) wants a gentler growth than one that doesn't.
