Detailed Description

Bakes a FormationShape into the wire mesh its ink is drawn on (the BalloonParty/Sprite/ShapeInk shader, through ShapeInkView), in the shape's own unit space.

Every closed walk is cut into segments, and the mesh is three kinds of square, each four vertices:

  • A ribbon piece for each segment: two vertices at each end, one for each side of the ribbon. They sit on the walk itself; the shader pushes them apart to the ribbon's width, facing the camera.
  • A joint at each corner of a walk that turns more than JointTurnDegrees: a square on the corner, in which the shader draws the ink round, so two ribbon ends meeting at a sharp corner close without a notch or a spike.
  • A pen's ball for each segment: a square on the segment's near end, which the shader moves to wherever on the segment a pen is and folds away while none is. A segment longer than the gap between two pens carries a square for each pen it can hold.

They are listed in that order, which is the order they draw in: joints over ribbons, balls over both. What each vertex carries:

  • UV0: its distance along the walk (x), the gap between two pens on that walk (y), the walk's index (z), and its kind (w): RibbonKind, JointKind, or FirstBallKind for the segment's first pen ball, one more for its second, and so on.
  • UV1: the segment's other end, or for a joint the walk's next point (xyz), and the vertex's side of the ribbon or of the square (w, -1 or 1).
  • UV2, ribbon: the walk's next point past this end (xyz), for the corner, and whether this is the segment's far end (w). UV2, joint and ball: the segment's far end's distance along the walk (x, balls only) and the square's corner along the segment (y, -1 or 1).
  • UV3, ribbon: the segment's other end's distance along the walk (x), so a pixel past the end of a stroke that runs on beyond its corner still knows the corner's distance.

Straight edges are one segment each. Arc edges are cut so no piece turns more than MaxArcStepDegrees, at points a pen passes through (FormationShape.PointOnWalk). The mesh has two submeshes with the same triangles: the view draws the shape's far half with one and its near half with the other, so the near side lies over the far side.
