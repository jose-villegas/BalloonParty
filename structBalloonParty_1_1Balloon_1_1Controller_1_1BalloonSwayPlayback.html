Detailed Description

One balloon's place in the idle sway.

It steps the way the plain balloon's Animator controller did, so the pose it gives is the pose that controller gave. Each rule here was read off a real Animator, step by step (BalloonSwayAnimatorParityTests):

  • A fresh playback (default) is at rest with Stable off, as an Animator starts in its empty default state with its parameters reset.
  • Each step first moves what is playing on, then looks at Stable. A step that finds the wanted loop isn't the playing one starts a crossfade into it, at no progress and with the new loop at its beginning: the pose that step is still the old one. From the next step the crossfade and the new loop move on, the old loop still playing under them.
  • A crossfade is never interrupted. Stable flipping back during one waits for it to end. The step it ends on plays the entered loop alone and starts nothing; the step after starts the crossfade back.
  • Two loops mix as rotations, not as angles: the two poses' quaternions are weighed by the crossfade's progress, summed and normalised. Between this sway's loops that is up to a tenth of a degree off a straight line between the angles.
  • Rest mixes with nothing, because the Animator's empty state has no pose to weigh against a clip's: the first loop plays whole from the step it's asked for. Its enter time still runs, as a crossfade nothing can interrupt, so a flip of Stable during it waits.
