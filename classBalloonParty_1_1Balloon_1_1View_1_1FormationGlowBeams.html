Detailed Description

Turns the formation glow's rings into the lights they cast on the scene (FormationGlowView, through the scene-light field).

A light per ring would spend the field's few lights on one long formation, so the rings of one formation that stand close enough to join are one group, and a group casts one beam: a capsule from its two members furthest apart, which is exact for a line or a diagonal and close for a ring round an item or a bent snake.

Groups the first count of points (xy centre, z brightness, w presence, as FormationGlowField.Step writes them, with each one's formation in formations ) and writes one beam per group into beams , returning how many.

Two rings of one formation within joinDistance of each other are in one group, and so is any chain of them; rings of different formations never are, as their outlines never join. groups is scratch, at least count long.
