Detailed Description

Teaches the golden balloon, which the shot's ordinary hit bounces off and only piercing pops: once the run's first golden balloon has turned away IAssistSettings.FirstGoldenSnipeAfterDeflects hits, the next ball loaded is a Snipe lance whatever the Sharpshooter card's chance (ISnipeGuarantee).

Once a run: a retry keeps it given.
