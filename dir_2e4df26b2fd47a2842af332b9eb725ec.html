Detailed Description

Every ball in flight carries a clear circle of one fixed radius, so the player can see what the ball is doing however busy the board gets. Score shapes slide out of the circles, and the effect shaders read them. This folder owns the circles; the features that keep out of them read them through IClearanceField and never look at the balls themselves. Design: Pen Clearance.

Contents

File What it does
ClearanceCircle One circle this frame: centre in the board's XY plane, effective radius (the settings' radius × strength), and strength 0..1
IClearanceField The read side: Count, CircleAt(i) (an index is good for one frame only), and Deflect(point), which bends a point out of every circle in turn
ClearanceGeometry The bend itself, for one circle: a pure function, EditMode-tested
ClearanceField The circles themselves: ClearanceCircle.MaxCircles (8) fixed slots, keyed by the ball's model reference. Plain C#, EditMode-tested (ClearanceFieldTests)
ClearanceFieldTicker IStartable + ITickable + IDisposable (game scope): publishes an empty field on start, then each frame tracks every ball in IProjectileRegistry's in-flight list into the field, advances it, and publishes it to the shader globals. On dispose it zeroes _ClearanceCount, since globals outlive the scope

Tuning lives in IClearanceSettings (Configuration/Effects/ClearanceSettings, the Assets/Configuration/ClearanceSettings.asset wired on the GameLifetimeScope prefab; an unwired scope falls back to the defaults).

A circle's life

  1. Launch. A ball that enters the registry's in-flight list takes a free slot, at strength 0. Its strength rises to 1 over FadeIn (0.15 s), so a circle never pops in.
  2. Flight. The circle follows the ball every frame.
  3. Landing. Once the ball leaves the in-flight list (it landed, or the run reset), its circle stays where the ball was last seen and fades to 0 over FadeOut (0.4 s). A ball's last pop is where most effects start, and a circle that vanished on the frame the ball did would let them close over that spot at once.
  4. Freed. At strength 0 the slot is free again.

The strength a reader sees is smoothstepped, and the radius is already scaled by it, so everything that keeps out of a circle lets go gently.

Eight slots cover the ball cap (3), every Extra Pen card (3) and a couple of circles still fading. A new ball takes a free slot, or, when none is free, the weakest fading one; it can't run out, since at most six balls fly at once. A ball's model can come back from the pool for a new flight while its old circle still fades; the new flight gets a fresh slot, so the old circle doesn't jump to the cannon.

The ball held in the thrower gets no circle: it sits below the board, where nothing draws over it.

Who reads it

  • Score formations (Game/Score/Behaviours/ShapeFormationTicker): after their own overlap packing, each moving formation is pushed out of every circle into its persistent SeparationOffset, at an eased speed of up to FormationPushSpeed world units a second. The circles never give way. See Game/Score/README.md, Overlap Resolution.
  • Score trails (UI/Score/FlyingTrail): ScoreTrailService builds its TrailSpawners with the field (or null with BendTrails off), and the spawner hands it to every trail it spawns on a flight. In LateUpdate, after DOTween or the follow has moved it, a trail on its own flight replaces its position with Deflect(position), so its ribbon curves round the ball. A head nothing moved since its last bend (a tween paused by the level-up cinematic) is left alone: the bend is not idempotent and would creep it out to the band's edge. The bend's soft band lies just outside the circle (out to radius × (1 + RimSoftness)) and everything inside is squeezed into it, so a bent trail never enters the circle; the effect fade's soft edge lies just inside, so a bent trail is never also faded. Formation pens, range pens and the heart trail don't bend (see the plan for why).
  • Effect shaders: the ticker publishes _ClearanceCircles (8 × float4: centre xy, effective radius, strength), _ClearanceCount, _ClearanceFloor and _ClearanceSoftness every frame. With FadeEffects off it publishes a count of 0. Shaders/BalloonParty/Include/Clearance.cginc reads them: ClearanceAlpha(worldXY) is 1 outside every circle and eases down to the floor inside. Materials opt in with the "Fade Round Balls" toggle (_CLEARANCE_FADE) on Grid/PuffCloud, Particle/Glow, Sprite/ShinyDefault and Paint/PaintBlob; it is on for the effects that draw over the ball (puff clouds, the laser, lightning, the bomb burst, paint blobs and splash, score trail ribbons) and off for the balloons and pickups that share those shaders. The shader README lists the materials.
