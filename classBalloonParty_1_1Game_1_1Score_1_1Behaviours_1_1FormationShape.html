Detailed Description

A drawable formation: its vertex count (== its score denomination == its trail count), the local 3D vertex positions (normalized to a unit bounding radius), and the closed walks its pens orbit.

Pens are distributed across walks proportionally to walk length (PensPerWalk, summing to the denomination), spaced evenly along each walk, and orbit continuously during Draw + Collapse: the first lap draws the shape, later laps re-ink it, and k pens sharing a period-P walk cover it in P/k.
