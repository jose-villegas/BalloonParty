Detailed Description

Hands ShapeFormationTicker a ShapeInkView for each shape in flight and takes it back when the shape is done.

Owns what the views share: each shape's baked mesh, built the first time that shape flies, and two copies of the ink material carrying the ink ramp, one drawing the half of a shape behind its centre and one, after it, the half in front.
