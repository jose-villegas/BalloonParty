Detailed Description

Pure polyline-vs-polyline math: where a trace first crosses one stroke of an ItemPreviewShape, expressed as an arc-length offset along that stroke.

Factored out so the intersection walk is edit-mode testable without the ticker's pen/pool machinery, mirroring BalloonParty.Prediction.TraceHitGeometry.

Finds where tracePoints first crosses shape 's stroke at strokeIndex , walking the trace segment by segment so "first" means earliest along the shot's own direction of travel rather than merely the first stroke segment tested.

A closed stroke's wrap leg (last point back to first) counts as a segment. Ties within one trace segment — more than one stroke segment crossed at once — are broken by the intersection nearest that trace segment's own start.

Parameters
arcTableThe caller's cumulative-arc-length table, parallel to ItemPreviewShape.Points: arcTable[stroke.Start + i] is the distance from the stroke's first point to its i-th (see ItemPreviewTicker.BuildArcTable). Read only, never rebuilt here.
offsetThe arc length from the stroke's first point to the crossing, along the stroke itself (not the trace). Zero when this returns false.
traceOffsetThe arc length from the trace's own first point to the crossing, measured along the trace polyline — lets a caller (the leading pen's approach) travel the trace itself rather than cut a straight line to the entry. Zero when this returns false.
