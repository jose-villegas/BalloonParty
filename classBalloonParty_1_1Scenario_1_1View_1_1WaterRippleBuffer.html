Detailed Description

The water's surface simulation, published as the global _WaterRippleTex (r = surface height, g/b = the water's flow across each texel's right and top edges, a = foam) with its texel size in _WaterRippleTexel, which BalloonParty/Scenario/Water reads.

A shallow-water solver over the disturbance field's bounds (Hidden/BalloonParty/Scenario/WaterRipple): height and flow trade back and forth so waves travel, surface tension sends short ripples ahead of long ones, the screen's edges reflect, crests foam where they fold over sharply, and the foam rides along with them. Each impact the game reports presses the water for a moment and drags it along the impact's heading, so a ball leaves a wake. Fixed steps on game time: the same at any frame rate, held by a pause, slowed by the pop slow-mo. Only while water shows.
