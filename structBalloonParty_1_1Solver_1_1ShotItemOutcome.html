Detailed Description

The buff/state side-effects one activation grants, applied by the caller (the item's EffectHits against the board are returned separately, via Resolve's hitsOut).

SpeedBuffMultiplier of 0 means "grants none" — 1f can't express that (it's also the buffless multiplier), mirroring why ShotFlightState.HasSpeedBuff exists. Damage/Flags (Shot Solver Accuracy Phase C2) are this activation's OWN item-type config — every EffectHitKind.Damage hit ApplyEffectHits applies for THIS activation reads them (a EffectHitKind.PiercingDamage hit ignores Flags entirely and always pops with DamageFlags.Piercing alone — see the class's own Bomb-case doc); 0/ Normal for Shield/Snipe, which never emit hits at all.
