Detailed Description

Bakes the plain balloon's idle sway out of its Animator controller into the Idle Sway block of BalloonsConfiguration, which BalloonSwayTicker plays for every balloon in place of an Animator each.

The controller and its clips stay where the sway is authored; this copies their curves and crossfade times, nothing typed by hand.

A curve is baked as the Animator plays it, which isn't always as it was drawn: see AsPlayed.

The ticker reproduces one shape of controller only: an empty default state, a stable and an unstable looping clip that each turn one child about Z, switched by the IsStable bool over fixed-time crossfades that can't be interrupted. Read refuses anything else and says why, so a controller that grows past that shape can't be baked into a sway that no longer matches it.

authored as the Animator plays it.

A stretch of curve between two keys is played as drawn unless either key's tangent on that side is weighted. A weighted stretch the Animator plays as straight lines between the curve's values at the clip's frames, up to a hundredth of a degree off the curve on this sway (read off a real Animator, which ShippedBalloonIdleSwayTests compares the result with). Such a stretch is baked as a key a frame.
