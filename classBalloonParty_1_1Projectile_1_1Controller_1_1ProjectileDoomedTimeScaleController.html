Detailed Description

Claims a global slow-motion time scale for the duration of a shot's doomed 'last breath' (see ProjectileDoomedStartedMessage) and releases it when the moment ends.

The time-scale value is driven by an animation curve sampled on normalized doomed progress (elapsed / duration), so the slow-mo can ease in and out instead of snapping to a flat value.
