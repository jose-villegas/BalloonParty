Detailed Description

One pencil stroke along a Snipe lance's run: a two-point LineRenderer carrying the aim trace's smoke-and-glitter material, so the shot's path reads in the same carbon hand as the cannon's aim.

Driven entirely by SnipeLanceLines; it only renders what it is told.
