Detailed Description

Projectile deflected off a balloon for the Countth time in a row without touching a wall (from DeflectChainRule.MinChain on).

Position is the deflecting balloon's. PaidShield says this deflect earned the ball a shield; its ShieldGainedMessage follows, marked ShieldGainSource.Ricochet.
