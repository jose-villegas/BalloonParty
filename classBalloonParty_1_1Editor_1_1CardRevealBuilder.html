Detailed Description

Builds the card reveal: imports the baked pop sheets under Assets/Sprites/UI/CardReveal/ as linear data, lists them on the CardRevealSettings asset (made if missing, and assigned to the game scope), makes the rubber's material, and builds the CardRevealView on GameCanvas: the balloon's back layer, the two ribbons (the card's title and what it does), its front layer.

The ribbons are cream satin written in the tooltip's pencil font. A starting look to restyle by hand; running it again rebuilds it.
