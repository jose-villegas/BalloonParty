Detailed Description

Builds the card reveal: imports the baked pop sheets under Assets/Sprites/UI/CardReveal/ as linear data, lists them on the CardRevealSettings asset (made if missing, and assigned to the game scope), makes the materials, and builds the CardRevealView on a canvas of its own, the CardRevealCanvas prefab, placed in the Game scene: the balloon's shadow and back layer, the two ribbons (the card's title and what it does), its front layer, and the pushpin that pricks it (its baked sheet, pin.png, lit as the rubber is).

The canvas is World Space, fitted to the camera (CameraFittedCanvas), on the Balloons layer and sorted just under the screen-space light, so the reveal takes part in the game's lighting as the board's balloons do. The ribbons are cream satin written in the tooltip's pencil font. A starting look to restyle by hand; running it again rebuilds it.
