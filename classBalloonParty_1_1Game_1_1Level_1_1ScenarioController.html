Detailed Description

Keeps ActiveScenario on the current level's scenario (sky or ground, per ILevelPacingConfiguration.ScenarioForLevel).

Cuts on run start and on a plain restart, so the start-level cheat and a restart land on the right backdrop. Reveals through the mist over the length of the beat on a level-up Ascent and on the restart rise after a loss. A loss's restart leaves the scenario alone until its rise starts, since the reveal scales the content root and the new scenery is placed under it first.
