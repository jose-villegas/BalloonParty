Detailed Description

The motion of a mushroom's cap after a hit (Meadow Mushrooms): a spring that is pushed along the ball's way in, so the cap leans away from the hit and wobbles back along that same line, and a second one that swells the cap when the bounce pays the lance.

Pure: MushroomView steps it and draws what it holds.
