Detailed Description

Counts the opening angles that collect every shield on the board, and draws them one at a time in the Scene view.

Shields planned as a chain still look like items on a hex lattice, so whether the board actually holds a chain is not answerable by looking at it. Drawing every opening at once is just as unreadable — a count, plus one path at a time, is what makes the answer legible.
