Detailed Description

Bomb's telegraph: a circle at the blast's kill radius, centred on the host.

Reads BombSettings.Radius — the same field BombBlast selects targets with — and deliberately NOT RainbowEffectScale, which scales only the activation VFX and never the kill radius (see BombItemHandler.Activate). A telegraph drawn at the visual scale would promise reach the blast doesn't have.

The drawn circle is the radius alone, plus IBombPreviewSettings.RadiusOffset — a display-only nudge on top of the source of truth. BombBlast actually catches an occupant whose centre lies within the radius plus its OWN radius, so the true catchment is meaningfully larger than the bare-radius circle; a small positive offset reads CLOSER to that real reach, not further from it. Revisit if players read the edge as exact.
