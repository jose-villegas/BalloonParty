Detailed Description

One short handwritten line on the canvas: written on in pencil where it's asked for, held, then faded out.

A new Show(string, Vector2) replaces the line showing. Written plain it keeps the label's own look; written with a HeadsUpStyle it takes that colour, size and weight for this line only.
