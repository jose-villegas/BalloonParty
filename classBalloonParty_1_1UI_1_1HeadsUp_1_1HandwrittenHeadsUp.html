Detailed Description

One short handwritten line in world space (a TextMeshPro, not on a canvas), so it sits where it happened through the camera rig's zoom and shake: written on in pencil, held, then faded out, or held until released.

Pooled by HeadsUpLayer, which moves it on with two clocks: the write, the hold and its own fade on live time, which stops under a pause; a release fades on unscaled time, so a line pushed out during a pause doesn't stay frozen beside the one that replaced it.
