Detailed Description

One short handwritten line in world space (a TextMeshPro, not on a canvas), so it sits where it happened through the camera rig's zoom and shake: written on in pencil, held, then faded out.

A new Show(string, Vector2) replaces the line showing. Written plain it keeps the label's own look; written with a HeadsUpStyle it takes that colour, size and weight for this line only.
