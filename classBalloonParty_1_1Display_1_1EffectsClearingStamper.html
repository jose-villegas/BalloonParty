Detailed Description

Turns the balls in flight into the clearing mask's stamps: one capsule per ball, from where it was last frame to where it is now, in mask UV (xy the start, zw the end).

A capsule rather than a disc so a fast ball leaves an unbroken corridor. A ball not stamped last frame (just fired, or back in flight after a gap) stamps a disc, and so does every ball while the clearing has no trail. Each stamp's radius grows with its ball's shields.
