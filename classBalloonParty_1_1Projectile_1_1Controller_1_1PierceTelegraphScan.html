Detailed Description

Picks the tough a piercing shot's telegraph points at out of everything its forward cast crossed: the nearest one it hasn't already plowed this run.

A piercing shot pops ordinary balloons in passing, so they never stop the line — only a tough is an armored contact worth warning of.
