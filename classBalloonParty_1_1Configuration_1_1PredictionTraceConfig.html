◆ MaxReflections

int BalloonParty.Configuration.PredictionTraceConfig.MaxReflections
get

Reflections the trace will draw before it stops — walls and balloon deflections together, because to the player they are the same event: the line turned.

The leg leaving the last allowed reflection is still drawn; the one after it is not.

Deliberately one budget rather than one per kind. The telegraph is an aid, not a solution — showing the whole ricochet answers the shot for the player. Expected to rise with progression.
