int BalloonParty.Solver.ShotMushroomTally.LancesArmed

How many times the flight's mushrooms armed the ball as the lance.

The chain rule arms only a ball that is not a lance, so this passes 1 only when a lance was spent at a wall and a later chain armed the ball again.
