Detailed Description

Gathers the board's unbreakables into formations, as many as there are members for.

At each wave start the balancer asks it to arrange: each formation holds for IUnbreakableFormationSettings.HoldWaves waves, then plans a different shape, and the unbreakables left free form new ones. It hands the balancer the moves and anchors the members. A formation that loses members below the minimum breaks at once. One that stands untouched may call in another unbreakable (IFormationReinforcements).

void BalloonParty.Balloon.Formation.UnbreakableFormationService.ArrangeAtWaveStart ( Action< IWriteableDynamicSlotActor, Vector2Int > relocate)
package

Runs once per wave start, before the roam pre-pass.

Every formation that stood untouched through the last wave rolls for a reinforcement. A formation still holding counts down; one done holding plans its next shape, or breaks if none fits. Then the free unbreakables form new formations while there are enough of them. relocate moves each member and owns the grid change and the glide.
