Detailed Description

Gathers the board's unbreakables into formations, as many as there are members for.

On each cycle of the unbreakables' clock (IUnbreakableFormationSettings.BeatsPerCycle) the balancer asks it to arrange: each formation holds for its shape's hold cycles, then plans a different shape, and the unbreakables left free form new ones. It hands the balancer the moves and anchors the members. A formation that loses members below the minimum breaks at once. One that stands untouched may call in another unbreakable (IFormationReinforcements). A level may allow only some shapes (ILevelParameters.AllowedFormationShapes, the snake alone on the meadow): a shape it doesn't allow is never planned, so with none left to plan the unbreakables stay loose. The board's toughs are one snake of their own (IUnbreakableFormationSettings.ToughSnakes): it starts from the deepest with every other tough lined up behind it at once, even while they are still gliding in, each tough that arrives later falls in behind its tail as soon as it appears, and it walks through the balloons in its way, keeping its order and its shape, until its last member pops. The balloon an item cover stands round is its host (ICoveredHosts) for as long as the cover holds that shape, and the service holds it in its slot meanwhile (IWriteableCoverHost), anchored as the members are, so it stays inside.

Runs once per cycle of the unbreakables' clock, before the lone unbreakable's move.

On a reshape (the run's first cycle, then every IUnbreakableFormationSettings.ReshapeEveryCycles) every formation plans its next shape, or breaks if none fits, and then the free unbreakables form new formations while there are enough of them; on any other cycle every formation holds. Then the toughs start their snake if none walks. relocate carries out a batch of moves at once and owns the grid change and the glides.

Every unbreakable snake steps once: its head into the next open slot (SnakeWalk), each member after it into the slot the one ahead just left.

A snake boxed in turns round and its tail leads. Like ArrangeOnCycle, relocate carries out each step's moves at once. afterArranging : the same balance just arranged on a cycle, and a snake formed or re-planned there waits for the next beat rather than stepping off its slots the moment it lands.

void BalloonParty.Balloon.Formation.UnbreakableFormationService.TurnOnTheBeat ( Action< IReadOnlyList< SlotMove > > relocate,
bool afterArranging = false )

Every ring and every spinner turns once, the way it is turning.

A ring's members each go to the next of its centre's neighbours; a spinner's go 60° round the member in its middle. A balloon in the way goes to a slot the formation leaves. One that cannot turn that way turns back at once, and one that cannot turn either way misses the beat. Like StepSnakes, relocate carries out each turn's moves at once, and with afterArranging a formation formed or re-planned in the same balance waits for the next beat.
