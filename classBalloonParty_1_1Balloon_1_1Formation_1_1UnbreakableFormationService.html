Detailed Description

Gathers the board's unbreakables into formations, as many as there are members for.

At each wave start the balancer asks it to arrange: each formation holds for IUnbreakableFormationSettings.HoldWaves waves, then plans a different shape, and the unbreakables left free form new ones. It hands the balancer the moves and anchors the members. A formation that loses members below the minimum breaks at once. One that stands untouched may call in another unbreakable (IFormationReinforcements). The board's toughs are one snake of their own (IUnbreakableFormationSettings.ToughSnakes), ordered by armour: it starts from the toughest with every landed tough lined up behind it at once, each tough that lands later falls in behind its tail as soon as it lands, and it walks through the balloons in its way until its last member pops.

void BalloonParty.Balloon.Formation.UnbreakableFormationService.ArrangeAtWaveStart ( Action< IReadOnlyList< SlotMove > > relocate)
package

Runs once per wave start, before the roam pre-pass.

Every formation that stood untouched through the last wave rolls for a reinforcement. A formation still holding counts down; one done holding plans its next shape, or breaks if none fits. Then the free unbreakables form new formations while there are enough of them, and the toughs start their snake if none walks. relocate carries out a batch of moves at once and owns the grid change and the glides.

package

Every snake steps once: its head into the next open slot (SnakeWalk), each member after it into the slot the one ahead just left.

A snake boxed in turns round and its tail leads. Like ArrangeAtWaveStart, relocate carries out each step's moves at once. afterArranging : the same balance just arranged at wave start, and a snake formed or re-planned there waits for the next beat rather than stepping off its slots the moment it lands.

True when the toughs can form up: none is still gliding in, and either IUnbreakableFormationSettings.MinMembers toughs are on the board with no snake yet, or a snake walks and a tough has landed free of it.

The balancer asks every frame, so the toughs never wait for a wave start or a beat. Read-only.
