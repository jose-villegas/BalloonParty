◆ DwellSeconds

float ISpinningItemVisual. BalloonParty.Item.LaserItemRotation.DwellSeconds
getprivate

How long, in seconds, the item rests at one angle before a transition to the next begins — the same span IsSettled reads true for within one step.

Lets a consumer compare its own re-draw cadence against how long a spin actually dwells (ItemRangePreviewController's hold-loop-vs-rotation arbitration) without re-deriving the rotation's own authored step length and easing fraction.

Implements BalloonParty.Item.ISpinningItemVisual.

◆ IsSettled

bool ISpinningItemVisual. BalloonParty.Item.LaserItemRotation.IsSettled
getprivate

True while AngleDegrees is holding still (dwelling on a step, or a frozen snapshot with nothing left to move), false while a transition toward a new angle is still in flight.

Lets a consumer that only ever sees the raw angle — the aim-time range telegraph (ItemRangePreviewController) — tell "arrived" from "still easing in" without inferring it from a frame-to-frame delta, which a Mathf.SmoothStep ease drives toward zero on its own tail and would misread as arrived early.

Implements BalloonParty.Item.ISpinningItemVisual.
