Detailed Description

A bubble cluster that reaches IBalloonsConfiguration.BubbleMergeMax is ripe: any hit bursts it whole, and a fuse on the level clock runs until it hands its bubbles out and leaves.

◆ BurstSeconds

float BalloonParty.Configuration.Balloons.IRipeBubbleSettings.BurstSeconds
get

Seconds of level clock from the hit that pops a ripe cluster to its ripple: the cluster folds into one big bubble meanwhile, and the ripple lands as that bubble pops.

A ripe neighbour the ripple pops waits as long again.
