Detailed Description

A bubble cluster that reaches IBalloonsConfiguration.BubbleMergeMax is ripe: any hit bursts it whole, a small bomb whose blast (the ripple) hits every balloon within RippleRadius, and a fuse on the level clock runs until it hands its bubbles out and leaves.
