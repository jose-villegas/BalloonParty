Detailed Description

How one kind of heads-up looks, so each kind reads at a glance: its colour, its size against the line's own font size, how much it grows as its count escalates (a longer chain, more pickups) and its weight.

Words inside the text can carry their own rich-text tags on top (an italic verb).

Growth is logarithmic and capped: equal ratios read as equal steps (Weber–Fechner), so doubling the count adds the same visible step, and a long chain tops out instead of filling the screen.
