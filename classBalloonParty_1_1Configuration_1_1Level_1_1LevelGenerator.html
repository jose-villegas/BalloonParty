Detailed Description

Builds a level's mix from LevelGenerationSettings.

Pure and seeded: the same level and seed always give the same mix.

Staples (cost 0) and pieces still in their featured levels are always in. Each role's budget then buys a pick of its other unlocked pieces. Threats and aids have separate budgets so that depth reliably means more threats, not a random mix of threats and help. Picks are weighted by cost, so the dearer threats aren't the ones a tight budget always leaves out.
