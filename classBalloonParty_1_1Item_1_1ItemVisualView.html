◆ SetRainbow()

void BalloonParty.Item.ItemVisualView.SetRainbow ( bool enabled)

Rainbow holder: flips PaintBlob-shaded sprites to their radial palette rings (the global rainbow bands) instead of a flat tint.

A no-op on sprites whose shader lacks _RainbowEnabled; the flag is per-renderer so pooled reuse resets cleanly.
