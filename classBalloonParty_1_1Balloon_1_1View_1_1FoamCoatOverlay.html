Detailed Description

The soap bubble round a balloon wearing foam (BalloonParty.Slots.Capabilities.ICoatable): a child sprite drawn with the soap cluster's material showing one bubble, centred on the balloon's body and sized to enclose it.

BalloonView creates it the first time its balloon is coated, so the balloons that never are carry nothing; it shows it while the coat lasts and hides it on bind, pop and despawn, so a pooled view never comes back coated. A coat a hit takes off puffs away (PuffAndHide) rather than vanishing. All per-instance shader state goes through one MaterialPropertyBlock, so every coat batches on the shared material.

staticpackage

The centre and radius of body 's sprite in host 's local space, from the local position, rotation and scale of each transform between the two.

Neither the host's own scale nor anything above it enters, so a balloon held at zero scale through its spawn flight, or caught mid-pulse, measures as it does at rest. A link at zero scale counts as 1: the body is mid-tween, and its rest size is the one to fit. A body that is not under the host measures as a unit balloon at the centre.
