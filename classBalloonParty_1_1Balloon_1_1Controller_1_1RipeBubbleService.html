Detailed Description

Ripens bubble clusters that reach the merge cap (RipeBubbleRule) and runs each one's fuse on the level clock (ISpawnClock.SecondsOnLevel), which holds through pause, the level-up and game over.

A ripe cluster popped by a hit bursts as the player's ripple (RipeBubbleBurst.Ripple) IRipeBubbleSettings.BurstSeconds after the hit, as the big bubble its view folds into pops; a ripe neighbour the ripple pops does the same in turn. One whose fuse runs out hands its bubbles to its neighbours and leaves the board, the level's foam (RipeBubbleBurst.PlanFoam, RipeBubbleBurst.HandOut). Either way a RipeBubbleBurstMessage goes out, and each throb of a fuse sends a RipeFuseBeatMessage. Looks for new ripe clusters when anything could have filled one: the grid changing, a cluster being hit, a merge, or a hand-out. A level change, game over or run reset forgets every fuse and every burst still waiting.
