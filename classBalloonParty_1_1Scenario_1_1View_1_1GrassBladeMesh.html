Detailed Description

Builds the one mesh that holds every grass blade of the ground: a jittered grid over a tile, each blade a tapered five-vertex strip.

The mesh carries no blade shape: every vertex of a blade sits on its root, and BalloonParty/Scenario/GrassBlades places, bends and wraps it per frame. A jittered grid tiles seamlessly, so the shader can wrap the roots as the meadow scrolls.
