Detailed Description

The slots of a meadow level's mushrooms: walls of caps, from the baked catalogue (MushroomWallPicker).

StaticActorSpawner uses it for the Mushroom entry, whatever placement mode that entry has, and asks it for as many walls as the level's gate says; it answers with the caps, wall by wall, and the spawner asks which wall each belongs to (WallOf).

It reads the board at the moment it is asked: the slots nothing stands in are the ones handed to it. The spawner asks it before it places anything else, so the pick depends on the level's seed alone and a retry stands the walls where they stood. What is placed after the mushrooms (the clouds, the bushes) keeps off the slots next to a cap (IsBesideACap), so a wall reads as a wall.
