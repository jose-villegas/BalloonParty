Detailed Description

The light the water's surface focuses on the bottom, published as the global _WaterCausticTex that BalloonParty/Scenario/Water lays under its surface.

Each frame one blit of Hidden/BalloonParty/Scenario/WaterCaustics draws one tile of the moving caustic net into a small square target that wraps, with mips, so the water can tile it at any scale and read it softer where the water is deep. Only while water shows.
