Detailed Description

Every occupied slot whose actor would turn an ordinary shot away, as circles.

Walks the grid rather than a type-specific registry because deflecting is a capability, not a family: toughs and unbreakables deflect, and so do the static deflector and a gatekeeper with hits left. Asking the grid asks all of them at once — a new deflecting archetype needs no change here.

Geometry comes from the spawned view, not from the slot's world position: an actor animating into place, or one with an offset collider, deflects where its collider is.
