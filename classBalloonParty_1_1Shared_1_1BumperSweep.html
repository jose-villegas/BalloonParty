Detailed Description

The swept test of a ball against the board's bumpers: which bumper circle the ball's centre enters first on a straight run, and how far along.

The flight, and everything that looks ahead of the ball, ask the one test, so they agree on what a mushroom is in the way of.

A run is tested whole, from its start to its end, so no speed carries a ball through a bumper between two fixed steps. The answer is the exact entry point of the centre's path into the circle (CircleContact.TryFindEntry), not where a step happened to end, which is what IBumperBounceRule asks of its callers.

How far along direction (unit length) from from the ball's centre enters bumper .

Ahead of the ball the distance is above zero. A ball already inside the circle (set down there by the tail of a wall bounce or of a deflect) entered it behind itself: the distance is then zero or less, the entry being where its line came in. False when the line misses, when the circle is behind the ball, or when the ball is on its way out within a rounding error of the edge.

The first bumper the ball's centre enters flying length from from along direction (unit length), or false when the run is clear.

entryDistance is how far along the run the entry is. It is zero or less when the run starts inside the circle (see TryFindEntry): the entry then lies that far behind the start, and it wins over every entry ahead. skipId names a bumper to leave out: the one the ball is leaving, which a line close to its tangent would be read as entering again. Pass MushroomChainState.NoBumper for none.
