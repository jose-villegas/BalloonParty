Detailed Description

The room a drifting launch balloon takes up around its body's centre, and from that where the centre may be on a screen.

Alone it has the whole screen. Two balloons share it one above the other (RoomAbove, RoomBelow): everything of the upper one, down to the bottom of its ribbon, stays a gap above everything of the lower one wherever each is in its room. A ribbon streams further sideways than a phone is wide enough to keep two apart, but hangs no lower than at rest, so apart in height is the one arrangement in which neither can cover the other's. The lower balloon's shadow is kept under the gap too, so it never darkens the upper one's ribbon.
