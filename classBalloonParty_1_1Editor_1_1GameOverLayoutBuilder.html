Detailed Description

Rebuilds the game-over screen in the UI theme ("glossy balloon": layered buttons and panels that carry the balloons' specular, see UI/GameOver/README.md).

It makes a card sized to its content with the title on a plaque across its top edge, the result in a cream well, Retry as the one green primary button, the ways to make the retry easier as tiles (each with a ribbon across its corner saying what it gives), and Start Over as a red button, smaller than Retry: it throws the run away. The Retry and bonus retry buttons are kept and moved in, so their clicks and sounds survive. The prefab is a variant of Popup.prefab, whose own Restart button can't move, so the builder replaces it with a copy of its components and the same click, and removes the rest of the old panel. Running it again rebuilds the screen the same way. Edges are soft (outlines are glows), the speculars run along each shape's rim toward the time-of-day light (TimeOfDaySpecular) and the faces take a little of its tint.
