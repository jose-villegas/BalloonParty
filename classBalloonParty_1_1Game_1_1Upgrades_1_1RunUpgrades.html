Detailed Description

The cards a run holds, the hand a level-up deals, and the weapon the cards add up to.

The hand follows the level-up phase: it opens on LevelUpPhase.Pending, its pick is held on LevelUpPhase.Transitioning (the level has advanced), and anything else voids it. So a card is held only once the level that dealt it is completed, and the cards held at a loss are the ones held when that level began, which is what a retry keeps. Each card held at a level-up is announced with UpgradeCardHeldMessage (the card reveal's cue). A retry bought with a rewarded ad also gets a bonus card: dealt at the retry's reset (announced with BonusCardDealtMessage), applied on top of the held cards, and dropped when that attempt ends, won or lost.
