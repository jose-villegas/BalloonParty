Detailed Description

Writes a line on both faces of a RibbonCloth's ribbon, each glyph corner carried on the cloth where it sits, so the writing rides the folds.

The back face's copy is laid along the ribbon mirrored: seen from behind, once the ribbon has swung round, it reads the right way. Each glyph shows on the face turned to the screen where it sits and fades out as that part of the ribbon turns edge on. Both texts are laid out flat first (Layout), centred on this object; the cloth is simulated in their local space.
