Detailed Description

Tuning of the meadow's mushrooms (Meadow Mushrooms): the chain that pays the lance, the bounce, where mushrooms may stand, and how a cap answers a hit.

◆ Blast

MushroomBlastTuning BalloonParty.Configuration.GridActors.IMushroomSettings.Blast
get

How far a bomb's blast sways the mushrooms whose caps it leaves on, as a share of the bomb's kill radius (MushroomBlastRule).

Which caps it takes off is the bomb's own rule and no setting: the slots next to it.
