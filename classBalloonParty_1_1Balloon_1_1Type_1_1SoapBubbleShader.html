Detailed Description

The SoapBubbleCluster shader's per-instance properties and the drift every soap bubble shares: the cluster (SoapBubbleClusterVariant) and the foam coat round a balloon (BalloonParty.Balloon.View.FoamCoatOverlay) draw with the same material, so a coat floats and spins as a cluster does.

How far the shader's layout for count bubbles reaches from the quad's centre to the outer edge of its farthest bubble, in bubble radii (bubbleRadius , in quad widths): 1 for a lone bubble, about 2.1 for the ring of six at the default radius.

Counts outside 1 to 6 read as the nearest; the per-bubble size variance is left out.

The _TimeOffset that keeps a bubble's float where it is when its speed changes at time (the shader's _Time.y).

The shader floats on _Time.y * _FloatSpeed + _TimeOffset, so a new speed alone would jump the bubbles to another phase.
