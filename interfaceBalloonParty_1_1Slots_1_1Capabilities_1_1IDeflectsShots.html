Detailed Description

A slot actor that bounces an ordinary shot rather than letting it through or popping.

Asked by the aim telegraph and the shield planner, both of which need the answer WITHOUT mutating anything — IHitable.EvaluateHit spends durability, so it cannot be used to preview. A capability rather than a base-class rule because deflecting is not a balloon trait: the static archetypes deflect too, and a rule living on the balloon hierarchy is invisible to them by construction.
