Detailed Description

How large the quad a mushroom is drawn on has to be.

The shader draws the cap, its pieces and its shadow from the quad's own position and never past the mesh's corner, so the mesh must hold the most the mushroom can ever need at once: the cap at its largest, leaned and rocked its furthest, and its shadow beside it. MushroomPrefabBuilder sizes the quad by this, and a test holds the shipped quad to it, so a number raised without the prefab rebuilt is caught before the cap's edge is cut off square.
