Detailed Description

Builds everything a meadow mushroom needs to appear in play (Meadow Mushrooms), or brings it up to date: the material on the Grid/Mushroom shader, the quad the shader draws on, the Mushroom prefab (a MushroomView and the quad's renderer, no collider), and the GridActorType.Mushroom entry in the grid actor configuration, which is what makes the spawner place them.

Safe to run again: the material keeps the values tuned on it, the quad and the prefab keep their place in the project, and the entry is added once and pointed at the prefab each time. It shows no dialog, so a script can call it.
