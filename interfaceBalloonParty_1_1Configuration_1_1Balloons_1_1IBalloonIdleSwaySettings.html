Detailed Description

The plain balloon's idle sway, baked from its Animator controller so one ticker (BalloonSwayTicker) plays it for every balloon in place of an Animator each.

The controller stays the place the sway is authored: Tools ▸ BalloonParty ▸ Balloons ▸ Bake Idle Sway reads it again.
