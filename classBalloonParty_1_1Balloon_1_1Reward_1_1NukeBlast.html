Detailed Description

The nuke going off (PLAN-NukeBalloon), when its fuse runs out (NukeFuse).

The nuke pops and every shot in flight dies (ClearShotsInFlightMessage). Then a disc grows from it across the whole board, easing out over INukeBlastSettings.Seconds. Each balloon and grid actor is nudged outward just before the edge arrives (INukeBlastSettings.NudgeLead). Each balloon the edge reaches pops through IBalloonPopper: its pop VFX play and its slot is freed, but no hit is published, so nothing scores, no streak moves and no item fires. The other reward balloons and the grid actors stay. When the time is up a last sweep pops anything left, a line that landed meanwhile included, and the spawn clock gets the breather a big clear earns (IBreatherGrant). A run reset, a level change or game over stops the blast where it is, with no breather. It runs on scaled time, so a pause holds it. The light, the push and the cloud are the views'.
