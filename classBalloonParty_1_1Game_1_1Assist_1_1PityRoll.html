Detailed Description

A chance roll that forgives a player in trouble.

Each roll missed while in need adds that need to its pity, and the chance is the base raised by IPitySettings.GrowthPerMiss times the pity times the need now, up to IPitySettings.MaxChance. A hit clears the pity. Out of need the chance is the base and no pity builds, so the help comes only when it is needed, and the base can stay low.
