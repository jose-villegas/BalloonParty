void BalloonParty.Slots.Capabilities.IHasScoreColor.ResolveScoreAttribution ( in DamageContext context,
IReadOnlyList< string > incompleteColors,
IList< ScoreAttribution > results )

incompleteColors lists the colours whose progress bar isn't full yet — scatter implementers (Tough/BubbleCluster/Unbreakable) confine their random split to these so completing a colour never wastes points on it.

Single-colour attributions ignore it.

Implemented in BalloonParty.Balloon.Model.BalloonModel, BalloonParty.Balloon.Model.BubbleClusterModel, BalloonParty.Balloon.Model.ToughBalloonModel, and BalloonParty.Balloon.Model.UnbreakableBalloonModel.
