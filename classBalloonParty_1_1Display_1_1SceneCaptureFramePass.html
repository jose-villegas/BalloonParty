Detailed Description

Draws the scene capture inside the main camera's frame, so no second camera goes through the pipeline for it.

The capture camera stays as the capture's description (its culling mask, view, projection, target and clear colour) and never renders: SceneCaptureService culls with its parameters, and this pass draws what that cull found into its target with its camera properties, as the camera would.
