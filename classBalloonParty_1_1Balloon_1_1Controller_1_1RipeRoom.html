Detailed Description

What a bubble cluster may be filled to right now (RipeBubbleRule.FillLimit), which keeps ripe clusters few and apart: on a fast clock, with nothing to stop them, full clusters followed one another into a run of blasts with no gap.

A merge or a foam's hand-out fills a cluster to IBalloonsConfiguration.BubbleMergeMax, where it ripens, only while fewer than IRipeBubbleSettings.MaxRipeClusters clusters on the board are full and IRipeBubbleSettings.RipenCooldownBeats of the bubbles' off-beats (IBoardBeat, IRipeBubbleSettings.MergeOffBeat) have passed since a ripe cluster last ended (RipeEnded); otherwise it stops a bubble short and waits there, unripe. The gap is counted on the rhythm clusters merge on, so it is over on an off-beat, when the merge service looks. Whoever fills clusters counts the full ones once (CountFull) and adds each it fills, so one pass that could fill several stops at the cap. With ripeness off (IRipeBubbleSettings.FuseSeconds 0) nothing is limited.
