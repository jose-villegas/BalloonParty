Detailed Description

The pierce-arming item hosts the aim telegraph must know about, in the geometry the projectile will actually meet — so it can stop drawing a deflection off every tough beyond the first one hit, matching a piercing shot's real behaviour.

Positions come from the views, not from slot coordinates, for the same reason as IDeflectorField: balloons drift between slots while the board settles.
