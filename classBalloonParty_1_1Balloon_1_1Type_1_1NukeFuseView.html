Detailed Description

The nuke's fuse on its body (PLAN-NukeBalloon).

The body shader draws the LED counter and the lamp from what is pushed here: _FuseArmed (the lamp green or red, and the LED a heart or the count), _FuseDigit (the count) and _FuseBeat (when the last count came, so the lamp blinks with the beep). Armed, the god rays behind it turn from their green palette entry to _armedColorId.
