Detailed Description

How large each layer looks at one frame of a reveal, scaled about the camera.

The ground sits deeper than the sky, so going down it grows into place while the sky grows past the camera, and going up the ground shrinks away while the sky settles. Incoming and Outgoing are the same two scales sorted by which level the board's content belongs to.
