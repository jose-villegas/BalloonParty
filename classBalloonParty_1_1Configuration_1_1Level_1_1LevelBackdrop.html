Detailed Description

The scenario a level's backdrop shows.

A bonus (extra-colour) level plays on paper; every other level shows its scheduled scenario. Only the look changes: the level's content (puff clouds, bushes, bubbles) still follows ILevelPacingConfiguration.ScenarioForLevel.
