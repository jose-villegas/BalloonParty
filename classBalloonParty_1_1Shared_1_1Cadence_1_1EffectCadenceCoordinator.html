Detailed Description

Assigns staggered phase offsets to all ICadencedEffect services at startup, ensuring their periodic RT blits never cluster on the same frame.

On tile-based mobile GPUs (Adreno/Mali) each blit forces a tile flush to DRAM (~0.3 ms); spreading them reduces worst-case per-frame cost from 10+ flushes to 2-3.

The heaviest services (highest ICadencedEffect.BlitWeight) are placed at maximum phase separation (0.0 and 0.5), lighter services fill the gaps evenly.
