Detailed Description

Substitutes the pop wave: detaches the old balloons into the transition's outgoing group (off the grid, out of logic, reparented under ScenarioContentRoot.OutgoingBalloons so they travel with the fake-camera descent), then floats each up on a phase-randomized sine with a sway tilt, and hands them back to the pool.

The level-up alternative to BoardPopWave.
