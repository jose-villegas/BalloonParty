Detailed Description

A ring's geometry: its members hold neighbours of one slot, its centre, and it turns by each member taking the next neighbour round, clockwise or the other way (HexCoordinates.Step numbers the six from east, clockwise).

One neighbour at least is always open, so a shot can reach the centre through the gap as it comes round. Pure: it reads the grid and plans moves, and moves nothing.

Plans one turn into moves : each member to the next neighbour of centre , and each balloon a member lands on to a slot the ring leaves.

False, with no moves, when a member is not beside the centre, when a slot the ring would turn into holds something it can't trade places with (SnakeWalk.CanPassThrough), or when the turn would close wall (null checks nothing).
