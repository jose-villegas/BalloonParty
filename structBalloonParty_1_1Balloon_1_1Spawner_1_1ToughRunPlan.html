Detailed Description

Where the initial fill deals the toughs when they walk as one snake: side by side on one line, so they rise together and land as one body.

Line is the deepest board line a snake walks; Length toughs take it from column Start eastward, and any past a full line go on the line above it, UpperLength of them from the east edge westward, so the body bends round the line's east end. A Line of -1 means no run.
