Detailed Description

A bomb's blast reached a meadow mushroom without taking its cap off (Meadow Mushrooms): the mushroom is not in one of the six slots next to the bomb, where caps are blown off, and its cap still overlaps the blast.

The cap is pushed away from the blast and springs back toward it, the same elastic reaction a ball's hit gives. Published once for each such mushroom, in the frame the bomb goes off, beside the MushroomCutMessages of the caps the same bomb blew off.

Nothing about the rule changes for a mushroom that swayed: its cap is whole, the ball bounces off it and the chain counts it as before. Only whole caps sway: a bare stem (a cap already cut or blown off) gets no message. A nuke sways nothing, since it blows every cap off.

How strong to expect it. With the shipped numbers (slots 0.75 apart, a kill radius of 1.25, a cap radius of 0.3, a sway's share of 2.4) the sway reaches 3.3 from the bomb: the second ring of slots, 1.30 or 1.50 away, gets about 0.78 or 0.71, the third (1.98, 2.25) about 0.52 or 0.41, and the fourth (2.60 to 3.00) 0.27 down to 0.12. At a share of 1 only the second ring is reached, at 0.31 and 0.06, which a view cannot show (IMushroomSettings.Blast).
