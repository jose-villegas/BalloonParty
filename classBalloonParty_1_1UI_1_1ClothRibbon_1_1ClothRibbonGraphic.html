Detailed Description

Draws a RibbonCloth's ribbon: its three rows of points as a strip, notched at both ends, seen from whichever side faces the screen.

It feeds BalloonParty/Sprite/CelRibbon what RibbonGraphic does (how far each fold turns, the row, and a copy of the strip for the drop shadow), the turn read from the cloth's normal. The cloth is simulated in this graphic's own local space.
