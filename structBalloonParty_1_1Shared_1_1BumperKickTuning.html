Detailed Description

The numbers of the forgiving kick (BumperKick): how wide its cone is, and how far beside a mushroom's centre it may land the ball.

Authored on the mushroom settings (IMushroomSettings.Kick).

An offset is the ball's impact parameter on a cap as a share of the radius it bounces at (BumperCircle.Radius): 0 is dead centre, 1 a graze. A ball landing at an offset turns by TurnDegrees there, so the two offsets are also the range of turns a kicked ball can make.
