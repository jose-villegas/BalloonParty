Detailed Description

When one plated reward balloon (the golden balloon, the disco ball) is due, and whether one is on the board.

On a purple level it rolls every few lines from PlatedRewardTuning.PurpleAtCompletion, each roll's chance the next entry of PlatedRewardTuning.PurpleChances, so each further one is rarer and the list's length caps the level. A first entry of 1 makes the first one sure.

Off a purple level, every PlatedRewardTuning.OrdinaryEveryLevels levels from the sure level on roll once, on their first line, for a single one at PlatedRewardTuning.AtCompletion. The chance eases off the further past the sure level the run is (PlatedRewardTuning.ChanceHalvedAfterLevels), and a hard pity makes the roll sure once PlatedRewardTuning.PityLevels levels in a row have gone without one placed, so the late run sees them on the pity's beat. PlatedRewardTuning.SureLevel needs no roll and brings its one early (PlatedRewardTuning.SureAtCompletion), the player's first sight of the balloon.

A won roll stays due until the balloon is placed: a line with no room for it passes it on.
