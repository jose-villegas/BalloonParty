Detailed Description

A SightRampReaction that dampens a PierceConeSpiral renderer's Climb Speed (_Speed) when the shot's aim isn't sighted on this item.

The material's authored speed is the ceiling (sighted = full speed); off-aim it eases down to _outOfSightFactor of it, so the drill idles slow and spins up to its normal rate as the aim centres — never faster.
