Detailed Description

A meadow mushroom on the board (Meadow Mushrooms): the quad the Grid/Mushroom shader draws the mushroom and its shadow on, and the cap's answer to what happens to it.

A ball bouncing off this mushroom pushes MushroomCapSpring along the way it came in, and a bomb's blast that leaves the cap on sways it the same way. A lance cutting it starts MushroomCutTimeline: the cap falls in two and leaves the stem, until it grows back. A blast that takes the cap off throws it away whole. Each frame the view hands the shader how far the cap leans and swells, and where what came off it is.

A mushroom at rest costs nothing a frame, whole or cut: the view is switched off until something happens to the cap, and switches itself off again once nothing moves. Neither the bounce nor the cut is the view's to decide: the ball bounces off the circle MushroomBoard builds for the model, the rule says when a lance cuts a cap and when it has grown back, and the prefab has no collider.
