Detailed Description

Night-mode TimeOfDaySource.Realtime driver: rotates the ambient light direction continuously on a wall-clock, one full circle per ITimeOfDaySettings.SecondsPerCycle (unscaled time, so it runs through pause and the level-up freeze).

Starts at the authored rest direction and rotates forward from there. Inert unless night mode is on AND the source is Realtime — the level-paced alternative is TimeOfDayCycle (Game/Level); only one is ever live, gated by the source. Owns policy only; the ambient state/push lives on TimeOfDayService.

The angle is per-scope: it resets to the rest direction whenever this scope loads (game or launcher), rather than persisting a single clock across scene loads.
