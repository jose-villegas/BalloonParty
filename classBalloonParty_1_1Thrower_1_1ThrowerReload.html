Detailed Description

The thrower's reload rules: how far the held ball has slid from the pivot to the muzzle, and whether a release fires it.

The slide advances only while a slot under the ball cap is free, so a ball held short of the muzzle is the player's "not yet" cue, and one at the muzzle fires. Its speed depends on what's in the air: near-instant with no ball flying, slow while one is, so firing again before a shot lands costs time. A ball that dies mid-slide speeds up the rest of it. The cap is read live; the two reload times are the run's, cards included, taken at each Restart so a pick applies from the next shot.
