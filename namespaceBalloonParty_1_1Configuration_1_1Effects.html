enum BalloonParty.Configuration.Effects.TimeOfDaySource
package

What drives the night-mode time-of-day angle.

Enumerator
LevelSweep 

The light steps forward one notch per level and sweeps during the level-up transition (the level-paced cycle).

Realtime 

The light rotates continuously on a wall-clock, one full circle per ITimeOfDaySettings.SecondsPerCycle — independent of levels.
