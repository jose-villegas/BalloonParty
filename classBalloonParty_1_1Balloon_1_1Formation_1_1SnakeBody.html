Detailed Description

Where the tough snake's free toughs fall in: a line of slots on from its tail, away from the body, that they take in one batch.

A slot on the line may be empty, hold a free tough (landed or still gliding in), or hold a balloon the snake can walk through (SnakeWalk.CanPassThrough), which moves out to a slot a gathered tough leaves.
