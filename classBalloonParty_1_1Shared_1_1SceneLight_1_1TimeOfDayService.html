Detailed Description

Owns the LIVE ambient scene light and is the single writer of its shader globals (_SceneLightDir/_SceneLightColor/_SceneLightIntensity).

A passive holder: it publishes whichever toward-light direction it's given — the authored rest value, or the swept target that the Game-layer TimeOfDayCycle pushes via SetDirection while night mode is on — deriving the colour/intensity and exposing them both as globals and through ISceneLightRuntime for the CPU-side consumers (sky tint, GI) that can't read the globals. Replaces the ambient push formerly bolted onto SceneLightFieldService, which now owns only the local light field.
