Detailed Description

A pen stroke through a ribbon's line, bent by the same wave as the ribbon and its writing, so the strike rides the folds with the letters.

Lives under the ribbon's text, in the text's space (measured from the ribbon's centre); RibbonPiece places it and keeps it moving.
