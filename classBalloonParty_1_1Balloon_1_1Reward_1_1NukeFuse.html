Detailed Description

The armed nuke's fuse (PLAN-NukeBalloon).

The hit that arms a nuke (any ActorHitMessage on a NukeBalloonModel that is now NukeBalloonModel.Armed) starts it. It first waits INukeFuseSettings.HeartSeconds, while the counter's heart flashes, then counts INukeFuseSettings.Seconds down, all on scaled time, so a pause holds it, and shows the whole seconds left on the nuke's counter, with a NukeCountdownMessage for each. Meanwhile it pulls the balloons around the nuke in towards it, harder as the count runs down, and the view implodes the disturbance field at it. At zero INukeDetonator sets it off. A level change, a game over, a run reset or the nuke leaving the board stops the fuse with no blast.
