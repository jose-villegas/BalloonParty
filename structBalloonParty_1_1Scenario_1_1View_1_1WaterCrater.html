Detailed Description

The crater a blast (a bomb, the nuke) leaves in the water, and the shape it is dug in.

A blast moves water instead of pressing on it: over its seconds it carries the water under it outward along the slope of a potential (Potential), so what leaves the middle lands in a ring round it and no water appears or is lost. The potential is chosen for the crater it digs (Profile): flat over its inner half, a wall, and past the edge a rim a third as tall as the crater is deep. Meanwhile the water answers as it always does: the rim runs off as a wave and the crater floods back in, which the dig holds off while it lasts (DugBy). A blast with a Line (a stretch of a piercing shot's flight) digs a furrow instead: the same potential runs the line from end to end at a steady pace, digging as it goes. One stretch after another makes one unbroken furrow, as deep down its middle however the path is cut into stretches, which opens in the order the shot flew it and closes behind in the same order, its water thrown out to both sides. WaterRipple.shader's step evaluates the same potential; WaterRippleBuffer feeds it.

◆ DugBy()

float BalloonParty.Scenario.View.WaterCrater.DugBy ( float age)
package

The share of the dig done age seconds in, 0 to 1.

A crater's is fastest at the start, as a blast is, and eases off to nothing: the crater opens at once, and the rest of the dig holds it open against the water flooding back for as long as it lasts. A furrow's is steady, as its share is also how far along its line the dig has come (OffsetAt).

The crater a reported blast digs: as wide as the blast times radiusScale , as deep as its strength times depthPerStrength rest depths, dug over the blast's seconds (never less than stepSeconds , one step of the simulation), along its line if it has one.

A furrow's dig is also given the time to run its line no faster than MaxSpreadsAStep: a lance outruns the simulation, and its narrow furrow follows a few hundredths of a second behind it rather than break into holes.

◆ PotentialScale()

float BalloonParty.Scenario.View.WaterCrater.PotentialScale ( float texelsPerUnit)
package

What the potential is scaled by over the whole dig, on a grid of texelsPerUnit .

A crater's, so its middle ends Depth down: the potential's Laplacian at the middle is 4/3 over its spread squared. A furrow's, so the middle of its width ends that far down all along it: in proportion to its length, so a path digs the same however it is cut into stretches, and a shot that stalls digs nothing.

The height the dig leaves at u (as in Potential) per unit of depth: -1 at the middle, 0 at the edge, positive in the rim.

Joins two furrows into one when next carries straight on from where first ends, as wide and as deep: the stretches a shot flew in one frame's fixed steps.

One furrow of the whole length, run in the time of both. A frame that runs many steps at once (a hitch) then asks for one dig, not one a step, however far the shot flew in it. False, and nothing joined, for craters, a turn (a bounce) or a gap.
