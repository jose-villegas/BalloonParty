Detailed Description

Decides when the reward balloons come (PLAN-RewardBalloons).

At most one of each on the board, and only a type the catalogue has an entry for.

The golden balloon, on a purple level: the first from the line that finds the level IRewardBalloonSettings.GoldenAtCompletion done. Its pop rolls once for another (IRewardBalloonSettings.GoldenRepeatChances, up to three a level), which comes a few lines later; a board clear or a level change rolls nothing.

The heart balloon: on a purple level, the schedule's steps (IRewardBalloonSettings.PurpleHearts), each rolled once as the level's completion reaches it, at full hearts too. Elsewhere, a roll on every line while a heart is missing, likelier the more are, after a cooldown since the last.

A golden balloon's pop rolls once for the level's next one.

Only a pop rolls: the leave callback also fires for a board clear and a level change. The pop's leave has already run when its hit arrives (HitPipeline routes the hit before publishing it), so the guard is the placement, not whether one is on the board. The spawn cheat can place a second golden beside the first; either one's pop then rolls for the newer.
