Detailed Description

Inspector-authored IMushroomSettings.

The field initialisers are the defaults the feature was planned with, so a fresh instance plays the rules as settled.

The layouts a level's walls are dealt from were baked with these numbers (MushroomWallCatalogue). Retuning the kick's cone or offsets, the radius, the chain's rules or the placement means baking the catalogue again (Tools/BalloonParty/Bake Mushroom Wall Catalogue): until it is, the layouts measure something else than they were kept for, and MushroomAimGridTests fails on the numbers that no longer match.
