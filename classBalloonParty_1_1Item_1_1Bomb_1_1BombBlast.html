Detailed Description

Config-free Bomb blast-selection core (Shot Solver Accuracy Phase C2) — runs identically over GridEffectBoard (live) and ShotSimEffectBoard (solver), mirroring BombItemHandler.BlastBalloons/RainbowBlast's geometry exactly, minus the shockwave/ disturbance-field/light presentation (unmodeled — Shot Solver Accuracy Risk R5) and Physics2D itself (the board's own IEffectBoard.Occupants already excludes the popped host and any disabled-collider view — see GridEffectBoard.Rebuild).

Normal (non-rainbow host) blast: every occupant whose CENTRE lies within BombBlastParams.Radius plus its own radius takes a hit — mirrors Physics2D.OverlapCircle's circle-vs-circle overlap test exactly.

A hex neighbour of hostSlot among THOSE hits is a guaranteed kill (EffectHitKind.PiercingDamage) — a hex neighbour OUTSIDE the overlap radius gets no hit at all, exactly like live (the neighbour check there only ever runs over OverlapCircle's own result set, never a separate query). Rainbow host: centre-distance ALONE (no added occupant radius — mirrors RainbowBlast's plain sqrMagnitude classification, unlike the physics-driven normal path) within BombBlastParams.Radius is a guaranteed kill; within the wider BombBlastParams.RainbowConversionRange ring, a paintable occupant recolors instead of dying.
