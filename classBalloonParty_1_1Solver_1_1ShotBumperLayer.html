Detailed Description

The meadow's mushrooms for the shot solver (Meadow Mushrooms): the bumper circles as they stood when the board was gathered, and the same bounce rule, chain rules and reward the live flight has.

A peer of ShotBoardDynamics and ShotItemLayer: ShotSimulator.Simulate takes it nullable, and with none the flight is the one it was before the meadow had mushrooms.

It holds no geometry of its own. ShotSimulator sweeps the circles with BumperSweep, counts with MushroomChainRule and turns the ball with Rule, in the order ProjectileMotionResolver does, so the two cannot bounce a ball differently. The circles are copied at gather: the field's own list is refilled when the board changes, and a sweep of thousands of flights has to see one board.

Cut caps. The field lists whole caps only, so a cap a lance cut before the gather is not here, as it is not there for the game. A simulated lance cuts caps too (Cut): they leave Circles for the rest of that flight, and the next flight starts from the board as gathered. A bomb the simulated flight sets off blows caps off the same way (BlowOff). Nothing grows back inside a simulated flight: the game's caps return on the board's beat, seconds later, which a flight does not model.

◆ Cut()

The simulated lance cut the cap at index of Circles: it is gone from them for the rest of the flight, as IBumperField.Cut takes it from the game's.

False, with nothing changed, for an index that is no cap: the caller must then not count a cut, nor look for that cap again.
