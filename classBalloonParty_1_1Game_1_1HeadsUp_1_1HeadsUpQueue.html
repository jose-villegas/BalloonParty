Detailed Description

Which heads-ups get a place on screen.

A request is checked against its style's argument range, then rewrites a showing line of its style and merge key, then waits out its style's cooldown, then takes a free slot, or evicts the oldest of the lowest priority showing if that is lower than its own, or equal and shown at an earlier moment, or is dropped. A rewrite makes its line the newest. Held styles (the cannon's ready) have a lane of their own outside the cap. Plain C#: the time is passed in.
