Detailed Description

When balloon lines arrive: one line per interval of scaled game time, the interval shrinking each level, quickly until it reaches _minInterval, then at a gentler late rate down to a floor.

The late rate is what keeps deep levels getting harder once the level generator has bought every threat. A formula rather than a per-range field, so it reaches every level and survives the move to generated levels (PLAN-FastPaced Phase 4).
