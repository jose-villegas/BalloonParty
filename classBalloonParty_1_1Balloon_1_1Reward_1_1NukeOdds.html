Detailed Description

The nuke balloon's chance on a clock line (PLAN-NukeBalloon): INukeOddsSettings.BaseChance out of trouble, rising to INukeOddsSettings.MaxChance with the urgency.

The urgency is the weighted sum of four signals, each 0 to 1, capped at 1: the board's fill from INukeOddsSettings.FillFrom to INukeOddsSettings.FillFull, the spawn clock's pressure, the hearts' need (0 at full, 1 on the last), and the hearts lost recently over INukeOddsSettings.LossesForFull. Each WaveDamageMessage adds its hearts to the recent losses, and each clock line keeps INukeOddsSettings.LossKeptPerLine of them, so hearts lost one line after another add up and a single loss fades.
