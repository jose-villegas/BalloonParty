Detailed Description

The rings over the balloons item covers stand round (ICoveredHosts).

Each covered balloon is a ring source of the formation covering it, so that formation's outline closes over it: without one the covered balloon is a hole in the middle of a full cover, and a notch in an open one. The covered balloon's own view knows nothing of it. FormationGlowField knows a ring by its source, so a covered balloon keeps one source for as long as the same cover stands round it, and Sync changes nothing, and allocates nothing, while the covers stand as they did. A balloon no longer covered loses its source, and the field dissolves its ring where it was.
