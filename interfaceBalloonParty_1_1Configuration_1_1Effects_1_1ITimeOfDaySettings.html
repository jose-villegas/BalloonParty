Detailed Description

Read-only tuning for the night-mode time-of-day cycle.

The ambient light direction walks the circle as the player climbs: level 1 sits at the authored ISceneLightSettings.LightDirection, each further level advances by DegreesPerLevel, and the level-up transition sweeps between them.

float BalloonParty.Configuration.Effects.ITimeOfDaySettings.DegreesPerLevel

Degrees the toward-light direction advances per level, counter-clockwise (the gradient's t = angle / 360).

A full day spans 360 / this levels; it wraps continuously, so the cycle never reverses.
