Detailed Description

Pure answer to "what share of a line's places are bubbles, this far into a water level?": none during the grace, then a straight climb from IBubbleRiseSettings.StartShare to IBubbleRiseSettings.MaxShare over IBubbleRiseSettings.RampSeconds, then held.

The longer the player stays on the level, the more of every line is bubbles.
