Flies the stretch from from to to against the bumpers and returns where it really ends.

The whole stretch is swept, so no speed carries the ball through a bumper, and the earliest entry wins. At each one the bounce is counted (MushroomChainRule.OnBounce), then the bounce rule is asked with the exact entry point, the ball turns, and what is left of the stretch is flown along the new direction, as Deflect carries a deflecting balloon's remainder. The bumper being left is not tested again while the ball flies that direction. from comes back as where the last straight leg of the stretch began: the last bounce, or where it was.

A ball the mushrooms no longer bounce (MushroomReward.PassThrough: its stretch between two walls has had every bounce it is allowed) is not turned and the bounce rule is not asked: it flies on through them to a wall. A piercing shot is not turned either, and nothing is counted for it: it cuts the caps on the stretch (CutCaps) and the stretch ends where it was going to. A ball found inside a bumper (set down there by the tail of a deflect) bounces at the point its line came in, and what it has flown since is carried along the new direction too.

The bounce that arms is the end of the step. When the chain rule answers MushroomReward.Arm the ball turns and stops on the mushroom: what was left of the stretch is not flown, this step or later. The ball is armed by whoever hears the step's bounces, after the step, so nothing the step did past that bounce could be flown as the ball it had just become: a wall there would be settled as an ordinary ball's (no pierce to discharge, a shield from a ball that might die there and be armed dead). Ending the step on the mushroom means the next one flies from the bounce as the lance, at the lance's speed, and no wall shares a step with the arming. It costs the ball less than one step's length of travel, once per arming. The cap that armed the ball is the one it is leaving, which no sweep tests while it flies that way, so it is not cut by its own lance.

Reflects the projectile off a deflecting balloon at the ANALYTIC contact point, returning that point for the caller to snap the shot onto.

The trigger fires at a discrete fixed step, so the reported position sits up to a step length inside the balloon — a radial normal there is displaced by up to ~30° from the true tangency, turning aim→outcome into a step-phase staircase. Backtracking the travel ray to the exact circle entry keeps deflections the clean billiard the shot-geometry puzzle work relies on (see plan_shot_geometry).

Advances one fixed step, mutating direction/shield count on a wall bounce.

On a board with bumpers (the meadow's mushrooms) the step's stretch is flown against them before the walls: the ball bounces off each one it meets, spending no shield, and the step reports how many (ProjectileStep.BumperBounces, each in StepBumperBounces). The bounce that arms the ball (MushroomReward.Arm) ends the step where it happened: see BounceOffBumpers. A piercing ball bounces off none: it flies on unturned and cuts every whole cap its stretch enters (StepCapCuts). On a board without bumpers the step is the walls alone, as it always was.

Backtracks the travel ray from the (penetrated) trigger position to its entry into the contact circle — smallest positive t with |position − t·direction − center| = radius — and returns the unit normal there.

False when the ray's line never crosses the circle or the entry lies ahead of the position (both only reachable through degenerate trigger states).

IReadOnlyList<BumperBounce> BalloonParty.Projectile.Controller.ProjectileMotionResolver.StepBumperBounces
getpackage

The bumpers the ball bounced off during the last Step, in order.

Read them straight after the step that reported them (ProjectileStep.BumperBounces): the next step, of any ball, starts the list again. Copy them before publishing anything about them: a subscriber that stepped a ball would refill the list under the loop reading it.
