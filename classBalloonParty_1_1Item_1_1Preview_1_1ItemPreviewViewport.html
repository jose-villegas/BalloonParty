Detailed Description

Single shared owner of "what is visible" for the range-preview figures — the camera-derived world rect, expanded by a margin derived from IItemPreviewConfig.DashLength and IItemPreviewConfig.DashSpacing.

Both ItemPreviewTicker (culling pens) and LaserRangePreview (clipping beam lines) read this one instance, so the two agree on one notion of "visible" instead of drifting apart.

Refresh is idempotent per frame (guarded on Time.frameCount), so both ItemRangePreviewController and ItemPreviewTicker can call it before reading without either depending on tickable ordering — the whole reason this lives as a shared object rather than inside one of them.
