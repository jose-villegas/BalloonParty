Detailed Description

The clearing mask (Effects Layer Clearing): a small two-channel target, bound globally as _EffectsClearMask, that says how cleared each point of the screen is (red) and how wide the dome there is (green, its radius in view heights).

Once a frame one blit decays last frame's mask and stamps each ball in flight on it. A stamp peaks on the ball's path, so as it decays the cleared area shrinks inward. Goes idle (no blit, mask black) once nothing has stamped for a full linger time.
