◆ MaxSpeed

float BalloonParty.Configuration.Effects.SpeckMotionSettings.MaxSpeed
get

Hard per-speck speed cap (world units/sec) applied after drag each step, so a steep gradient off a fresh disturbance stamp can't fling a speck into an unbounded streak.

A missing/0 serialized value falls back to a built-in default, so the clamp is always active.
