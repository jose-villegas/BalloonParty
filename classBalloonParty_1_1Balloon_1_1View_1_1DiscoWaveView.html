Detailed Description

Draws the disco ball's wave: a quad on the BalloonParty/Sprite/DiscoWave material, centred on the pop and sized to the disc each frame, so the shader only runs over the ground the disc covers.

The shader draws the rainbow edge and the wash from the radius and progress pushed here.
