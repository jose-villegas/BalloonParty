Detailed Description

Laser's telegraph: two crossing lines — the beam's axes, clipped to what's actually visible.

LaserCross casts four arms along the rotated ±right/±up axes, each a swept circle of CircleCastRadius travelling RaycastDistance. An occupant is caught when the segment-to-centre distance is within the cast radius PLUS its own radius, and at 0.065 the cast radius is negligible next to a balloon's — the catchment is dominated by the balloon radius either way, so an axis line represents the beam at least as truthfully as the 0.13-wide rectangle it replaced, without implying a precision the number doesn't carry. Opposite arms still share one corridor, so the four collapse into two lines rather than four.

Each half-arm is clipped to ItemPreviewContext.Viewport (the same shared bounds ItemPreviewTicker culls pens against) rather than drawn the full RaycastDistance: the real cast still runs the full distance — nothing is under-promised — but nobody can see past the edge of the screen, so drawing the unclipped 40-unit corridor would mostly be off-screen and would starve the shared pen budget for no visual gain. When the viewport is unavailable (no camera yet, or it isn't orthographic) this falls back to WallLimits: falling back to the raw RaycastDistance instead would spike the derived dash count straight into MaxPens, which is the exact problem the clipping exists to prevent.
