◆ ShieldLossVelocityCurve

AnimationCurve BalloonParty.Configuration.Effects.IDisturbanceFieldSettings.ShieldLossVelocityCurve
get

Scales the projectile's shield-loss wall-impact stamp (radius + strength) by its velocity, normalized 0 (base/non-cruising speed) → 1 (the cruise ramp's max) — see BalloonParty.Shared.Disturbance.DisturbanceFieldService.StampShieldLoss.

Author y≈0 at t=0 so a base-speed hit reproduces the un-scaled ProjectileImpact profile.
