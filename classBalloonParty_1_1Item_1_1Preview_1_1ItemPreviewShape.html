Detailed Description

The figure one item's range draws, as a set of polylines.

Reused across rebuilds (cleared, never reallocated), so an aim held on an item costs no garbage however often it refreshes.

Points live in one flat list and strokes index into it, rather than a list-of-lists: the ticker walks strokes every LateTick, and a jagged structure would chase a pointer per stroke for no gain — every figure here is a handful of short polylines.
