Detailed Description

Turns an unbreakable's red lights blue while it holds a formation: the core glow sprites, the inner rim, and the exhaust trail.

A glitter swirl fades in behind it at the same time. The change starts once the member has arrived in its slot, and eases back to red when the formation breaks.
