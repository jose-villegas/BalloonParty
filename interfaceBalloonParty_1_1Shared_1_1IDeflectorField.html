Detailed Description

The deflectors the aim telegraph must bounce off, in the geometry the projectile will actually meet.

Positions come from the views, not from slot coordinates: the real deflection reflects off where the balloon is drawn (see BalloonController.Deflect), and balloons drift between slots while the board settles — which is exactly when a player is lining a shot up.
