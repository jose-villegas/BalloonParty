Detailed Description

How a mushroom's cap answers a hit (MushroomCapSpring): how far it leans, how it wobbles back, and how hard it swells when the bounce pays the lance.

Authored on the mushroom settings (IMushroomSettings.Lean).

The reach of a lean and of a swell are authored as the furthest the spring gets, not as the push that sends it there, so changing how fast or how long the cap wobbles leaves how far it goes alone.
