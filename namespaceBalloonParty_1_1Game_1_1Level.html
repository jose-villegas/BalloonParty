enum BalloonParty.Game.Level.LevelUpPhase
package

The level-up ceremony as one explicit state, owned by LevelController.

Replaces the scattered guard flags (pending / transitioning / nav / pause) that had to be kept in sync: a level-up is only detected in Playing, and every out-of-phase input (a second detection, a straggler trail, a duplicate dismissal) is rejected because no transition exists for it. Cycles Playing → Completing → Pending → Transitioning → Playing.

Enumerator
Playing 

Normal play: scoring is open and a completed colour set can trigger a level-up.

Pending 

Detected and published; the popup ceremony is up.

The level and progress have not advanced yet — that waits for dismissal.

Transitioning 

Dismissed: the level has advanced and the Ascent is running.

Ends when the Ascent reports completion.

Completing 

The tipping claim landed — holding for the shot's flight to end before presenting the ceremony.

Progress keeps confirming (bars fill), spawns/reload are held.
