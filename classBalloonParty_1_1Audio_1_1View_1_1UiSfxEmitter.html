Detailed Description

Drop-in component that plays a configurable GameSoundId when triggered.

Automatically binds to sibling Button onClick if present; can also be wired manually to UnityEvents, Animator events, or called from code. Receives ISoundPlayer via VContainer's build callback injection (see GameScopeRegistration.RegisterAudio).

Emitters present in the scene at App-scope build time are auto-injected by FindObjectsByType. Dynamically spawned emitters must be manually injected via resolver.Inject(emitter), otherwise the sound player will be null and Play will silently no-op.
