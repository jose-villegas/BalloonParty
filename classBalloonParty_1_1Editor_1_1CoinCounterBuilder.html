Detailed Description

Builds the coin counter (UI/Coins/README.md) and puts it in the game canvas, bottom left.

Its backing and number are copies of the shield counter's, so the HUD counters match; the icon is the coin. Running it again rebuilds the counter the same way and replaces the one in the canvas.
