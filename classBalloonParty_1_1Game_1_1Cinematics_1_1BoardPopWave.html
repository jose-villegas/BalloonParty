Detailed Description

Detaches every balloon into the transition's outgoing group so they ride the fake-camera travel out, then pops them in a slow-mo wave along anti-diagonals from the far corners inward — the "gone" beat of the game-over restart (vs.

the level-up's float-away "survived"). Detaching frees the grid so the incoming scenery fills fully; the balloons stay registered so the wave can still pop them. Scoreless (no ActorHitMessage); tuned by the LevelAscend pop settings.
