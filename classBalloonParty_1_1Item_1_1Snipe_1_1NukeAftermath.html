Detailed Description

The stretch after a nuke goes off (NukeDetonatedMessage) in which the Sharpshooter card's Snipe roll is a little likelier: the board refills fast after the blast's breather, and a lance is the follow-up that keeps the room the nuke bought.

A new run starts with none.
