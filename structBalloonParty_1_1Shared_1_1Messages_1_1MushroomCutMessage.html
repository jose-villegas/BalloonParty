Detailed Description

A meadow mushroom lost its cap (Meadow Mushrooms): a lance cut it, or a blast blew it off.

Published once for each cap, when it goes. The cap has already left the board's bumpers when this is heard, and until MushroomRegrownMessage names the same slot the mushroom is a bare stem and nothing bounces off it.

A lance's cut (MushroomCutCause.Lance). Point and Direction give the line the cap splits along: the mushroom's view draws the two pieces either side of it, so a lance that clips the cap's edge takes a sliver off and one through its middle halves it.

A blast (MushroomCutCause.Blast). Nothing split the cap, so there is no line: Point is the cap's own centre and Direction is the way the cap is blown, the unit direction from the blast's centre to the cap. A view shows the cap thrown off whole that way. A bomb blows off the caps of the mushrooms next to it, the ones in the six slots round its own, and sways the others its blast reaches (MushroomSwayedMessage); a nuke blows off each cap as its blast reaches it. Several caps blown by one bomb arrive as several messages in the same frame, each with its own direction.

A lance's cut banks a charge (Charges). Every cap a flight cuts saves that shot one lance charge, whatever armed the lance: when its pierce would end at a wall, a charge is spent and it stays a lance. The charge is already banked when this is heard. Charges counts them for the flight, 1 for the first cap it cuts, so whoever shows or sounds the bank can step with it. A blast banks nothing.

Whatever the cause, the cap grows back the same way: on the board's beat, the fifth after it went, a beat later for each beat a ball is in its way.

◆ Charges

readonly int BalloonParty.Shared.Messages.MushroomCutMessage.Charges

How many lance charges the flight that cut this cap has banked by cutting caps, this one included: 1 for the first cap a flight cuts, 2 for its second, and so on, so above 0 says this cut banked a charge and the number says which.

It counts what the flight's cuts earned, not what the shot still holds: a charge spent at a wall is not taken off it, and a Snipe picked up mid-pierce is not in it. 0 when nothing was banked: a blast, or a cut no flight made (one staged by hand).

◆ Direction

readonly Vector2 BalloonParty.Shared.Messages.MushroomCutMessage.Direction

A unit direction in world space.

For a lance, its direction of travel. For a blast, the way the cap is blown: from the blast's centre to the cap's (straight up for a blast centred on the cap).

◆ Point

readonly Vector2 BalloonParty.Shared.Messages.MushroomCutMessage.Point

For a lance: a point inside the cap that the split passes through, in world units; with Direction it gives the line the cap splits along.

It is the point of the lance's line nearest the cap's centre (the line carried on both ways, so it can lie behind a lance that began its step inside the circle), drawn in toward the centre when the line passes outside the cap and only the ball's edge clipped it. It is not always a point the ball's centre flew through. For a blast: the cap's centre, in world units.
