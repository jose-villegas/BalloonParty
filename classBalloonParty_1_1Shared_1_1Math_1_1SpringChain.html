Detailed Description

A line of points joined by springs between two pinned ends, simulated the way the launch screen's spring line is (UI/ClothRibbon/RibbonCloth): position-based, at fixed steps, each step letting the points carry on and fall, then a few passes pulling every spring back toward its rest length.

Its springs give, so it sags when its ends close in, stretches when they part, and swings as they move.

reach below 1 leaves the far end loose: the chain is that share of its length, grown out of from , and its loose end reaches toward to , harder the further it has grown. At 1 the far end is tied on at to .
