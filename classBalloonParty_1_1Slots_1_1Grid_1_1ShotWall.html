Detailed Description

Whether the balloons no ordinary shot gets through (unbreakables, deflectors) link one side of the board to the other along hex neighbours.

Such a chain shuts every ordinary shot out of the board above it, and a standing formation also holds back the balloons below, so whatever moves an unbreakable must not close one. Load the board, try a move with Set, ask Spans, then undo the move.
