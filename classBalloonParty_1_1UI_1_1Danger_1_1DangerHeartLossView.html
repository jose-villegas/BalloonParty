Detailed Description

Displays how many hearts the next wave will cost.

Fades in when danger is non-zero, speeds up the shine sweep proportionally, and shows "-{N}" via rolling text.

The shine is the BalloonParty/Sprite/ShinyDefault material on _shineImage, driven through its _ShineSpeed property. Tick that material's Shine Uses Unscaled Time if the sweep should keep moving while a popup holds Time.timeScale at 0.
