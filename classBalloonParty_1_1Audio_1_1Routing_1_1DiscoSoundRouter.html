Detailed Description

The disco ball's wave in sound (PLAN-DiscoBalloon).

A glissando climbs two octaves of the board's key over the wave's time, and each balloon the wave paints ticks at the degree its distance from the pop reaches. Both play their entries as ScaleWalkUp, with the degree passed as the melodic step, so every note lands in the key. The tick's cooldown in the sound bank is what keeps a full board from stacking a clip per balloon.
