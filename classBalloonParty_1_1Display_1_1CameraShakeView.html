Detailed Description

Camera-shake View: owns the shake/recoil animation and the additive LateUpdate compositor on the (persistent) camera.

Pure presentation — CameraShakeController decides WHEN to shake (message-driven, cinematic-gated). Split out of the former CameraShakeService so the component can ride the persistent camera while its gameplay triggers stay in the run scope.
