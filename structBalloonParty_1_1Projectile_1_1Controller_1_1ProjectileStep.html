Detailed Description

Result of one fixed-step advance by ProjectileMotionResolver.

On a bounce, Position is the mirror-reflected continuation (the exact billiard point, up to a step INSIDE the field) while WallContact is the wall-projected point the bounce visually happened at — VFX and shield-loss feedback belong there.
