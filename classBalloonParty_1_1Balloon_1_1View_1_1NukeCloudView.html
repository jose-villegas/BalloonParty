Detailed Description

The nuke's mushroom cloud (PLAN-NukeBalloon) on the BalloonParty/Sprite/NukeCloud material, seen from above as the board is: one square quad centred where the nuke went off, its shader drawing the cap, its shadow and the dust ring from the progress pushed here (0 at the blast, 1 when it has eroded away) over INukeBlastSettings.CloudSeconds.

It runs on scaled time, so a pause holds it, and outlives the blast that started it.
