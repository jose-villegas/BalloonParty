Detailed Description

The sway a bomb's blast gives the mushrooms it reaches without taking their caps off (Meadow Mushrooms).

Plain arithmetic, so the reach and the strength can be held in tests.

How far from a bomb's centre a mushroom's centre may be and still sway: the kill radius times the sway's share, and the cap's own radius, since the blast reaches a cap as it reaches a balloon, when the two circles overlap.

0 when the share turns the sway off.
