Detailed Description

Pays a reward balloon's pop.

The golden balloon gives a free card, or a heart back when every card is at its limit. Only a pop pays: the leave callback also fires for a board clear and a level change, and the level's closing pop wave publishes no hit at all. A pierce can land after the level's last claim; IFreeCardGrant holds that card back while the level-up hand is on screen, and gives the heart then if the pick took the last card. The remover cheat's forced pop pays too.
