Detailed Description

A SightRampReaction that jitters a transform while the shot's aim is sighted on this item — the amplitude scales with centrality, so it trembles harder as the aim centres and settles to rest off-aim.

Positional shake around the transform's authored local position, leaving rotation free for a sibling ProjectileFacingRotator.
