Detailed Description

The launch screen's Continue: a white balloon drifting about the screen, shown only while a run is saved, carrying a ribbon that says so on a spring line.

It keeps clear of Play (BalloonWander), with room below for what hangs from it; the line and ribbon are simulated (RibbonCloth) and pushed off Play's balloon if they swing into it. Wire Continue to the balloon's ScaleDownButton On Scale Complete, as Play is. LaunchContinueController decides when it shows and what a press does. Sits stretched over the launch canvas, centred on it, so its own space is the canvas's; the line, ribbon and writing sit at its centre.
