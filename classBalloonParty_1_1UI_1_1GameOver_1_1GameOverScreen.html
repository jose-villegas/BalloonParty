Detailed Description

The loss screen: the level reached and its points, the best run, and the ways on: retry the level (the primary action), retry with a bonus card, or start over.

Visibility is driven by a CanvasGroup so the GameObject can stay active and keep its subscriptions alive (never toggle with SetActive). The layout is built by Editor/GameOverLayoutBuilder.
