Detailed Description

Which rings the formation glow draws (FormationGlow shader), how brightly, and how far each has dissolved.

A ring is connected while its member is in a formation with a fellow member within IFormationGlowSettings.FadeDistance: its brightness follows how close the nearest fellow is, full at IFormationGlowSettings.FullDistance. Disconnected for any reason (out of formation, left alone, popped, cleared with the board), the ring stays where its member was last and dissolves over IFormationGlowSettings.DissolveSeconds at the brightness it had, instead of vanishing.
