Detailed Description

Which formation members light the formation glow, and how brightly (FormationGlow shader).

A member's weight is how far into its formation it is times how close its nearest fellow is: full at IFormationGlowSettings.FullDistance, none past IFormationGlowSettings.FadeDistance.
