Detailed Description

Holds the board while a card reveal plays over play (a retry's bonus card, or a reveal that runs on into play): the reveal takes the screen and the player can't aim, so lines landing and balls flying meanwhile would be time taken from them.

Pauses PauseSource.CardReveal for as long as one plays in NavigationState.Game; a level-up's reveal during its ceremony and ascent needs no hold.
