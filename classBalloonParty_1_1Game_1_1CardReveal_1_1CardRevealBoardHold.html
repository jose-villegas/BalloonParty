Detailed Description

Holds the board while a card reveal plays over play (a retry's bonus card, or a reveal that runs on into play): the balloon takes the screen and the player can't aim, so lines landing and balls flying meanwhile would be time taken from them.

Pauses PauseSource.CardReveal in NavigationState.Game until the reveal's card is out (ICardReveals.Shown), then hands play back with time eased up from a standstill over ICardRevealSettings.BoardReturnSeconds of real time, while the ribbons still fly. A level-up's reveal during its ceremony and ascent needs no hold.
