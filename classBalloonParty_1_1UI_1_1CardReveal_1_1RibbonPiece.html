Detailed Description

One piece of ribbon with a line of the card written on it, flown off the popped balloon by CardRevealView.

Before the pop it rests rolled up inside the balloon; once released it bursts out to its spot, drifts and sways there while its wave runs and the text rides the wave, and at the end flies off and fades. The ribbon sizes itself to its text.
