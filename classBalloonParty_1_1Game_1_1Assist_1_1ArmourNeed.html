Detailed Description

Need from armour left standing: each tough (or tougher) or unbreakable balloon on the board counts from 0 when it lands to 1 once it has stood IAssistSettings.ArmourSecondsForFull, and IAssistSettings.ArmourCountForFull of them at full age are full armour.

The need is that share times IAssistSettings.ArmourNeedWeight, read off the board when a roll asks.
