Detailed Description

A launch screen balloon drifting about the screen, carrying a ribbon that says what it does on a spring line (BalloonDrift).

Play and Continue each have one. Alone, a balloon has the whole screen; while both show they share it one above the other (BalloonReach), a gap between everything of the upper one and everything of the lower, so neither balloon nor its ribbon can cover the other's ribbon wherever the two drift and however their ribbons swing. Sits stretched over the launch canvas, centred on it, so its own space is the canvas's and the two balloons share it; the line, ribbon and writing sit at its centre. The balloon it moves is a child or a sibling: Play's is the Play button itself, where the launch canvas has it.
