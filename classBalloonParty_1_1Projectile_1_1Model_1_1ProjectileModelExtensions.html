static void BalloonParty.Projectile.Model.ProjectileModelExtensions.ArmPierce ( this IWriteableProjectileModel model)
static

Arms piercing (idempotently) and starts the acceleration into the tap count's new target, anchored at the speed the shot is actually travelling right now — so arming, and every armed tap after it, is continuous rather than a snap or a dip.

Both rules that earn a tap (the resolver's cruise bounce and the view's sweep) come through ProjectileTapResolver.TryGrantTap.

Starts a tap's freeze-then-pickup beat — the same transition as the arm ramp, anchored at a standstill instead.

That anchor IS the beat: the shot drops and winds back up, which is how earning a tap reads.

◆ SpendPierce()

static bool BalloonParty.Projectile.Model.ProjectileModelExtensions.SpendPierce ( this IWriteableProjectileModel model)
static

Spends the pierce at a discharge.

A banked Snipe charge re-arms the lance in the same breath: the cruise that fed the old pierce still ends (a re-armed lance starts from base speed, so the ramps never compound), but IsPiercing never dips — a dip would release the level-up hold keyed off it (LevelController) mid-flight. Returns true when a charge kept the shot armed; the charge itself is consumed by SnipeItemHandler on the discharge message, which owns the grant it re-applies.
