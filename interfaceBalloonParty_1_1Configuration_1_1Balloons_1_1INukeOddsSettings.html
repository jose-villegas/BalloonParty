Detailed Description

When the nuke balloon comes (PLAN-NukeBalloon): a tiny chance on each clock line, raised toward MaxChance by the trouble the player is in.

The trouble is the weighted sum of four signals, each 0 to 1, capped at 1: the board's fill, the spawn clock's pressure, low hearts, and hearts lost in quick succession.
