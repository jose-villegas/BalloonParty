Detailed Description

The bounce with a forgiving kick: a ball leaving a mushroom close enough to the line of another one is bent onto it and landed where its next bounce carries the chain on, so a chain needs a good first hit and not a perfect flight.

The geometry is BumperKick; this reads the mushroom settings for it on every call, so a retune takes hold at once.

in MushroomChainState chain )

The unit direction the ball leaves contact along.

bumpers is every bumper on the board, the one hit included, in any order. chain is the flight's chain after this bounce was counted. The same arguments always give the same answer, and nothing is allocated.

What every caller (the projectile, the shot solver, the aim line) owes the rule:

  • MushroomChainRule.OnBounce first, then this, and not this at all when the answer was MushroomReward.PassThrough: the ball flies on unturned.
  • The contact point is the exact point where the ball's centre enters the bumper's circle, not where a step happened to end.
  • After the bounce, do not test the bumper just left again on the same step. The ball starts on its circle, and a line leaving close to the tangent would be read as entering it again.

Implements BalloonParty.Shared.IBumperBounceRule.
