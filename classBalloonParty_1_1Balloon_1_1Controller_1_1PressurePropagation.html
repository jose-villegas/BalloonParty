Detailed Description

Directed, weighted pressure search: a board-up shove enters at a blocked column's lowest blocker and propagates through occupied movables — bending toward each shoved neighbour — until an actor can vacate into an empty slot.

Heavier movers cost more per hop, so lighter chains are explored (and chosen) first. If the directed pass exhausts without a mover, a single undirected retry guarantees any reachable pocket still gets filled.
