◆ ClampInside()

Vector3 BalloonParty.Shared.WallLimits.ClampInside ( Vector3 position)

Clamps a position inside the walls WITHOUT reflecting or reporting a crossing — used to keep a snapped point (e.g.

a deflect contact off a near-wall balloon, whose collider radius can extend past the wall) in-bounds so the next step doesn't read it as a spurious wall bounce.

◆ Reflect()

Vector3 BalloonParty.Shared.WallLimits.Reflect ( Vector3 position,
out Vector3 reflectNormal,
out Vector3 wallContact )

Mirror-reflects position back across every wall it crossed (the exact billiard continuation — the overshoot travels on along the reflected heading, so neither distance nor time is lost), reporting the summed inward normal (Vector3.zero if none) and the wall-projected contact point for presentation.

Clamping instead would keep the full parallel advance while zeroing the perpendicular overshoot, displacing every post-bounce path laterally by up to one step — an error the deterministic-shot work cannot absorb.
