Detailed Description

A trial look (IToughSnakeSpringSettings): a spring between each pair of neighbours in the tough snake, tied to their knots.

Each link is a SpringChain drawn as a line, so it sags while the pair stands side by side, stretches as the one ahead steps away and swings as the one behind catches up. A link lives as long as the same pair stands next to each other in the snake. A link shows only once both its balloons are on screen, and it reveals: it grows out of the knot ahead and reaches for the one behind until it ties on, then stays tied. A snake's links reveal head to tail, each once the one ahead has tied on, and one whose balloon behind is further than the attach distance (a tough flying in to the tail) stays hidden, growing and reaching as it closes in. Each line is on its knot's layer, so the scene capture sees it as it sees the balloons: it casts the soft shadow and takes the bounce light. When a level hands its board off (the level-up's ascent, the loss restart's pop wave), the tied links stay with the balloons they tie, riding out with them, and each drops once either balloon is popped or pooled. It's drawn in a checker of two colours (IToughSnakeSpringSettings.Colour and IToughSnakeSpringSettings.CheckerColour) tiled along the line, so it reads as textured.
