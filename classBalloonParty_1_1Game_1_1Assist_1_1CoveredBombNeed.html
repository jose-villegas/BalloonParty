Detailed Description

Need from a bomb the player can't get at: a balloon carrying a bomb counts as covered while at least IAssistSettings.CoveredBombMinCover of the slots next to it hold unbreakables in formation (the item-cover formation closes round it from below).

Its need grows from 0 as the cover closes to 1 once it has stayed covered IAssistSettings.CoveredBombSecondsForFull, times IAssistSettings.CoveredBombNeedWeight; the longest-covered bomb on the board is the one read. A cover that opens, or a bomb that goes, starts from nothing again.
