Detailed Description

The card reveal's stage (ICardReveals): plays each reveal the controller hands it.

A balloon in one of the level's colours rises to its place with two pieces of ribbon rolled up inside it, pops (one of the baked pops, its two rubber layers either side of the ribbons), and the ribbons fly off it, one with the card's title on it and one with what the card does, readable as they drift. On a level-up they fly with the level's ascent and are gone just before the next level settles. The rubber is surface data lit live by BalloonParty/Sprite/LitFlipbook, so it takes the scene's light. Attaches as the stage only when there are pops to play; until then the tooltip announces the cards. Built by Tools/BalloonParty/CardReveal/Build Card Reveal.
