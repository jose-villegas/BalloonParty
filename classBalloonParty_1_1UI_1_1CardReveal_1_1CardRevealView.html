Detailed Description

The card reveal's stage (ICardReveals): plays each reveal the controller hands it.

A silver balloon floats up to its place with two pieces of ribbon rolled up inside it, a pushpin pricks it, and it pops (one of the baked pops, its two rubber layers either side of the ribbons, starting as the needle goes in at the pop's puncture), and the ribbons fly off it, one with the card's title on it and one with what the card does, readable as they drift. On a level-up they fly with the level's ascent and are gone just before the next level settles. The rubber and the pin are surface data lit live by BalloonParty/Sprite/LitFlipbook, so they take the scene's light, and the tear flashes a light into the scene light field, stirs the disturbance field as a board pop does, and flings shreds far out. It sits on its own World Space canvas among the board's sprites (CameraFittedCanvas), so the screen-space light tints it and sees it. Only the playing pop's sheet and the next one's are in memory (PopSheetLoader). Attaches as the stage only when there are pops to play; until then the tooltip announces the cards. Built by Tools/BalloonParty/CardReveal/Build Card Reveal.

How far down the frames move sincePop seconds into the pop, in frame heights: the bake's camera's path.

Nothing until the fall's frame, then speeding up under the settings' gravity, counted in the pop's frames as the bake's knot falls, so the rubber falling with it in the frames matches the motion at any frame rate; plus the recentring, the bake's camera easing further down to keep the gathered rubber in the frame, added back so the knot's path stays the fall's.
