Detailed Description

The caps a lance has cut (IBumperField.Cut) and that have not grown back yet, for whoever keeps the time they grow back by (Meadow Mushrooms).

The field holds the caps and counts what it is told; it keeps no clock of its own.

A cap that leaves the board while cut (the level ended, the run was reset) is dropped from the list without being regrown: the level that follows places whole caps of its own.
