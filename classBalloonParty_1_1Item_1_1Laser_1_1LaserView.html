Detailed Description

Animator-driven laser effect that tints its wired renderers to the colour it receives — the host balloon's colour, which is white for a rainbow host (the palette resolves the wildcard id to white).

Renderers the beam always keeps at a fixed colour simply stay out of the array. A rainbow holder instead lerps the wired renderers through a colour set over the anim (see SetCycleColors).
