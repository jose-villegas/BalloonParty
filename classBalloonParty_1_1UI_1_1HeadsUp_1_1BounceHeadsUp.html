Detailed Description

Writes how a ball's bounces go, where it happens: "+n bounce" where it picks up a shield (n counting its pickups this flight), "-1 bounce" where a wall spends one, and "xn ricochet" where it deflects off balloons n times in a row without touching a wall.

A ricochet that earns the ball a shield writes the "+1 bounce" small under itself, in the pickup's colour, so the reward reads as coming from the ricochet rather than from a pickup nobody saw. Each kind has its own HeadsUpStyle, and the escalating ones grow with their count. Lines still showing push a new one aside (HeadsUpPacking), so a burst doesn't stack into one smudge.

The colours follow the heads-up research in UI/HeadsUp/README.md: blue for a gain and plum for the skill shot (both apart from the balloon colours and readable to colour-blind players), and a quiet graphite for the wall, which happens on nearly every flight: emphasis goes to consequence, not frequency, so only the wall that leaves the ball on its last shield turns vermilion.
