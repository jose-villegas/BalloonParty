Detailed Description

What a level's mushrooms keep to: how many walls of caps, how long a wall is, how many caps in all, how far apart the walls stand, which slots are off limits, and what a layout must measure to be dealt.

Authored on the mushroom settings (IMushroomSettings.Placement). The layouts themselves are baked to these limits (MushroomWallCatalogue); a level picks one.

◆ DefaultMinSpacing

const float BalloonParty.Slots.Actor.MushroomPlacementLimits.DefaultMinSpacing = 1.9f
staticpackage

The spacing a layout keeps when none is authored (see MinSpacing): two and a half slots.

Slots stand on a lattice, so two of them are 0.75, 1.3, 1.5, 1.98, 2.25 or 2.6 apart and so on: 1.9 keeps the nearest caps of two walls 1.98 or more apart.
