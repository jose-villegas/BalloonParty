Detailed Description

Finds where a formation of one shape can stand and which candidate goes to which of its slots.

Reads the grid only; the caller moves the members. A formation slot must be empty or already hold a formation member, and never sits in the bottom IUnbreakableFormationSettings.BottomClearance rows. Among the spots that fit, the planner picks at random among the few with the least total travel.
