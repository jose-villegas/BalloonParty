Detailed Description

Finds where a formation of one shape can stand and which candidate goes to which of its slots.

Reads the grid only; the caller moves the members. A formation slot must be empty or already hold one of the candidates, so a formation never plans over another formation. It never sits in the bottom IUnbreakableFormationSettings.BottomClearance rows (a horizontal line, the bottom IUnbreakableFormationSettings.HorizontalBottomClearance), and never where it would link the board's unbreakables side to side (ShotWall). Among the spots that fit, the planner picks at random among the few with the least total travel.
