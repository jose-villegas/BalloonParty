Detailed Description

The "lone ball" rule for effects that reach beyond one ball: the doomed slow-mo, the board freeze and the held spawn act only while the doomed ball is the only ball in flight.

With others flying, one ball's last breath must not slow or freeze the game under them. Also where a given ball is while it flies, for what is written at the ball.
