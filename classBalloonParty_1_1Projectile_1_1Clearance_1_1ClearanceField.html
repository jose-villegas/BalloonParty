Detailed Description

Fixed-capacity circle slots, one per ball, keyed by the ball's reference.

Each frame the owner calls Track for every ball in flight, then Advance once: a tracked slot follows its ball and eases in, an untracked one stays where its ball was last seen and eases out, and a slot that reaches zero is freed.
