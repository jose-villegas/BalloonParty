Detailed Description

Brings balloon lines in on a timer, whatever the player does: one line per ISpawnClockSettings.IntervalForLevel of scaled game time, after a grace at each level's start.

Scaled, so slow-mo slows it with everything else. It stops outright through pauses, the level-up and game over, and while a lone doomed ball glides its last breath: lines landing in that frozen moment read wrong, and the board must hold still under the glide. Firing while another ball is still flying pulls the next line closer (ISpawnClockSettings.OverlapShotPull), so every extra ball in the air costs room. A sparse board runs the clock faster (ISpawnClockSettings.SparseBoardSpeedup), so clearing it never leaves the player waiting on an empty screen. Balls in the air push it too, by how long each has flown (ISpawnClockSettings.FlightSpeedup): the board refills while long ricochets and rapid fire clear it. A big clear in a short window (ISpawnClockSettings.BreatherClear) earns a breather instead: the clock drops to ISpawnClockSettings.BreatherPace and eases back to its full pace, so the release after a great shot lasts a moment before the tension builds again. A cooldown after each (ISpawnClockSettings.BreatherCooldownSeconds) keeps releases rarer than the clears.
