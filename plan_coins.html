Coins and the Card Shop

Working plan for a coin currency earned deep in a run and spent on the game-over screen for a card that helps the retry.

Status: Planned, not started. Decided with the owner: a card bought with coins applies to the retry at the level reached; coins are a saved wallet; they drop from pops from level 15 on; the counter sits bottom left and appears only once coins exist. Claude draws the art in the existing UI style (§7); the coin is drawn.

1. Why

The playtest bot (Tools/playtest/) shows where each skill starts losing hearts: low skill from about level 4, average from 9, high from 12. Most runs that reach level 15 are in the stretch where hearts go. Coins there reward getting that deep, and the shop turns a loss into a choice: spend what the good runs earned to make the retry easier.

It is a second way to buy what the rewarded ad already buys (a bonus card for one attempt, see Game/Upgrades/README.md), paid with play instead of an ad. It never buys score.

2. The loop

  1. From CoinsFromLevel (15) on, each balloon popped has a CoinDropChance of dropping a coin.
  2. The coin flies to the coin counter, like a score trail, and is added to the wallet when it lands.
  3. The wallet is saved, so coins carry over between runs and app restarts.
  4. On a loss, the game-over screen offers Buy a card next to Retry, priced at CardPrice coins.
  5. Buying one shows a hand of three cards (as at a level-up), the player picks one, the coins are spent, and the retry starts with that card for this attempt only.
  6. The retry opens with the card reveal (PLAN-CardReveal.md): a balloon pops and leaves a slip with the card written on it.

3. Rules

Rule Why
Coins drop only from level CoinsFromLevel (15) on They reward reaching the stretch where hearts go; earlier levels stay about learning the board
Any pop can drop one, an item's blast included "Per pop", as decided; big combos should feel rich
A coin counts only once it lands in the counter Same as score, so a loss mid-flight can't credit coins that never arrived on screen. Lands even through the game-over pan-in
The wallet is saved on game over, on app pause and on quit, not on every coin PlayerPrefs.Save is slow on mobile; RunMeta already saves this way
A bought card lasts one attempt: dropped when the level is won or on any reset The same rule as the ad's bonus card, so spending never stacks power across levels
One bonus card per attempt: buying with coins and the ad bonus exclude each other Keeps the retry's power bounded, and the screen simple
The coin card is picked from three; the ad's card stays random Gives the coins a reason to exist next to the ad: you choose
Never an Extra Ball, never a maxed card The same deck rules as the ad's bonus card (Plans/PLAN-FastPaced.md §8)
The price is the same every time Easy to read. If the bot shows wallets piling up, a price that rises per purchase in a run is the lever (§9)
Coins are progression, not telemetry Kept on the device on purpose. Coins earned and spent per level are also reported as metrics, which leave the device (see Game/Telemetry/README.md)

4. Architecture

Follows the codebase's MVC split: models and controllers are plain C#, only views touch Unity.

Piece Where Responsibility
ICoinSettings Shared/, authored in RunConfig as Coins CoinsFromLevel (15), CoinDropChance (0.05), CardPrice (25), CardsOffered (3)
ICoinWallet / CoinWallet Game/Coins/ IReadOnlyReactiveProperty<int> Balance, Add(int), TrySpend(int). Loads from and saves to PlayerPrefs (key beside RunMeta's)
CoinDropController Game/Coins/ Subscribes ActorHitMessage; on a Pop at level ≥ CoinsFromLevel, rolls the drop (seeded from the level seed, so a retry of the same level drops the same way for the same pops) and publishes CoinDroppedMessage(worldPosition)
CoinTrailService + view Game/Coins/, UI/ Flies each dropped coin to the counter (reusing the score-trail flight), publishes CoinLandedMessage, on which the wallet adds 1. Pooled like score trails
CoinCounterView UI/HUD/ Bottom left of the screen: the coin icon and Balance. Hidden until coins exist (see below); punches on each landing
FailureOffers Game/Run/ (extended) FailureOffer gains CanBuyCard and CardPrice; new TryCardRetryAsync(level, card, ct) spends the coins and stages the retry with that card
IUpgradeDraft / RunUpgrades Game/Upgrades/ (extended) A shop hand (DealShopHand, same draw rules as the bonus card, seeded by level and retries used) and staging a chosen bonus card instead of a random one
CardShopView UI/GameOver/ The hand on the game-over screen, reusing UpgradeCardView slots, with the price, the balance and a disabled state when the wallet is short

When the counter shows. Not before coins exist for this player, so a new player never sees an empty wallet before the game has said what coins are. It appears the first time either holds: the run reaches CoinsFromLevel (fading in with the level's start), or the wallet already holds coins (a returning player sees it from level 1, since they can spend at any loss). After that it stays for the session. It sits bottom left, clear of the cannon and of the heads-up lines, which keep to the board.

Telemetry gains coins_earned and coins_spent per level. The playtest bot reports coins earned per run and per level, so the drop chance and the price can be tuned from runs (§8).

6. Edge cases

  • Retry farming. A retry replays the level reached, so a player can earn coins at level 16, lose, and earn them again on the retry. That is the loop working as meant: it costs play, and the coins buy only one attempt's card. Watch it in the bot's numbers.
  • Coins from the rainbow self-feed. A shot that rainbow-converts balloons and pops them again (see the memory note on the rainbow multiplier) would also roll more coin drops. It is capped by the board, unlike the multiplier, but check the bot's high-skill coins per level for outliers.
  • Losing mid-flight. Coins still in the air when the run ends land during the game-over pan-in, so the screen's balance is complete when it shows.
  • No card can be dealt. When only Extra Ball and maxed cards are left, the shop hides, as the ad's bonus button does.

8. Tuning targets

From the bot's runs: at levels 13 to 15, high skill pops about 180 to 330 balloons a level, and average skill about 150 to 250. With CoinDropChance 0.05 that's about 8 to 16 coins a level, so CardPrice 25 is two or three levels past 15. A player who reaches 17 can afford one card on their next loss. Phase 2 checks this with runs and sets the shipped values.

9. Open questions

  • Can a player buy more than one card for the same retry?
  • Should the price rise with each purchase in a run, to stop a large wallet from buying every retry?
  • Is level 15 right for average skill, which reached 14 in ten minutes in the last runs? Earning from 12 would let average skill take part, and keep it a reward for depth.
  • Should coins also show in the main menu, so the wallet is visible between runs?
