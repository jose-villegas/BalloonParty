Detailed Description

The camera the HUD renders through: an overlay in the main camera's URP stack with post-processing off, so the canvases (the UI and Launch layers) and the score and heart trails flying into them (the Trail layer) draw over the finished, tonemapped frame in their authored colours.

URP applies post-processing per camera in a stack, so the main camera's tonemapper never touches them. It mirrors the main camera's projection every frame, so the canvases keep the world positions the trails fly to, through a cinematic zoom too. CanvasCameraBinder binds the canvases to it.
