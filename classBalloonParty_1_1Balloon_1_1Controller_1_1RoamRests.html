Detailed Description

How many cycles each lone roamer has stood since it last moved, and which one moved last.

The balancer counts a cycle with BeginCycle, then Stand for every roamer on the board, then EndCycle, which forgets the ones it didn't see: a roamer that left the board, or joined a formation, starts its rest over when it's free again.
