Detailed Description

The disco ball's pop (PLAN-DiscoBalloon): a disc grows from where it popped across the whole board, easing out over IDiscoWaveSettings.Seconds.

Each balloon its edge reaches is nudged away from the pop once; a paintable one of a concrete colour bursts in that colour and turns rainbow, and one immune to paint (PaintTargets) takes the drip and keeps its colour. Balloons that move while the disc grows are caught by a last sweep when it ends. What the wave paints is booked to the breaking shot (ShotPaintLedger), so that shot can't climb its streak on it. A run reset, a level change or game over stops the wave where it is. The pop also owes the next clock lines rainbow (RainbowLines). The wave's sound follows its messages: how far through its time it is (DiscoWaveAdvancedMessage), and each balloon it paints (DiscoWaveReachedMessage).
