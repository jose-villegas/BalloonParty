Detailed Description

The ground backdrop shown on ground levels, between the sky scatter and the cloud backdrop: a stationary, overscanned floor quad (BalloonParty/Scenario/Ground) and over it one mesh of grass blades (GrassBladeMesh, BalloonParty/Scenario/GrassBlades) that bend away from anything stamping the shared disturbance field.

Follows IActiveScenario: on ground it shows both, hides the sky they fully cover, and raises the global _GroundCoverage the cloud backdrop reads to fade its bodies (their shadows stay on the grass). The Launch begin-screen always shows sky. The meadow scrolls with ScenarioContentRoot, so on the Ascent the bushes ride in planted on their ground rather than sliding over a still one.
