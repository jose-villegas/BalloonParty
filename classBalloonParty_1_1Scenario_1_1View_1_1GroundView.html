Detailed Description

The ground backdrop shown on ground levels, between the sky scatter and the cloud backdrop: a stationary, overscanned floor quad (BalloonParty/Scenario/Ground) and over it one mesh of grass blades (GrassBladeMesh, BalloonParty/Scenario/GrassBlades) that bend away from anything stamping the shared disturbance field.

Follows IActiveScenario: at rest on ground it shows both, hides the sky they fully cover, and raises the global _GroundCoverage the cloud backdrop reads to fade its bodies (their shadows stay on the grass). A switch that isn't a cut plays the mist reveal (GroundRevealCurve, BalloonParty/Scenario/GroundMist): sky and ground both draw, the ground inside a hole the mist opens or closes, and every GroundCoverageAt reader follows the hole. The Launch begin-screen always shows sky. The meadow scrolls with ScenarioContentRoot, so on the Ascent the bushes ride in planted on their ground rather than sliding over a still one. A reveal also plays the drop between the layers (GroundRevealDepth): it scales the meadow, the cloud backdrop and the content root about the camera, so going down the ground grows into place and going up it falls away. On water levels the same floor quad draws the water surface (BalloonParty/Scenario/Water) instead of the meadow, with no blades: every impact the game reports moves a simulated surface (WaterRippleBuffer), and its waves bend the caustic net under it (WaterCausticBuffer), and the reveal, the depth and the coverage play the same way. Sky always sits between two surfaces in the schedule, so a reveal never swaps one surface for the other; a cut between them just swaps the floor's material.
