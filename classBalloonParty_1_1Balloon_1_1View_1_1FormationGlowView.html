Detailed Description

The blue light ring round unbreakables in formation, on the ground beneath them (FormationGlow shader): rings that join like metaballs as members draw close, lit only as close as they are.

One quad, fitted each frame round the members that glow, so the shader only runs where the light can be; hidden, and costing nothing, while no member glows. The rings also light what stands round them: the quad is only drawn, so each group of joined rings casts one capsule light through the scene-light field (FormationGlowBeams).
