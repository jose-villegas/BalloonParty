Detailed Description

The Sharpshooter card's roll, once per ball the thrower loads.

A hit arms the ball through the Snipe's own path (ISnipeArmer), so it is a Snipe lance in every way, pencil line included. The card's chance is low; pity (IAssistSettings.SnipePity) raises it for each ball loaded without a Snipe while the player is in trouble (IPlayerNeed), armour has stood on the board too long (IArmourNeed) or a bomb has sat under a formation's cover (ICoveredBombNeed), whichever is the most. After a hit, no load rolls for IUpgradeSettings.SnipeCooldownSeconds, so rapid fire can't chain lances. A purple level raises the card's chance (IUpgradeSettings.PurpleLevelSnipeChanceScale). A guaranteed load (ISnipeGuarantee, the first golden balloon's lesson) arms with no roll and no cooldown.
