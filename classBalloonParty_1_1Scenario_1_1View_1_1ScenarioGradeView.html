Detailed Description

Grades the whole frame per scenario (IScenarioGradeSettings), so the sky, the meadow and the water read in the same light.

One global Volume holds a runtime profile whose values blend from the sky's grade to the surface's by how much of the screen the surface covers (BackdropMix), so the grade follows the mist reveal frame by frame. The grade needs the main camera's post-processing, which this turns on while the grade is enabled and gives back otherwise. The HUD canvases render through that camera, so they take the grade too.
