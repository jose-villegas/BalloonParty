Detailed Description

Grows back the caps a lance cut (Meadow Mushrooms), in time with the board: a cut cap pops back on one of the board's beats (IBoardBeat), the one that brings its count to IMushroomSettings.RegrowBeats, and is announced (MushroomRegrownMessage).

Which beat is the first. The beat is read once a frame, in Tick. A cap is first seen by the tick that follows its cut, and its count starts with the first beat seen to fall after that tick: a beat that fell in the very frame the cap was cut in, whichever of the two came first, is not the cap's. That is the one rule for every cap, the first cut on a whole board and one cut while others wait alike. So a cap cut a frame before a beat has that beat as its first, and one cut in a beat's own frame waits a whole beat for its first: with five beats of 1.7 seconds a cap is gone for between 6.8 and 8.5 seconds, and always comes back on the beat. A pause holds the beat and so holds the caps.

Not under a ball. A cap whose beats are up does not grow back while a ball in flight has its centre inside the cap's circle: a ball cannot be put inside a bumper. It is asked again on the next beat, and the one after, until its place is clear.

It keeps the beat it last saw and how many cut caps it had seen by then, and nothing else: which caps are cut, and how many beats each has left, is the field's (IMushroomRegrowth, which lists them in the order they were cut, so the caps cut since the last tick are the last ones). While no cap is cut a tick is one comparison and the beat is not read. A level's end and a reset take the cut caps off the board with the rest of it, and the field forgets them: nothing is regrown and nothing is announced, since the level that follows places whole caps.
