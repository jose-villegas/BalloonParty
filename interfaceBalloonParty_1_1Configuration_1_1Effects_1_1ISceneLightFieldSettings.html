◆ DirectionResponse

float BalloonParty.Configuration.Effects.ISceneLightFieldSettings.DirectionResponse
get

How strongly a light's local magnitude bends the field direction toward it: weight = saturate(localR * DirectionResponse).

Higher = the direction snaps to a local light at a lower brightness. (Falloff shape is now per-BalloonParty.Shared.SceneLight light, not global.)

Implemented in BalloonParty.Configuration.Effects.SceneLightFieldSettings.

◆ FieldFrameInterval

The field's re-render cadence, authored as "every N frames at 60 fps" and reinterpreted in seconds (see BalloonParty.Shared.SceneLight.SceneLightFieldService) so cost doesn't scale with display refresh — a 120 Hz panel would otherwise double it.
