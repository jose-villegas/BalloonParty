Detailed Description

Orders simulated shots the way a player who knows the rewards would.

A golden balloon's free card outweighs any score, and a heart balloon outweighs score while its heart counts: up to the hearts missing, or any number on a purple level, where each is an extra one. A disco ball's rainbow board comes next: it pays the shots after this one, so a heart that counts now goes first. Score decides the rest. No reward balloon scores, so ranking on score alone never aims at one.
