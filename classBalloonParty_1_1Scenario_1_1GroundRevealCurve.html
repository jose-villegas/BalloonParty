Detailed Description

The timing of the mist reveal, as one curve over how open the ground is.

Sky to ground plays it forward: the mist rolls in over the sky, then a hole opens from the middle and grows until the meadow fills the screen. Ground to sky plays the same curve backwards: the meadow closes into the mist, which then clears to the sky.

The layers' scales over the whole reveal, eased so the travel starts and lands gently.

The sky is always zoom times the ground, and whichever layer the reveal ends on reaches 1, so the scales are continuous at both ends and the level that stays on screen never jumps.

Parameters
opening0 = sky with no mist, 1 = all ground (see Opening).
zoomHow many times larger the sky looks than the ground; 1 or less = no depth.
