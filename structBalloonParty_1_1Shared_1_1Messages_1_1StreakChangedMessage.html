Detailed Description

Published whenever a colour pop-streak changes — grew, switched colour, broke, or reset.

A colour switch changes two colours at once (old drops to 0, new starts at 1), so consumers should re-query IColorStreak.GetStreak for their own colour rather than trust the payload.

The ball whose streak changed, or null for the shared streak (pops no ball caused) and for the reset of every streak at level-up.

With several balls in flight one ball's reset is not the streak breaking, so a consumer that tracks one ball checks this first.
