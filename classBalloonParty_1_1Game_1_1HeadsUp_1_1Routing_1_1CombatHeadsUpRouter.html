Detailed Description

The shot's heads-ups: a streak growing (x3! at the pop, rewriting itself per ball and colour), a speed tap off a wall, a pierce arming and what its discharge shattered, and the rainbow picked up.

Which numbers are worth writing is each style's own range in IHeadsUpSettings.
