Detailed Description

A rounded UI shape's specular, lit like the balloons: a soft stroke that runs along the inside of the shape's rim, centred where the rim faces the time-of-day light (gliding along the edges as the light sweeps, as on a curved skin), following the corner's curve onto the straight edges and tapering away at both ends.

Its colour leans toward the light's. Stretch it over the shape it lights. The mesh is rebuilt only when the light or the size changes.
